  raw fresh  1 - cos(r_c, r_{c+-k}) with each checkpoint's OWN fresh FM.
             Floor = the instrument's own disagreement at a fixed checkpoint.
  excess     the same, minus the per-position same-checkpoint floor.
  fixed      1 - cos(r^(c)_c, r^(c)_{c+-k}); one FM on both sides, floor = 0 exactly.
  All three are quantile-binned WITHIN (level, checkpoint), so the bins carry no
  level or checkpoint information and the three are directly comparable.
