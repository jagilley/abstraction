3. THE HEADLINE: advantage per target per direction
   All columns are held-out SEQUENCES at the HELD-OUT CHECKPOINT BLOCK (the strict
   evaluation) unless marked (seq), which is held-out sequences at the training
   checkpoints. Two advantage statistics are given, because the dispositional
   setting admits a third-party observer paper 2's occurrent setting did not:
   O_hist sees M's own output summary at checkpoints c, c-1, ..., c-4.
   O_act (handed M's early activations) stays a ceiling and is never an advantage
   comparator.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ADVio  = self - best single-snapshot O_io  (paper 2's statistic)
   ADVpub = self - best THIRD-PARTY observer, i.e. max(O_io, O_hist). O_hist reads
            only M's outputs, just over several snapshots, so for a dispositional
            target it is a legitimate comparator; for paper 2's occurrent target it
            would not have been. This is the conservative statistic here.
   fracpub= (best third party - chance) / (self - chance): the fraction of the
            self's above-chance score reachable from outside. 1.0 = fully public.
            component_control's `frac`, with the self as ceiling (the self is far
            from saturation on every dispositional target, so raw advantage alone
            confounds privacy with how well the self can report the target at all).
   Columns are held-out sequences @ HELD-OUT CHECKPOINTS unless marked (seq).
