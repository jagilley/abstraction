========================================================================================================
DISPOSITIONAL SELF-KNOWLEDGE -- stratified re-analysis  [tag=main]
========================================================================================================
Does the pooled dispositional result hide structure, as the grammar sibling's
hierarchy-level cut does? Language has no level labels, so the same cut is run with
proxies for computational depth: three public, one private and for comparison only.

All numbers are the STRICT evaluation set: held-out sequences at the held-out
checkpoint block, scored WITHIN stratum.
  ADVio  = self - best O_io, the max over the three observer capacities evaluated
           IN THAT STRATUM (which lets the observer pick its best capacity per
           stratum while the self cannot -- generous to the third party).
  ADVpub = self - max(O_io, O_hist): the conservative statistic for a dispositional
           target, since O_hist reads only M's outputs, just over several snapshots.
  O_act  = handed M's early activations. A ceiling, never an advantage comparator.
  chance = the WITHIN-STRATUM majority-class share. No pooled head or observer may
           condition on the stratum label, so it is not a baseline any of them could
           have used -- but a large advantage in a stratum where nobody reaches
           chance is a different object from one where they all do. Read it.
Strata with fewer than 200 evaluation positions are dropped.
