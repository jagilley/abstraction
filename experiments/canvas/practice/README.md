## Scope

The practice arc's third substrate: image *styles* on the aligned tile grammar, inpainting under a
forward-pass budget as the piece, the adjacency-support test as the oracle-free grade of record and
typicality as the logged taste gauge — everything [`plant/`](../plant/README.md) built and
described. The port plan is the arc's own sequence: **(1) `ratchet`** earn a level-indexed
vocabulary; **(2) `native`** consolidate it into the plant, including the port canvas makes
natural — minting a tile token into the plant's own input stream — beside RHM's routing head;
**(3) `spiral`** re-earn the next level over the minted plant.

## Children

### [`ratchet/`](ratchet/README.md) — earning a tile vocabulary on canvas (2026-08-26)

**Goal**: fork the RHM ratchet onto aligned tiles — T[2] ≈ tiles, T[3] ≈ 2×2-tile motifs, an
inpainting depth ladder, G = 8 forward passes per solve, support as value target, mining gate,
audition and meter — and grade earned vocabularies against `never_base` and a `given` table read
off genuine exemplars through the withheld tile ids.

**Finding**: **earned ≈ given, and the descent is purely vocabulary-carried.** At eras 2–3 the
sensibly-timed practice arms cluster with `given` (earned-vs-given 0.85–1.14; `practice_prov`'s
203-entry strict subset of `given`'s 364 ties it — the third sighting of "the used part of the
list is the list"), `practice_early` is foreclosed at ~0.45, and `never_base` — search plus a free
truth grader, with no value head anywhere and an inert plant — is 0.2–0.35 worse. Oracle validity
orders the arms exactly as support does. The level-3 vocabulary nests and forecloses correctly
but buys almost nothing yet (T[3] at support is 538 of 10 752; coverage is the next level's
binding term), and the taste gauge *rises* .86 → .98 across eras while validity falls to ~.2 —
the two-organ dissociation, in-loop. Ranks are the claim; operating point dominated
(width 1 beats 2 at G = 8; ranks survive it).
