# HISTORY — experiments/mjc

## Why this substrate exists

The program needed a rung between RHM (discrete, known latents, cheap) and language (continuous, no ground truth) — something with a *real* command channel and genuine contact dynamics, but still knob-controllable rather than an RL benchmark to chase SOTA on. [`ideas/physical_control_substrate.md`](../../ideas/physical_control_substrate.md) pitched MuJoCo for exactly this, laid out five planned "cuts," and made one discipline non-negotiable from the start: every experiment is a knob sweep with a vanilla baseline, never a leaderboard number ([`README.md`](README.md) §"Discipline"). That fork held for the entire arc — nothing below is PPO-maxxed.

A second founding document, [`ideas/two_timescale_value_loop.md`](../../ideas/two_timescale_value_loop.md), supplied the hypothesis that would occupy most of mjc's life: a2a had built forward models but every value signal tried on them "lifts performance once and plateaus"; the missing piece was a *slow, non-stationary* outer loop shaping what the FM learns, distinct from any scalar injected into a residual stream. Almost everything from `value_shaping/` onward is a test of some piece of this interface.

## Foundations: three cuts that earned the substrate's keep (Jul 2026)

[`contact_residual/`](contact_residual/README.md) (Cut #1) and [`arity_torque/`](arity_torque/README.md) (Cut #2) reproduced RHM-flavored claims — residual structure at regime transitions, arity beating resolution — on real contact physics, establishing that the toy-domain intuitions survive the jump. [`dynamics_shift/`](dynamics_shift/README.md) (Cut #3) was the substrate's real payoff: intervening on the *transition operator* while holding the task fixed, something RHM couldn't offer. Model-based re-adaptation from ~50 reward-free transitions vs. a flat model-free baseline needing ~240× more reward-labeled data became the base every later node builds on, and its `replan_every` ablation — the benefit turns over at the FM's composition horizon — quietly seeded the entire ballistic-vs-reactive distinction that would surface again five weeks later.

*Small correction along the way*: `dimensionality_expansion.md`'s "historical negatives" section later reclassified Cut #1's early rank-based negative as an instrument failure, not a hypothesis failure — the first sign that this program's own measurement instruments would need as much scrutiny as its findings.

## The value↔FM interface arc, and its first real wall (Jul 18–21)

[`value_shaping/`](value_shaping/README.md) built the stationary control — value re-allocates a capacity-limited FM away from a reducible-but-irrelevant force field — before touching anything non-stationary. Then [`directed_readapt/`](directed_readapt/README.md) tried the obvious next move, a disagreement-driven exploration policy for reward-free re-adaptation, and hit two informative negatives: a global shift left no scarcity to exploit, and a *localized* shift exposed that disagreement is blind to confident-wrongness — every ensemble member warm-starts from the same pre-shift prior, so they agree in the very region they're wrong about. This is the arc's first pivot: away from verifying mechanistic atoms one at a time, toward phenomenon-first experiments that let a result redefine the next question.

[`meta_adapt/`](meta_adapt/README.md) (Cut #4 → #4e) is that phenomenon-first run, five sub-cuts deep: a 1-D damping floor collapses to nothing while an actuator-rotation conflict opens a meta-learning gap monotonically with rotation angle; a context latent decodes the task parameter yet system-ID turns out orthogonal to adaptation benefit; value-directed identification is a robust scarcity-gated negative; value-shaping and meta-conditioning turn out to be *separable* capacity levers that only interact under competition; and closing the loop with a reward-driven outer objective both prefers the informative signal and rediscovers value's support on its own (with a wireheading gotcha en route). [`curiosity_control/`](curiosity_control/README.md) then supplied the afferent half — an intrinsic surprise drive that tracks a *moving* frontier — and produced the arc's second pivot: on low-dimensional control, ensemble disagreement chases aleatoric noise (a noisy-TV pathology), the exact *inversion* of the a2a active-vision result where disagreement rejected noise. The fix wasn't a smarter filter; it was grounding the drive in task-relevance as a second additive signal.

## Building the online loop, and learning to read an obstruction as a finding (Jul 21–23)

[`online_value_loop/`](online_value_loop/README.md) tried to close both taps — afferent and efferent — into one fully-online loop and got obstructed on both: CEM-MPC control turned out robust to the exploration drive's tracking gradient, and the value-shaping benefit was a slow, commit-dependent transient invisible to any short online probe. Rather than a failed experiment, this was read as a mechanistic result: value genuinely lives on the slow timescale, so the two-timescale split the idea doc proposed is *forced*, not a design choice — and its payoff is adaptation *speed*, never converged competence. [`drift_value_loop/`](drift_value_loop/README.md) pushed this three cuts under perpetual drift and located the real bottleneck: it wasn't the value structure, it was the *grader*. Regrading the meta-loop by value-relevant FM prediction error (instead of control) produced a clean interior optimum where control itself was flat — the arc's central methodological lesson, that a blind grader can make a real signal look inert.

That lesson immediately caught a self-inflicted error. An audit of [`ballistic/directed/`](ballistic/directed/README.md)'s directed-collection loop found its published null had been read off exactly the blind grader the team had just learned to distrust, on a metric 62% contaminated by post-recovery plateau. The audit's own conclusion generalized further: every FM in the node had been trained on teleported, omnisciently-covering transitions, and the loop under audit had spent a 22× subsidy of free measurement to allocate a nominal budget of 100. That finding became the standing memo [`on_policy/COLLECTION_REALISM.md`](on_policy/COLLECTION_REALISM.md), which retiered every prior claim in the node (dissociations safe; absolute sample counts are upper bounds; anything about *where* to collect experience was previously unaskable) and made on-policy collection the new convention for future cuts ([`README.md`](README.md) collection-convention note, 2026-07-23).

## Ballistic control, the arm, and a retired founding claim (Jul 22–27)

[`ballistic/`](ballistic/README.md) resolved a standing puzzle from the online loop — why the FM→behavior bridge kept failing to transmit — as a fact about *control mode*, not value: a feedforward, committed controller makes the FM ~3× more behaviorally load-bearing than a replanning one, because replanning is a blind grader that re-grounds past a stale model. [`arm_substrate/`](arm_substrate/README.md) then built a second task family expressly to kill the "single-family, hand-manufactured perturbation" caveat that had shadowed every result to that point — and it worked, reproducing the ballistic aftereffect with a mirror-signed readout the pusher geometry structurally could not produce, while its own memo was careful to note that physics realism and *experience* realism (still teleport, at that point) are independent axes, one fixed doesn't fix the other.

[`expansion/`](expansion/README.md) (Cut #5) then closed out the founding idea doc's single largest untested claim, and retired part of it: [`ideas/physical_control_substrate.md`](../../ideas/physical_control_substrate.md) had pitched perpetual drift as a "novelty generator" that should force the forward model's representational frontier open. Measured against a calibrated unit of genuine expansion (+0.72 ± 0.42 from an actual added degree of freedom), drift produced ±0.08 — roughly 10× too small to count, confirmed by a 2×2 that separated *how many* parameters drift from *how hard* the operator is. The finding folded cleanly into [`beliefs/dimensionality_expansion.md`](../../beliefs/dimensionality_expansion.md) §Scope, added days earlier from other evidence: expansion needs hierarchical structure to expand *into*, and a fixed-DOF motor plant simply has none. Drift survives in the program for realism (it is still the mechanism behind every non-stationary result above); what's retired is only its claimed special role in growing representations.

## The performance-error bridge, tested three ways in one day (Aug 11)

[`ideas/performance_error_is_the_bridge.md`](../../ideas/performance_error_is_the_bridge.md) proposed a cerebellar-flavored signal, δ = (b−e)·σ(g/θ), gating plasticity as a multiplicative *gain* rather than an additive reward term. Three same-day probes stress-tested different pieces of it. [`agency_gate/`](agency_gate/README.md) showed the discrimination the theory needs lives in the *gate*, not the sensory stream — on identical sequences, δ fires when self-produced and is silent under passive playback, but the ungated raw signal fires in both cases — and caught that the doc's literal formula leaks at zero (σ(0)=0.5, needing recentering). [`plasticity_gain/`](plasticity_gain/README.md) found that on raw control performance δ barely beats a naive raw-error gain; its actual content is in *allocation and retention* (raw error spends ~19% of its budget on unimprovable noise forever), and only holds if the benchmark is context-conditional rather than a global scalar. [`curiosity_control/benchmark_vs_cost/`](curiosity_control/benchmark_vs_cost/README.md), a pure re-analysis of already-collected data, killed a standing cost term and reframed δ itself as a gain/allocation signal rather than a value estimate.

[`two_clocks/`](two_clocks/README.md) and [`bridge_assembly/`](bridge_assembly/README.md) then closed the arc's two remaining bets. Performance error and reward turned out to be genuinely separate channels — sharing one eligibility window was *destructive interference*, not a cheap compromise, worse than either channel alone. And when the corrected δ was finally run end-to-end, it produced the arc's cleanest inversion: the team's working law had been "ballistic control transmits FM quality, reactive control is a blind grader" (established back in `ballistic/`); here, δ's hygiene advantage reached behavior through the *reactive* channel instead. The resolution wasn't that the earlier law was wrong, but that it was underspecified — which controller transmits a forward-model difference depends on whether that difference is spatially localized (ballistic wins) or diffuse (reactive wins). A blind-vs-transmitting controller turned out to be a matching condition, not a fixed property of the controller.

## Where the priors sit now

- **The substrate's core discipline held for six weeks straight**: no experiment here has ever optimized a benchmark number; every result is a sweep-relative dissociation, and that discipline is what let two published nulls (`directed_readapt`, the audited `ballistic/directed` S2) get *used* rather than discarded.
- **The single biggest standing correction to the program's own practice** is [`on_policy/COLLECTION_REALISM.md`](on_policy/COLLECTION_REALISM.md): most of this node's history was quietly run on a teleporting oracle, and the fix (a `collection_mode` flag, backward-compatible by construction) is now the default for new work rather than a retrofit.
- **One founding hypothesis was cleanly retired** (drift as a novelty/expansion generator, [`expansion/README.md`](expansion/README.md)), and the belief tree absorbed the scope correction rather than treating it as a program failure — consistent with this repo's stated preference for updating the belief, not the intuition that motivated the experiment.
- **The live edge of the program** is re-running the retracted directed-collection question on the local-drift, on-policy substrate ([`README.md`](README.md) §Next steps, item 0) — the first place the "where should I practice?" question is askable without the teleportation subsidy that made it unanswerable everywhere else.
