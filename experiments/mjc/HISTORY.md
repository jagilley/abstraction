# History of `experiments/mjc/`

*A big-picture pass over the MuJoCo controllable-dynamics substrate — one rung of realism above RHM, below language. Written from the accumulated READMEs, not the raw commit log (the git history here is coarse: most of this tree entered in one large import, so the archaeology lives in the docs, not the diffs). See [`README.md`](README.md) for the current state and [`FILES.md`](FILES.md) for the full file index.*

## I. The substrate proves itself, then starts generating its own questions

The program opened as a port: re-run the a2a/RHM residual and arity stories on real contact physics and see if they survive. [`contact_residual/`](contact_residual/README.md) (Cut #1) and [`arity_torque/`](arity_torque/README.md) (Cut #2) are complementary by design — #1 owns the contact regime, #2 evaluates on free flight only, so neither result borrows the other's territory. Both land by refusing their own easy version: #1's residual concentration story moves from "contact" to "contact *onset*" (an event detector, not a state detector) after its rank story flatly contradicts the tempting read; #2's arity win only counts once i.i.d. commands kill the RHM-style confound where the command is recoverable from the state.

[`dynamics_shift/`](dynamics_shift/README.md) (Cut #3) is the hinge, and its most important result is a near-null it didn't discard. Per-step replanning nearly erased the forward model's relevance — feedback substitutes for a bad model when you re-ground every step. Rather than tune around this, Cut #3 turned it into the `replan_every` axis, and from here the program's question permanently shifts from *"is the FM good"* to *"under what control mode is the FM load-bearing."* The ceiling it finds (composition horizon ~6–8 steps) reproduces a2a's independently-derived number on a different substrate, and the biological reading (`replan_every` ≈ how ballistic a movement is) is named here as a reading — later, `ballistic/` promotes it to a causal knob.

## II. The value↔FM interface: a question that keeps refusing to stay answered

[`value_shaping/`](value_shaping/README.md) and [`directed_readapt/`](directed_readapt/README.md) attack the two halves of "does value shape the model" — the learning layer and the collection layer — and both come back the same shape: real mechanism, benefit gated on a scarcity condition the substrate doesn't supply for free. value_shaping's capacity "teeth" appear at 21K samples and vanish at 70K. directed_readapt's global-shift arm nulls because nothing is scarce, and its localized-patch arm manufactures scarcity only to find disagreement-directed collection **anti-correlated** with it — ensemble members agree confidently and wrongly in unvisited regions, so disagreement flags *missing* data, not *stale* data. Two independently-designed atoms failing in the same shape (2026-07-18) triggers an explicit pivot: stop trying to verify mechanisms in isolation, build the phenomenon first, recover the mechanism from what works.

[`meta_adapt/`](meta_adapt/README.md) (#4→#4e) is that phenomenon, run to exhaustion. Its own opening move is a floor collapse — meta and multitask are *genuinely* identical (not both pinned at a ceiling) until task **conflict** is dialed in, at which point the gap opens monotonically with rotation angle. #4b delivers the dissociation the rest of the arc keeps re-finding in new clothes: a context latent can decode the task perfectly (system-ID R²≈1) while adaptation-benefit stays at zero — **system-ID ⊥ adaptation-benefit**. #4c reproduces it on the collection side (info-MPC verifiably navigates to the informative patch, doesn't beat a max-‖u‖ heuristic). #4d and #4e close the efferent side: value-shaping is real, control-neutral without capacity competition, control-superior under it, and reward genuinely *causes* the re-allocation — with a live wireheading gotcha (an unnormalized weight budget games loss-scale instead of allocation).

[`curiosity_control/`](curiosity_control/README.md) does the same on the afferent tap and finds a substrate inversion: the reducibility filter that worked for active vision *fails* here, because low-dimensional bootstrapped noise makes ensemble members disagree instead of converge — a noisy-TV pathology this program had to discover fresh. The fix isn't a smarter filter but **grounding**: explore and exploit as two additive drives with an interior optimum, wired as one system for the first time. [`curiosity_control/benchmark_vs_cost/`](curiosity_control/benchmark_vs_cost/README.md), a same-day re-analysis, later closes off a tempting extra term: no standing cost is supported, and the driving `b−e` signal turns out to be a *gain*, not an allocation score.

[`online_value_loop/`](online_value_loop/README.md) tries to let the system tune its own explore/exploit balance and both taps fail, for different reasons — control turns out to be *robust* to the afferent drive's tracking gap (no sensitive window exists), and the efferent benefit is a slow, commit-dependent pre-convergence transient invisible to any probe short of a full retrain. The node's real move is refusing to read this as failure: it re-derives the idea doc's asserted two-timescale split as *forced*, not assumed, and reframes value's payoff as **adaptation speed, not converged competence** — which dissolves the washout finding, since perpetual drift never lets you converge.

[`drift_value_loop/`](drift_value_loop/README.md) runs the successor experiment and reassigns the credit again: compounding across a drift sequence is real, but it comes from the **memory architecture**, not the value-carving — both a factored and a veridical context-latent model compound, and pooled memory is actively harmful under conflict. The diagnosis this yields — **control is a near-blind grader** — retroactively explains the whole arc's difficulty, and swapping the outer-loop teacher to value-relevant FM prediction error (the literal cerebellum→VTA signal) produces the arc's cleanest single result: a clean interior optimum where control gave dead flat. Even this doesn't close cleanly: [`drift_value_loop/teacher_snr/`](drift_value_loop/teacher_snr/README.md), meant as a retrofit, instead diagnoses that the teacher's *form* was right but its *search procedure* remains the bottleneck (measured SNR 0.80, perturbation explaining 0% of advantage variance).

## III. Realism, redefined three times

The program's working definition of "realism" was rewritten three times, each time by a measurement rather than a taste argument. [`ballistic/`](ballistic/README.md) supplies the first: the FM→behavior obstruction from part II is a fact about the **control mode**, not the value — a committed, feedforward controller makes the FM ~3× more behaviorally load-bearing than a reactive one, because reactive replanning "re-grounds past a stale model" and is nearly blind to its quality.

The sharpest moment in the whole tree sits inside [`ballistic/directed/`](ballistic/directed/README.md): S1 cleanly recovers the oracle collection allocation, but S2 puts it in a loop and reports a null. An audit (not a re-run) finds the null was unmeasurable in principle — graded by the same control metric this tree had already shown was blind, dominated by post-recovery score, flipped by a single seed, and run under a **22× measurement subsidy** where "looking" (probing, monitoring) was free. The result is downgraded from *null* to *untested*, and the retraction is converted directly into a structural fix rather than a discipline to remember: [`on_policy/`](on_policy/README.md)'s `Body` charges every simulation step and **does not expose `set_state`**, so the subsidy becomes unbuyable. `on_policy/` then repays the debt with unusual honesty — E0 finds its own founding claim (embodiment matters) was mostly a mistuned teleport sampling knob; E1 refutes the memo's own live hypothesis about on-policy data; only E2's *local* drift makes embodiment genuinely load-bearing (2.5× more transitions, via bootstrap data-quality, not the guessed coverage mechanism). [`on_policy/directed_on_policy/`](on_policy/directed_on_policy/README.md) (E3) finally answers S2's original question, positively, once looking is metered honestly (1.84×, not 22×).

[`arm_substrate/`](arm_substrate/README.md) supplies the third redefinition — a second plant, built to retire ballistic's single-family caveat and replace *manufactured* capacity competition with physics that binds intrinsically (only at n≥5 links, separating "nonlinear" from "capacity-hungry" as distinct axes). Its most useful trait is a refusal: it explicitly declines to let "we moved to the arm" answer the acquisition question that `on_policy/` owns, keeping the three realism axes (control mode, acquisition, plant) legible as independent variables instead of one vague "more realistic" knob.

## IV. Cut #5: the flagship question, closed clean

[`expansion/`](expansion/README.md) runs the substrate memo's own billed centerpiece — unrun for months — and retires it. Built as calibrate → measure → calibrate (rank-shaped instruments had failed three times before in this repo), it finds drift moves the FM's representational frontier by **±0.08 directions** against a **+0.72 ± 0.42** calibration from genuine DOF accretion — an order of magnitude below a known-real effect, on the same plant, same instrument, same probe. The retraction is precise, not sweeping: only the claim that *drift* has special standing for representational growth is retired; drift remains essential to the program for realism (it's what the entire `drift_value_loop/`/`on_policy/` line runs on). Read against [`beliefs/dimensionality_expansion.md`](../../beliefs/dimensionality_expansion.md) §Scope, the null is confirmation, not damage — a fixed-DOF motor plant lacks the hierarchical structure expansion needs, so a flat frontier here is expected health. The belief's actual core claim (does *novelty* — not drift — grow representable directions) has never been measured anywhere and is relocated to a domain that has hierarchy: [`rhm/residual_decomposition/`](../rhm/residual_decomposition/README.md).

## V. The performance-error bridge: a signal whose job keeps narrowing

A same-day triangulation — [`agency_gate/`](agency_gate/README.md), [`plasticity_gain/`](plasticity_gain/README.md), [`curiosity_control/benchmark_vs_cost/`](curiosity_control/benchmark_vs_cost/README.md) — tests [`ideas/performance_error_is_the_bridge.md`](../../ideas/performance_error_is_the_bridge.md)'s δ signal (`(b−e)·σ(g/θ)`) three ways, after `teacher_snr/` had already re-scoped it from a meta-search reward to a per-trial gain. The gate is load-bearing (δ fires ~369× more under self-produced distortion than passive playback — agency, not raw error magnitude, drives it, and the doc's own ungated formula leaks). The gain consumption works (δ-gated plasticity beats fixed-lr on ballistic re-adaptation, 3/3 seeds) but its edge over even raw-error gating is specifically about *allocation and retention*, not control score. A standing cost term is unsupported, and — decisively — δ turns out to be a gain/value signal, not an allocation score at all (it's actively repulsive exactly where allocation needs to go).

[`two_clocks/`](two_clocks/README.md) then makes the arc's strongest positive claim: a fast per-transition precision signal and a slow trial-level value signal carry genuinely non-redundant content, each legible only through its own delay-matched eligibility window — and *sharing a window is destructive interference*, not a compromise. [`bridge_assembly/`](bridge_assembly/README.md) runs the fully corrected δ live and finds a humbling result: ungated uniform plasticity still wins the tested regime outright; δ's benefit is bounded to long, hard-recovery transients. Its most transferable finding reframes the recurring "control is a blind grader" frustration as a **matching condition**: which controller transmits an FM difference depends on where the difference's mass lives — localized staleness wants a ballistic line-integral, diffuse churn wants a reactive surface-average. Across this whole sub-arc, δ's candidate job keeps shrinking: not a meta-search reward, not an allocation score, regime-dependent as a gain — which sets up the reading in [`practice/`](practice/README.md) that δ's real, surviving role may be **detection**: a certificate that a segment is ready to commit, not a thing that modulates learning throughout.

## VI. `practice/etude/`: a mostly separate thread that lands on the same idea

[`practice/etude/`](practice/etude/README.md) tests whether motor compilation is distillation (averaging accumulated practice into a policy) and finds instead that **compilation is selection, plus verbatim commitment** — the best individual trace beats the mean of valid traces by 2×, and behavior-cloned regression compounds the loss further. Its corrections are load-bearing on their own terms (ranking candidates by their own realized error suffers a winner's-curse; auditioning and consumption states must match, or a 13.7× seam-state offset fully explains a spurious "optimism gap"). With those fixed, sequential assembly with seam-matched selection beats never-compiling on accuracy *and* time, 3/3 seeds, and committed units never degrade post-commit. This thread runs on its own metrics and doesn't share infrastructure with the value↔FM or bridge arcs, but it has one real joint: a compiled unit *is* a committed feedforward motor program, so its win is the constructive counterpart to `ballistic/`'s claim that commitment is what makes an FM matter — and its no-per-sample-δ-gain design is the direct predecessor of §V's "δ as detector, not modulator" reading.

## Where this leaves the program

The recurring shape across every thread above is the same: an apparent negative is almost always a **grader problem**, not a phenomenon problem — control is a blind grader of FM quality; disagreement can't see confident-wrongness; teleportation samples the wrong region; free probing hides a 22× subsidy — and each time, the response was to build a corrected instrument rather than abandon the question. The program has also traded, twice, a big a-priori-appealing thesis for a narrower, measured one: "drift is a novelty generator" (retired, cleanly, by [`expansion/`](expansion/README.md)) and "value structure explains the FM→behavior obstruction" (narrowed to a control-mode-and-teacher explanation by the `ballistic/`→`drift_value_loop/` chain). The live thread, per [`README.md`](README.md)'s Next steps, is re-attempting `ballistic/directed/`'s retracted S2 question on the local-drift on-policy substrate — the first setup where "where should I practise?" is a genuinely askable, metered question.
