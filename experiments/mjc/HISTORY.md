# History of experiments/mjc

*A big-picture reading of the mjc line (2026-08-11 → 08-21), tracing how the questions and priors moved, not a log of every run. For blow-by-blow detail, follow the linked READMEs — this file is the connective tissue between them.*

## Origins: a third rung, and a discipline against RL

[`ideas/physical_control_substrate.md`](../../ideas/physical_control_substrate.md) proposed MuJoCo not as a robotics benchmark but as a third controllable-dynamics DGP, one rung of realism above RHM/MNIST-reaching — physics parameters (friction, mass, damping, gear, drift) as knobs, exactly as RHM treats `(v,s,L,m)`. The explicit anti-goal, stated once and held for ten days: no PPO-maxxing, no external benchmarking — every experiment is a knob sweep with a vanilla baseline, reporting a slope or dissociation, never a leaderboard number ([`README.md`](README.md)). Five cuts were pre-registered. Three landed close to plan; two did not, and the two departures set the direction for everything after.

## Phase 1 — three foundational cuts, and one that becomes the substrate

Cut #1 ([`contact_residual/README.md`](contact_residual/README.md)) confirmed the headline (residual concentrates 8.1× at contact) but an onset-aligned event-triggered average sharpened the *reading*: the residual is an event/regime-transition detector, not a state detector — the first instance of a pattern that recurs throughout this history, where a clean positive gets its interpretation corrected without being overturned. Cut #2 ([`arity_torque/README.md`](arity_torque/README.md)) converged cleanly on the planned result: under i.i.d. commands, arity beats resolution outright (a 232-parameter arity-2 model beats a 70k-parameter arity-1 model).

Cut #3 ([`dynamics_shift/README.md`](dynamics_shift/README.md)) diverged hardest, and became the node everything else builds on. The planned flagship (a static sim2sim degradation sweep) was abandoned mid-flight for a sharper claim: a dynamics shift corrupts only the world-model factor, so a model-based agent re-adapts from ~50 reward-free transitions while a protocol-matched model-free agent needs ~240× more reward-*labeled* data — but only under open-loop commitment. Per-step replanning is a near-null, because feedback substitutes for the model. **This one ablation — that re-groundability erases the advantage of having a good forward model — becomes the arc's recurring antagonist**, reappearing as the reason the middle arc stalls and the reason the ballistic arc exists at all.

## Phase 2 — the value↔FM interface: confirmed, but only on a boundary condition

[`ideas/two_timescale_value_loop.md`](../../ideas/two_timescale_value_loop.md) predicted a slow reward loop steering a capacity-limited forward model via two afferent taps (explore/exploit) and one efferent gain (value-shaping). [`value_shaping/README.md`](value_shaping/README.md) got the efferent half working, but only after three failed substrate designs taught a load-bearing lesson: a distractor must be *reducible yet capacity-hungry*, not merely present, for value-shaping to have anything to bite on. [`directed_readapt/README.md`](directed_readapt/README.md) then produced the phase's pivot: an ensemble-disagreement drive **under-visits** a confidently-wrong region, because agreement on a stale prior looks like certainty — disagreement flags uncertainty, not wrongness. Two negatives in a row pushed the arc to go phenomenon-first rather than architecture-first.

[`meta_adapt/README.md`](meta_adapt/README.md) (#4→#4e) is the resulting six-part arc in one node: the meta gap is an order parameter in task conflict (collapses at the RHM-anchor floor, opens monotonically with actuator-rotation Φ); system-ID and adaptation-benefit are dissociated; value-directed identification is a **robust scarcity-gated negative** (matches a trivial max-‖u‖ heuristic — value-of-information was over-engineering here); value-shaping and meta-conditioning are two separable capacity levers, control-superior only under genuine capacity competition; and a reward-only outer loop rediscovers value's support on its own. [`curiosity_control/README.md`](curiosity_control/README.md) wired the two afferent taps into one system (grounding cures a noisy-TV pathology that inverts a result from the sibling a2a active-vision arc), but control payoff stayed modest. [`online_value_loop/README.md`](online_value_loop/README.md) then failed to self-tune either tap online — and converted that failure into the phase's thesis: **value is a slow, committed, offline-estimable quantity; its benefit is adaptation speed, not converged competence.**

[`drift_value_loop/README.md`](drift_value_loop/README.md) resolved the diagnosis one level deeper: compounding under perpetual drift is real, but it comes from the *memory layer* (a task-conditioned context latent), not from value-carving, which stayed capacity-gated and control-robust. Grading the meta-loop by value-relevant FM prediction error — the cerebellum→VTA signal made literal — gives the explore/exploit balance a clean optimum that control-grading could not find. *The missing piece was the teacher, not the value structure.* [`drift_value_loop/teacher_snr/README.md`](drift_value_loop/teacher_snr/README.md) then re-opened even that: Cut #3's self-tuning was optimistic (SNR 0.80 on a single scalar REINFORCE estimate) — the obstruction is the *estimator*, a methodological rather than conceptual gap.

## Phase 3 — ballistic control: blaming the controller, not the value

With replanning established as a blind grader, [`ballistic/README.md`](ballistic/README.md) made commitment horizon the causal knob directly. Its own 4a run was a confounded-drive negative (the explore/exploit scalar inverted roles under corridor geometry) — answered by manufacturing the quality axis directly rather than abandoning the question, yielding 4b (ballistic transmits FM quality 3.0–3.3× reactive) and 4c (4.3× endogenous reward-free re-adaptation gain). The synthesis: the FM is simultaneously the reward-free-maintainable asset and the ballistic-critical asset — the same object, transmitted only when execution stays committed long enough not to re-ground away the model's advantage.

[`ballistic/directed/README.md`](ballistic/directed/README.md) is this history's clearest epistemic event. S0/S1 landed cleanly, including the finding that ensemble disagreement *structurally cannot* detect a drift its members were trained before (they agree, and are wrong together). S2's headline — "relevance doesn't pay in a loop" — was then **retracted by its own audit**: graded on a metric the node had itself published as blind, 62% contaminated by post-recovery plateau, and resting on a 22× measurement subsidy from free teleported probes. The status was corrected from *negative* to *untested*, and the transferable lesson recorded explicitly: watch for reading a downgrade off an instrument you already know is compromised.

## Phase 4 — rebuilding the substrate rather than the hypothesis

The response to that retraction was to make the substrate itself more honest. [`on_policy/README.md`](on_policy/README.md) built embodied collection (a metered `Body`, no `set_state`), and its own results corrected the team twice more: E0 found most of on-policy's apparent advantage was a mistuned teleport knob, not embodiment; E1 refuted the founding memo's own hypothesis backwards (a global drift re-adapts from any motion — teleport's gradualness was the artifact); E2 found embodiment genuinely costs 2.5× only under *local* drift, via bootstrap data-quality rather than the guessed coverage mechanism. E3 ([`on_policy/directed_on_policy/README.md`](on_policy/directed_on_policy/README.md)) then re-ran the retracted S2 question on this corrected substrate at 1.84× monitoring overhead (vs S2's 22×) and resolved it positive, 3/3 seeds.

[`arm_substrate/README.md`](arm_substrate/README.md) supplied a second task family — needed to retire a standing single-family caveat — and its own corrections mattered as much as its confirmations: capacity does not bind on a 2-link arm at all; an under-sized planner had silently converted transmission into a null in three earlier configurations before this was caught. [`on_policy/metered_repair/README.md`](on_policy/metered_repair/README.md) is the honest sequel to E3: a diagnosis ports cleanly from RHM but the predicted repair does not fully cash out, and a plausible "rate vs level" hypothesis is tested and not supported — a genuine, scoped negative rather than a re-attempted one.

## Phase 5 — the performance-error bridge, tested and matched to structure

[`ideas/performance_error_is_the_bridge.md`](../../ideas/performance_error_is_the_bridge.md) proposed a cerebellar-style teaching signal δ=(b−e)·σ(g/θ); its own §12 pre-emptively falsified one planned experiment as a no-op. Three same-day tests followed. [`agency_gate/README.md`](agency_gate/README.md): the gate is load-bearing (δ fires on self-produced error, silent under passive playback, ~369×) but the literal formula leaks half its channel, forcing a centering fix. [`plasticity_gain/README.md`](plasticity_gain/README.md): gain-consumption buys ballistic speed, but raw error matches δ on control — δ's real content is allocation and retention, and the naive scalar benchmark is pathological, forcing a context-conditional `b(s)`. [`curiosity_control/benchmark_vs_cost/README.md`](curiosity_control/benchmark_vs_cost/README.md): no standing cost term is supported — δ is a gain signal, not an allocation score.

[`two_clocks/README.md`](two_clocks/README.md) then found the idea doc's central bet in a stronger form than expected: performance-error and reward are non-redundant channels, and *sharing between them is destructive interference* — every merged variant loses to both single channels. [`bridge_assembly/README.md`](bridge_assembly/README.md) closed the mechanism while reporting an honest caveat (ungated uniform plasticity wins the short-recovery regime outright) and produced the arc's most transferable law: **which controller transmits an FM difference depends on the difference's spatial structure** — localized staleness reaches behavior through ballistic commitment, diffuse churn through reactive averaging. "Control is a blind grader" is now a matching condition between signal geometry and reader, not a fixed property of either.

## Phase 6 — dimensionality expansion: a well-calibrated null that retires part of the founding memo

Cut #5 was billed in the founding idea doc as the single biggest open question — the intuition that drifting a plant's physics is a novelty generator that should grow a forward model's representable directions. [`beliefs/dimensionality_expansion.md`](../../beliefs/dimensionality_expansion.md)'s own §Scope had already predicted the null in advance: a fixed-DOF arm is a fixed map, drift moves the *target function*, and expansion is growth in the *space of representable functions* — only the second is expansion. [`expansion/README.md`](expansion/README.md) converted that argument into a measurement, built calibrate→measure→calibrate after three prior rank-shaped instruments had failed here: drift moves the readout by ±0.08 directions against a genuine +0.72 ± 0.42 calibration from real DOF accretion — ~10× too small to be the same phenomenon, de-confounded across drift geometry. **Retired**: drift-as-novelty-generator as this program's mechanism. **Preserved**: drift for realism (it still feeds the entire on-policy/drift-loop line); the belief's own §Scope, now unit-calibrated.

The instrument residue turned out to be the more consequential result: gap width *k* (commitment horizon) is the axis on which a wrong model's signature grows (1.4× at k=1 → 2.7–4.1× at the ballistic horizon) — the ballistic/reactive asymmetry showing up in a pure representation readout. Read against [`arm_substrate/README.md`](arm_substrate/README.md)'s composition-horizon measurement, this reframes the open question: a motor plant can't grow *how much* it represents, but it can be asked how far it composes before its own error dominates. That reframing — dimensionality to horizon — is the hinge into the next phase.

## Phase 7 — the practice arc: what to commit, and how far

[`practice/README.md`](practice/README.md) records four δ-consumption runs (`aleatoric_flip/`, `estimability/`, `priced_plasticity/`, plus the difficulty sweep) deliberately as *record, not interpretation* — their yield is apparatus-level, not a finding: Adam is invariant to global loss rescaling (so "matched budget" claims resting on a weight normalizer don't bind), the calibrated gain law is a binary gate rather than a graded one, and Pareto comparisons are sensitive to grid coarseness. The net effect demoted δ from plasticity-allocator to **detector**, the role it keeps for the rest of the arc.

[`practice/etude/README.md`](practice/etude/README.md) is the first node with real sequence structure. Its central finding reframes what "compilation" even means: it is selection-plus-commitment under the consumption distribution, not distillation-by-regression — averaging valid command sequences actively destroys them. Sequential assembly with seam-matched selection dominates never-compiling 3/3 seeds. Its structural coda — committed units are state-independent and fusion is provably vacuous — sets an explicit mandate for the next round: *hierarchy needs boundaries that carry information*.

[`practice/fingering/README.md`](practice/fingering/README.md) supplies such a boundary and inverts the etude's implicit frame: live content under committed *routing* dominates every frozen op, 1.6× at less priced time — the earlier degradation was a property of freezing content, not of committing at all. [`practice/legato/README.md`](practice/legato/README.md) pushes past the model's composition horizon and finds the crossover the whole arc had been converging toward: live planning wins inside the horizon, frozen measured chains win beyond it, and fusing frozen content is free while fusing live plans is catastrophic (2.2×) — a measured chain, produced by the body itself, carries no composition error. [`practice/span/README.md`](practice/span/README.md) closes the loop back onto Phase 6's null: the composition horizon itself roughly doubles with practice even while the flat task metric shows no improvement, but a live plan's reach does not follow — practice sharpens the model on-corridor and degrades it off-corridor. A representational expansion the task metric cannot see, recovered on the very substrate whose *global* frontier (Phase 6) was flat.

## Where this leaves things

Four cross-cutting shifts are worth holding in mind when reading any individual node above: (1) **re-groundability is the standing antagonist** — a good forward model only reaches behavior when execution stays committed past the point where feedback could substitute for it, discovered independently in Cut #3, the ballistic arc, and the bridge-assembly transmission law; (2) **negatives here are treated as instrument or geometry failures to be re-attempted under corrected conditions** far more often than as verdicts — 4a→4b, S2→E3, three failed planner/rank instruments before expansion's calibration held — while genuine, scoped nulls (arity's rank corollary, VoI≈heuristic, metered_repair's rate-tap, the standing cost term) are kept rather than laundered away; (3) **the founding memo's central intuition about drift-as-novelty-generator was retired on its own pre-written terms**, and the null that retired it became the instrument that discovered the composition-horizon axis the practice arc now runs on; (4) **δ was demoted from allocator to detector early and stayed there** — the arc's positive results (agency gating, the two-clocks dissociation, the spatial-matching law) all concern *what a signal is telling you*, not *how much plasticity it should buy*. The practice/étude line is the least settled thread — legato ran single-seed on a cancelled seed pair, and span's horizon-growth result is one seed — and is the most recent and most active edge of the arc.
