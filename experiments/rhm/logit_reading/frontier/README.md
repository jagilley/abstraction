## Goal

Four candidate readings of "where is my frontier", each computable by the learner from its own predictive, each
read against the oracle's per-level residual `KL(p_L‖q)_ℓ` as the ground truth of which level is still news:

- the **excess** `CE_ℓ − H(q)_ℓ`, realised loss beyond stated entropy by leaf level (altitude Q1's identity says
  it is zero in every class the model can scale separately);
- the **loss trend** `−ΔCE_ℓ` between checkpoints, learning progress as a derivative;
- the **period detector**, the offset at which the model's own per-position entropy repeats with period `s^k`
  (altitude Q2 A), and a template-free variant of it;
- the **candidate gain** from altitude Q3, re-read rather than recomputed.

1. On the clean 13-checkpoint trajectory, which readings locate the rung being absorbed at each checkpoint,
   and which go quiet when a level is held? Then the new arm: assign every position's level from the model's
   own entropy period instead of the oracle's parse and recompute the panel. Does it survive losing the labels?
2. Across a corpus wall: replay `reread/lm`'s arms with the panel logged on held-out windows and on each arm's
   own corpus. From the inside, does a level that stalls at the wall look like a level that was absorbed, and
   what if anything tells them apart?

Same substrate, checkpoints and eval windows as the parent throughout; leaf level `ℓ` is `flat_oracle.leaf_levels`
of the target's leaf index, 0 = an odd leaf … 6 = a sequence boundary. Levels are an instrument here, never an
input to the model.

## Instruments

- **The panel** ([`common.py`](common.py), [`panel.py`](panel.py)). Per checkpoint and per leaf level: the exact
  excess `CE_ℓ − H(q)_ℓ` and its realised form `NLL_ℓ − H(q)_ℓ`; the exact trend `−ΔCE_ℓ` and its realised form
  `−ΔNLL_ℓ`; the nested period detector (bottom-up over `k`, template against `E[H(p_k) | leaf level]` or,
  `meanprof`, no template at all: of the `s` candidate offsets take the one with the higher mean entropy); the
  oracle residual `KL(p_L‖q)_ℓ`. The detector's unconstrained per-`k` accuracy reproduces altitude Q2 A exactly
  (max |diff| 0.000 at all 13 checkpoints × 6 periods).
- **The level labels.** Oracle: `leaf_levels` from the window's phase. Endogenous: the detector's estimated
  depth-`L` boundary column induces every position's level by its `s`-adic residue, so one column labels the whole
  window. `field` = the fraction of positions whose induced label equals the oracle's.
- **The wall arms** ([`wall.py`](wall.py)). `lm_reread`'s training loop, model init, optimiser, sampler and
  seeds verbatim; the arms differ only in whether the corpus tensor is redrawn: `fresh`, `frozen_200000` (6.4×
  re-read, renewable in `lm0`), `frozen_16384` (78×, stalls) and `frozen_2048` (625×, stalls). Two venues per
  checkpoint: **V**, 2048 held-out fresh windows shared by every arm and checkpoint; **C**, 2048 windows from the
  arm's own corpus. The corpus is a concatenation of aligned sequences, so a window at offset `ix` has phase
  `ix mod 64` exactly and both venues get exact oracle labels for free. Checkpoints saved at 13 of the 23 steps.
- **Self-checks.** Fidelity gate: every quantity `lm0` logged is reproduced at the 12 shared checkpoints of all
  four arms, `|Δ val_nll| = |Δ train_nll| = 0.0`, `|Δ excess_by_level| ≤ 1.1e−16`. On a fixed window set
  `CE_ℓ = KL(p_L‖q)_ℓ + H(p_L)_ℓ` with the second term constant, so `−ΔCE_ℓ ≡ −ΔKL_ℓ` (max |diff| 5e−16): the
  exact trend *is* the ground truth relabelled, and the genuinely endogenous derivative is `−ΔNLL_ℓ`.

## Q1 — the panel on a learner that climbs

**(a) The excess is zero on-distribution at every level, approached from above at the deep boundaries.**
`CE_ℓ − H(q)_ℓ` in nats, oracle labels:

| step | `κ*` | ℓ=0 | ℓ=1 | ℓ=2 | ℓ=3 | ℓ=4 | ℓ=5 | ℓ=6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.00 | +0.172 | +0.131 | +0.124 | +0.093 | +0.100 | +0.101 | +0.098 |
| 2 000 | 2.55 | −0.002 | −0.025 | −0.067 | +0.064 | +0.087 | +0.071 | +0.082 |
| 8 000 | 3.70 | −0.005 | −0.003 | −0.009 | +0.020 | +0.051 | +0.043 | +0.056 |
| 24 000 | 4.55 | −0.006 | −0.014 | −0.003 | +0.009 | +0.016 | +0.016 | +0.023 |
| 64 000 | 4.90 | −0.003 | −0.009 | −0.007 | −0.000 | +0.007 | +0.010 | +0.013 |

The residue at 64k sits at ℓ = 4–6, above `κ*`, shrinks monotonically, and lies inside altitude Q1's own per-class
band (−0.015 to +0.013). Nowhere does the excess mark the rung being absorbed. The realised form the learner can
actually compute carries a floor the size of the signal: `NLL_ℓ − CE_ℓ`, a pure sampling fluctuation of the one
fixed eval set, is +0.032 to +0.039 at ℓ = 3–6 from step 250 to 64000 and cannot shrink, so at 4096 windows the
endogenous per-level excess reads +0.035 to +0.050 at the deep levels where the exact one reads 0.000 to +0.013.

**(b) The loss trend does not locate the rung.** `argmax_ℓ (−ΔCE_ℓ)` is ℓ=0 for the first interval, ℓ=1 for the
next two, and **ℓ=2 for all ten remaining intervals** while `κ*` climbs 2.2 → 4.9. Normalising by what is left at
each level (`−ΔKL_ℓ / KL_ℓ`) gives a flat 0.22–0.31 across all seven levels late in training with the argmax
wandering. The argmax is pinned by the residual's level-shape, which altitude Q2 B showed is constant over
training: a coarse observer's error is spread over every position inside its subtrees, so the level of a
position is not the coordinate along which absorbing a level shows up. The realised `−ΔNLL_ℓ` reproduces the
exact argmax at 12 of 12 intervals and goes negative at ℓ = 5, 6 on intervals where the exact derivative is
positive.

**(c) The period locates the altitude.** Nested detector accuracy, held-out windows:

| step | `κ*` | k=1 | k=2 | k=3 | k=4 | k=5 | k=6 | field |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.00 | 0.50 | 0.27 | 0.13 | 0.06 | 0.03 | 0.01 | 0.336 |
| 250 | 1.30 | 1.00 | 0.81 | 0.43 | 0.20 | 0.10 | 0.06 | 0.775 |
| 2 000 | 2.55 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.84 | 0.45 | 0.24 | 0.13 | 0.895 |
| 8 000 | 3.70 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.99 | 0.75 | 0.43 | 0.24 | 0.941 |
| 24 000 | 4.55 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.86 | 0.53 | 0.29 | 0.954 |
| 64 000 | 4.90 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.90 | 0.62 | 0.35 | 0.961 |
| chance | | 0.50 | 0.25 | 0.125 | 0.062 | 0.031 | 0.016 | |
| observer `k`'s own ceiling | | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.989 | 0.939 | 0.725 | |

It climbs one period at a time in step with `κ*`, at the observer's own ceiling through `k = 3`. The
template-free `meanprof` variant is 0.00–0.09 lower with the same shape (1.00 / 1.00 / 1.00 / 0.81 / 0.45 / 0.23
at 64k). **(d)** Q3's per-token candidate gain, re-read, crosses zero level by level in order (`j = 1` at step
2000, `j = 2` at 16000, `j = 3–5` still positive at 64k).

**(e) The panel survives losing the oracle's labels, through ℓ = 3.** With levels induced from the model's own
estimated boundary column (`field` above: 0.34 at random init, 0.90 at 2k, 0.96 at 64k):

| ℓ | `P(ℓ̂ = ℓ | ℓ)`, 64k | excess, oracle / endogenous | trend 48k→64k, oracle / endogenous |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1.000 | −0.0027 / −0.0027 | +0.0047 / +0.0047 |
| 1 | 1.000 | −0.0085 / −0.0085 | +0.0065 / +0.0065 |
| 2 | 0.999 | −0.0069 / −0.0069 | +0.0099 / +0.0099 |
| 3 | 0.895 | −0.0001 / +0.0011 | +0.0062 / +0.0070 |
| 4 | 0.624 | +0.0067 / +0.0073 | +0.0040 / +0.0028 |
| 6 | 0.351 | +0.0132 / +0.0084 | +0.0025 / +0.0025 |

Numerically identical at ℓ ≤ 2, within 0.001 at ℓ = 3, degraded above (the endogenous ℓ = 6 excess reads 36%
low). The confusion is one-sided and structured: a level is only ever mistaken for a *deeper* one at the same
2-adic residue, and ℓ = 0, 1 are never confused after step 0. The endogenous trend's argmax agrees with the
oracle's at 12 of 12 intervals, for both detector variants.

> **Of the four readings, the excess is silent on-distribution at every level, exactly as the identity says,
> and the per-level loss trend is a global quantity wearing a per-level costume. What tracks the altitude from
> the inside is the shape of the predictive — its period — model-free, at the observer's own ceiling through
> `k = 3`, and well enough that its induced level field replaces the oracle's labels below ℓ = 4 with no loss in
> the rest of the panel.**

## Q2 — the wall from the inside

**The identity holds on the arm's own corpus, wall or no wall.** Own-corpus excess stays ≈ 0 for every arm at
every checkpoint: `frozen_16384` within ±0.08 nats at every level to step 20000 while its held-out excess reaches
+5.25, `frozen_2048` within ±0.03 while its held-out reaches +9.17. Held-out excess at 20000 across levels: `fresh`
−0.009 to +0.051, `frozen_200000` −0.005 to +0.053, `frozen_16384` +2.04 to +5.25, `frozen_2048` +3.96 to +9.17,
against a step-0 floor of +0.088 to +0.118 at every level. The renewable arms never clear their own floor; the
walled arms clear it by 40–90×.

**To a derivative, a stalled level looks absorbed — on held-out data.** The exact held-out trend goes
persistently ≤ 0 at steps 3466–5372 (`frozen_16384`) and 482–1443 (`frozen_2048`), and never for `fresh` or
`frozen_200000`. The scope caveat to carry: within this 20k-step ladder no level of `fresh` went quiet either,
and neither did any level at 64k in Q1, so absorption never produced silence in the trend within the budgets run.
Only the wall's silence is on the record.

**What tells them apart, and when** (first persistent crossing; floors are the step-0 checkpoint; `–` = never):

| reading | `frozen_16384` | `frozen_2048` | `fresh` | `frozen_200000` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| held-out trend ≤ 0 onward (the stall) | 3466 (ℓ=3–6) / 5372 (ℓ=0–2) | 482–1443 | – | – |
| held-out excess above its step-0 value | 5372–6687 | 748–1796 | – | – |
| held-out minus own-corpus excess > 0.05 | 5372–6687 | 1159 | – | – |
| per-level memorisation gap > 0.05 | **4315** (ℓ=0), 5372–6687 | 1159–1443 | – | – |

Relative to the stall at ℓ = 2 in `frozen_16384`: the excess bottoms at +0.023 at step 3466, is +0.031 at 4315 and
+0.056 at 5372, while the exact trend is still +0.006 at 4315 and first negative at 5372. The excess and the gap
begin rising one checkpoint before the derivative turns and clear the step-0 floor one to two checkpoints after
it; the one reading that leads outright is the ℓ = 0 memorisation gap (4315 against a stall at 5372).

**The period is the latest indicator and the most exclusive signature, and it needs no held-out venue.** On
held-out windows the renewable arms peak at the final checkpoint with a drop of 0.000 at every `k`; the walled
arms peak and then fall:

| arm | period | peak (step) | final, 20000 | drop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fresh` | k=3 | 0.993 (20000) | 0.993 | 0.000 |
| `frozen_200000` | k=3 | 0.990 (20000) | 0.990 | 0.000 |
| `frozen_16384` | k=2 | 1.000 (3466) | 0.977 | 0.023 |
| `frozen_16384` | k=3 | 0.714 (6687) | 0.574 | 0.141 |
| `frozen_2048` | k=2 | 0.940 (1443) | 0.749 | 0.191 |
| `frozen_2048` | k=3 | 0.463 (1796) | 0.373 | 0.090 |

The same reversal is there on the arm's **own corpus**: `frozen_16384` k=3 0.78 at 6687 → 0.58 at 20000 and k=2
1.00 → 0.96; `frozen_2048` k=2 0.98 at 1796 → 0.69 and k=3 0.54 → 0.36. A level that was absorbed keeps its
period; a level lost to the wall gives it back, and it gives it back on the data the learner already has.

**The wall is not level-local in these readouts.** `lm0`'s finding 3 has the small arms stalling at the next level;
here the held-out excess and the per-level gap degrade at all seven levels within one or two checkpoints of each
other, the fully absorbed ℓ = 0, 1 included, consistent with `lm0` finding 5.

> **On the learner's own stream the excess is silent whatever the world is doing, and the derivative goes quiet
> at a wall as it would at absorption. The two are told apart by the period going backwards — which the learner
> can see on its own corpus — and, one checkpoint earlier, by the excess and the memorisation gap, which need a
> held-out venue the learner would have to construct for itself.**

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes** (one grammar, one architecture; the clean trajectory at 13 checkpoints; four reread arms at 23
checkpoints, uniform synonyms; one seed throughout):

- The per-level excess is ≈ 0 on the training distribution at every level and every checkpoint after ~2k,
  including the rung being absorbed, and stays ≈ 0 on a walled learner's own corpus while its held-out excess is
  nats off. It is a shift detector at every level and a frontier detector at none.
- The per-level loss trend's argmax is pinned at ℓ = 2 by the residual's fixed level-shape and never tracks `κ*`.
  Learning progress by position level does not locate the rung.
- The entropy profile's period tracks `κ*` one level at a time, at the observer's own ceiling through `k = 3`,
  template-free within 0.09, and its induced level field replaces the oracle's labels below ℓ = 4 with the rest
  of the panel unchanged.
- Across a corpus wall the held-out derivative goes quiet as absorption would, the held-out excess and the
  memorisation gap turn one checkpoint earlier, and the period goes backwards — on held-out data and on the
  arm's own corpus — where a renewable arm's period never drops.

**Does not establish:**

- What absorption's own silence looks like: no level of any renewable arm, or of the clean trajectory at 64k, had
  gone quiet within the budgets run. The "both go quiet" comparison has one side.
- Whether the period reading, or any reading here, can *drive* anything. Every consumer is absent; this is the
  gauge, not the loop.
- A frontier derivative in a better coordinate. The candidate coordinate (altitude Q3) crosses zero in order and
  was re-read, not re-derived; the position-level coordinate is the one shown not to work.
- Behaviour at the deep levels the detector cannot yet resolve (ℓ ≥ 4 at this model size), or off `v16 s2 L6 m4`.

## Gotchas worth not rediscovering

- **The exact per-level trend is the oracle residual relabelled.** On a fixed window set `−ΔCE_ℓ ≡ −ΔKL_ℓ`.
  If the claim is about what the learner can read, report `−ΔNLL_ℓ` and its agreement with the exact one.
- **A fixed eval set puts a floor under the realised excess.** `NLL_ℓ − CE_ℓ` is +0.03 to +0.04 at the deep
  levels and does not shrink with training. Below that, a realised per-level excess is reading its own tokens.
- **Phase on a re-read corpus is free.** `lm_reread`'s corpus is a concatenation of aligned sequences, so
  `ix mod 64` is the phase; no new instrument is needed to label levels on either venue.
- **A "persistent from here on" crossing rule catches late blips.** `frozen_2048`'s stall column reads 16065 at
  some levels because of one late positive interval in an arm diverging since ~1500; the first-turn column (482)
  is the honest early read.
- **Keep launch logs out of the tree.** This node's `results/launch_w0.log` raced Modal's source mount for a
  sibling node's builds on about half its attempts, the `ignore` notwithstanding. Write launch logs to the
  scratchpad.
