## One-liner

> The trunk's pre-event entropy is a real axis of the value reader and the arc had never read it. The norm carries it as a cost and rescales
> with the world; the response at matched surprisal runs the other way on the arc's instrument, and at the shallowest level the sign of the
> surprisal effect flips with the context's uncertainty. Half of the response's entropy dependence is the forecast moving more in confident
> contexts; the entropy's own knowledge of shallow-level damage is in no measure of movement. What the ridge on the raw state lacks is a
> nonlinear function of the belief: appending the entropy buys fit and nothing else, appending the sixteen log-probabilities or reading the
> state nonlinearly absorbs what the entropy knew and reverses the response's sign and its interaction, and layer norm does neither. The
> claims about the expectation, its calibration, its clock, its public readability, and the legality law, come out the same on every reader.
> The claims about the response at the event depend on how much of the belief the reader can see.

## Goal

1. At matched surprisal, do the norm, the response and the outcome surprise depend on the entropy of the trunk's forecast before the event,
   with what sign, at which levels, on a trained trunk against a random-init one? What does the 2-D (surprisal × entropy) surface of the
   response look like, the direct analogue of the literature's behavioural surface?
2. Is the response's entropy dependence carried by how much the event moved the world model (the forecast, the state), and is the entropy's
   own reading of damage?
3. Does the same-prefix twin contrast and the edit-versus-glitch persistence dissociation depend on the axis?
4. What does a reader with more expressivity than the ridge do on this axis: the entropy appended, the belief appended, a placebo, a
   precision-weighted form, an MLP, the layer-normed state?
5. Does the arc's law, legality at chance at matched consequence and surprisal, survive a reader that sees the belief, on a venue with
   enough legal-but-rare edits to read it?
6. Do the banked level-level claims, the norm's calibration, its clock, and the public–private split, survive those readers on identical rows?

## Instruments

- **The axis.** `H_pre = H(q_{t0−1})`, the entropy of the trunk's forecast of the token at the anchor, made one position before it, on the edited
  stream; on file per fixed row as `<anchor>__H_pre`, recomputed for the twin pairs by one forward pass per cell (`hpre.py`, gated against the
  banked surprisals to 1e−05). `excess_e = nll_e − H_pre` is on file and checked per cell, so **at matched surprisal the entropy axis is the negative
  excess axis**; every interaction fit is printed in both parameterisations.
- **The rows and the matching.** `norm/`'s banked cells, the held-out test rows at both anchors; `junction/analyze.py`'s matcher (exact strata on
  `k*`, `j`, a 4-wide position bucket; 1:1 on surprisal; sign-balanced), which never sees the axis. The strata explain 0.115 of `Var(H_pre)` and
  0.704 of `Var(nll_e)` at 64k, so the axis passes through the matching almost intact while the surprisal is nearly consumed by it.
- **The per-sd column.** A random-init trunk forecasts near-uniformly on every row (`H_pre` sd 0.017 against 0.669 at 64k, ceiling ln 16 = 2.77),
  so a raw step-0 slope is not comparable to a trained one; every slope is also printed as `b · sd(H)`, the readout's change over one sd of the axis.
- **The mediators** (`mediate.py`): the forecast's movement across the event (`phasic.py`'s KL), the post-event entropy, the residual stream's
  movement at three blocks, and for pairs the state distance between members.
- **The arms** (`express.py`): the same trunk, diet rows, split, lambda ladder and held-out selection as `norm/task.py`, the feature map augmented,
  every appended column standardised through the Gram to the state's per-dimension RMS, arm 0 gated against the banked columns before anything is
  read (6e−08). `+H`, `+log q` (16), `+norm` and `+tanh` (placebos), `+precision` (`H(q_t)`, `H(q_{t−1})`, `s_t`, `H(q_{t−1})·s_t`), `MLP`
  (128 units, `striatum/task.py`'s recipe, no lambda selection), `ln` (the model's own `ln_f` on the state, in place), `+ln`, `std`.
- **The venue for the law** (`legal65k`): 65,536 windows, half swap and half rare, no `none`, parse replay bit-identical; thousands of consequential
  matched pairs per level where `a1` had hundreds.
- **The reading rule** ([`../../../orbitofrontal/README.md`](../../../orbitofrontal/README.md) §5): a difference between conditions on identical rows is
  a claim; a rate or a direction along one trajectory mostly is not. Seeds are printed side by side and never averaged.

## 1. The axis and its guards

`H_pre` on the `swap65k` `t_v` fixed rows (n = 13,428, identical at every checkpoint): mean 2.711, sd 0.017 at step 0; 1.039, 0.711 at 8k; 0.856,
0.669 at 64k. At 64k it correlates with `k*` (+0.33), `j` (+0.28), position (+0.18) and surprisal (−0.31), with no two-position constituent-phase
sawtooth (r = 0.009 on `t0 mod 2`). Across seeds it is near-identical.

**The axis itself ranks realised damage where the surprisal cannot.** On surprisal-matched rows (guard 0.500), `AUC(H_pre, flip)` at ℓ=1 is
0.620 / 0.625 / 0.628 at 64k and 0.651 / 0.638 / 0.643 at 8k on the three seeds, at chance at ℓ=2–4 and at step 0. Uncertain contexts carry more
shallow-level damage.

## 4. The outcome surprise: the norm is short by half at ℓ=1 and over-adjusted at ℓ=3–4

Raw partial slopes on `H_pre`, `swap65k` `t_v` 64k, `full`, three seeds: the realised outcome falls at −0.124 / −0.123 / −0.129 at ℓ=1 while the
norm falls at −0.066 / −0.070 / −0.068, so `δ ~ H_pre` is **−0.059 / −0.052 / −0.061** (t −11 / −10 / −11); at ℓ=2 the two cancel to three
decimals on every seed; at ℓ=3–4 the norm falls faster than the outcome and `δ` goes positive (+0.017 / +0.013 / +0.017 at ℓ=3). At 8k the ℓ=1
under-adjustment is a third of this. **No reader form closes the 64k ℓ=1 gap** (§8): every arm moves the norm by at most a fifth of the way and
`δ` stays past eight standard errors.

## 5. The twins

`H_pre` is identical within a pair by construction. The pairs sit on the uncertain end of the axis (mean 1.575 against 0.856 on the fixed rows at
64k), because a legal token of matched surprisal after an identical prefix mostly exists where the context is uncertain. Token-balanced `dR ~ H_pre`
residualised on `sec_twin_kstar`'s control set, per sd, 64k: ℓ=2 +0.0064 / +0.0042 / +0.0027, ℓ=3 **+0.0068 / +0.0061 / +0.0051** (2.6 / 2.4 /
2.0σ), ℓ=4 +0.0095 / +0.0077 / +0.0069 (4.4 / 4.2 / 3.6σ); **at 8k the sign runs the other way at ℓ=1–3 on all three seeds**, so this is a
64k-only positive on a narrow slice, not a training trend. The `dR ~ V_pre` slope of norm §3 survives partialling out `H_pre` and strengthens
(ℓ=1 0.176 → 0.296 on `s42`): the two dependences are not one. `a1`'s twins are too thin for the axis.

## 6. What carries the response's entropy dependence: half is the forecast moving, and the damage side is not

**The premise holds and splits.** At matched surprisal, an event in an uncertain context moves the **forecast** far less (`kl_update` −0.51 sd per
sd of `H_pre`, t −67) and the **representation** at the critic's block slightly more (`dstate7` +0.16 sd per sd). The published belief and the
residual stream disagree about how much happened. The post-event entropy is a level on `H_pre`'s scale (r = −0.73), not a movement, so a control
set containing it removes the axis partly by arithmetic; the `+ movement only` set (`kl` and the three `dstate`) is the honest one.

**The mediation is partial and level-dependent.** `R ~ H_pre` per sd with movement in the controls, matched rows, 64k: ℓ=1 +0.031 → **+0.009 /
+0.018 / +0.015** (3.1 / 5.9 / 5.3σ), ℓ=2 → ≈ 0, ℓ=3 → −0.012 (reverses); at 8k the ℓ=1–2 rows keep most of their slope. The ℓ=1 interaction
keeps about two-thirds with all five mediators in the fit (3.6–6.2σ). Inside quantile bins of either mediator the response keeps essentially its
whole marginal slope, and inside bins of the response each mediator keeps its whole one: the reduction comes from the movements acting jointly.

**The entropy's damage reading does not go through the mediators.** `AUC(H_pre, flip)` at ℓ=1 on the matched rows stays 0.60–0.63 given `kl`,
given `dstate7`, and residualised on all five, while the mediators themselves rank damage at 0.42–0.52. Whatever the pre-event uncertainty knows
about how the episode ends at ℓ=1 is not in how far the event moved the model.

> **Visibility, read as "a confident model that is surprised revises more", is true of the belief and accounts for most of the response's
> entropy dependence above ℓ=1 and half of it at ℓ=1. It is false of the residual stream, and it is not where the entropy's knowledge of
> shallow-level damage lives.**

## 7. The glitch world: the persistence dissociation is entropy-independent

`basalis/`'s glitch world on the same windows at the same `t_v` (clean prefix, a token illegal under it, the untouched continuation). The two
worlds' entropies are not matched to each other (mean absolute difference 0.22 nats per common window at 64k, r ≈ 0.64; the caliper-selected pairs

uncertainty monotonically at ℓ=3 on all three seeds and low < mid at ℓ=2. The event-level twin gap matches the edit world's sign at ℓ=3–4 and not
at ℓ=1–2, on thin cells.

## 8. What the reader's form does: the missing thing is a nonlinear function of the belief, not its entropy and not normalisation

- **The entropy scalar was the wrong guess.** `+H` raises the fit as much as `+log q` and contains nothing: `H_pre` ranks damage slightly
  *better* given `+H`'s level than given the ridge's. The `+precision` arm's interaction contains its own supplied `H·s` column and is not emergent.
- **The belief's sixteen numbers, or a nonlinear reader, absorb what the entropy knew** and read damage 0.04–0.10 AUC better at ℓ=1–2; `a1` and
  the clean-only critic reproduce the pattern (the clean critic's absorber is `+precision`).
- **The ℓ=1 sign reversal.** Every reader that sees more of the belief reverses the response's entropy slope and its interaction on all three
  seeds; the placebos leave both untouched. Under those readers a surprising token is worse news in an uncertain context, the direction the damage
  runs (§1).
- **Normalisation is not the shape.** `ln` and `std` are identical to four decimals (the learned affine plays no role for a ridge); `ln` in place
  loses fit at ℓ=3–4 (the raw stream's scale carries something about deep levels); `+ln` recovers about 70% of `+log q`'s fit gain and none of its
  containment. The logits are linear in `ln_f(s)`, so a ridge on `ln_f(s)` already spans every linear function of them; `log q` differs from the
  logits by one number per row, the log-partition. The candidates left are that scalar, or the ridge's shrinkage under-using the belief's
  directions when they sit inside 256 normalised dimensions and using them when handed over as sixteen columns. A `+logits` arm separates them; it ran
  2026-09-23 (§8a below).
- **What no reader fixes**: the 64k ℓ=1 under-adjustment of the norm along the axis (§4). The same arms move the twin optimism of norm §3 up
  three-fold (`+log q`) and five-fold (`MLP`).
- **Fit at random init moves only under the MLP** (+0.03); the augmented linear arms add nothing there because the belief is near-uniform.

> **The linear reader on the raw state prices the belief's level and misses a nonlinear function of the belief that carries shallow-level damage;
> the entropy is not that function and normalisation is not the missing operation. The sign of the response's surprise-by-entropy interaction
> belongs to the reader's form on this record, not to the world or the target: readers with the belief in hand agree with each other and disagree
> with the ridge.**

| `+logits` (`s` + the 16 raw logits: the directions, no log-partition) | +0.004 / +0.003 / +0.004 | 0.607 / 0.621 / 0.608 | +0.032 / +0.034 / +0.028 | +0.011 / +0.011 / +0.015 | 0.611 / 0.583 / 0.601 |

| `+logits +lse` (contains `+log q`'s features) | +0.030 / +0.028 / +0.028 | 0.546 / 0.562 / 0.547 | −0.025 / −0.018 / −0.023 | −0.027 / −0.019 / −0.020 | 0.677 / 0.653 / 0.673 |

- **The log-partition candidate wins, cleanly.** The directions handed over without the scalar behave like `+ln` on every column, seed, venue and
  level; one scalar on the normalised basis behaves like `+log q` on all five columns; `+logits +lse` equals `+log q` column for column. The span
  diagnostic (section S): each logit is 98% linear in the raw state and `lse` 95–97% linear in `[s]`, `[s, z]` and `ln_f(s)`, so the arms differ
  only through a few percent of nonlinear residual, which carries the whole effect.
- **Which scalar is less settled.** `ln +max` does as well as `ln +lse`, so the record identifies a scalar of the logits' absolute level rather than
  the log-partition as such; `+H`, a nonlinear scalar of the belief invariant to shifting the logits, gives fit and no containment (§8). At 8k the
  scalar arms contain more than `+log q` (0.578 / 0.571 / 0.590 against 0.600 / 0.586 / 0.608) and leave the slope near zero. On the clean-only
  critic `ln +lse` is the strongest arm of any (0.509 / 0.544 / 0.531). The `ln`-based arms carry `ln`'s fit loss at ℓ=3–4 (to −0.010); `+lse` and
  `+logits +lse` keep `+log q`'s deep-level gain. Twins at ℓ=1, 64k: `+logits` equals the ridge (+0.009 / +0.009 / +0.011) and `ln +lse` sits near
  `+log q` (+0.021 / +0.021 / +0.023 against +0.023 / +0.026 / +0.027).
- A four-arm follow-up on `swap65k` (`ln +H`, `ln + log q_max`, `+max`, `ln + ||z||`, ~25 L4-min) would separate `lse` from `max`; designed, not run.
- **Read across substrates**: on the practice plant the block logits are linear in the pooled state, so a belief-appended reader there adds only
  this scalar, and on the outcome-shaped plant it adds nothing to the read ([`aliquot/rubato/README.md`](../../../../practice/voicing/sotto_voce/aliquot/rubato/README.md) §2).

> **What the ridge on the raw state lacks is how peaked the belief is, one number; the belief's directions it already has.**

## 9. The law survives a reader that sees the belief

On `legal65k` (three seeds, 8k and 64k, the onset anchor `fd`, the only anchor where a rare edit exists), swap against rare among consequential

The exact split of `+log q`'s response into a state part and a belief part is on the record: at face value the state part carries the ℓ=1 sign

> **Everything about the expectation is robust to what the reader is made of: its calibration, its clock, its public readability, and the law.
> Everything about the response at the event is not: its sign along the entropy axis, its interaction, whether it is private, and the size of its
> bet on the violator depend on how much of the belief the reader can see and how nonlinearly. The honest phrasing of the split is that the level
> is linearly public, the shallow-level response is public to a nonlinear read of the belief, and the deep-level response needs the state.**

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes** (one grammar, one trunk architecture, three trajectory seeds on every fixed-row reading, two order seeds on the clock, both venues
and anchors, every reproduction gate against the banked cells exact):

- The trunk's pre-event entropy is a load-bearing axis of the value reader: the norm falls with it in rank order with the world and calibration
  rescales with the diet; the response at matched surprisal rises with it on the ridge, half of that contained by the level at ℓ=1–2 and all at
  ℓ=3–4; the norm under-adjusts along it at ℓ=1 and over-adjusts at ℓ=3–4; the twins' dependence is a 64k-only positive on a narrow slice.
- Half of the response's entropy dependence is the forecast moving more in confident contexts; the entropy's own reading of shallow-level damage
  (0.62 at matched surprisal) is in no measure of the event's movement.
- The persistence dissociation between the edit and glitch worlds holds inside every entropy tercile.
- What the linear state reader lacks is a nonlinear function of the belief: the entropy appended buys fit and no containment; the belief appended

  layer norm recovers fit and no containment; the placebos do nothing.
- Legality at matched consequence and surprisal is at chance for every reader on a venue with thousands of pairs per level.
- The norm's calibration, its clock and its public readability hold on every reader; the response's privacy holds against linear public readers
  and not against a nonlinear one at the shallow levels.

## Gotchas worth not rediscovering

The full list is in [FILES.md](FILES.md) and [`rereads/FILES.md`](rereads/FILES.md); the ones that changed a reading:

- **The random-init trunk is a degenerate floor for any entropy-shaped axis.** Its forecast is near-uniform, so "absent at random init" is weak
  here; read the per-sd column.
- **`H_next` is a level, not a movement** (r = −0.73 with `H_pre`); a mediation column containing it removes the axis partly by arithmetic.
- **An appended column must be standardised through the Gram** before it meets a shared ridge penalty, or the penalty crushes or favours it for
  no reason; `express.py` does this exactly and maps the coefficients back.
- **Width and consequence depth cannot be pinned together** on a swap-versus-rare contrast; match both ways and control only what each pins.
- **The two worlds' entropies and surprisals are not matched to each other**; read each world on its own pairs.
- **The exact split of an augmented linear critic is collinear**; read part-level numbers beside their sum, never alone.
- **Modal volumes commit in the background**: a cell's json can be visible before its last npz is written; wait on the app's state, not on file
  counts, and fetch sequentially.
