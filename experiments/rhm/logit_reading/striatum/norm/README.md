## Goal

Three objects, in the arc's terms. The critic's level `V(s_{t−1})` before the event is its learned expectation of
cost given the state — the **norm**. At the violation the state moves and the critic revises: `R = V(s_t) −
V(s_{t−1})` is the anticipatory **response**, the value-side entry. Later the actor answers and `δ = outcome −
V(s_{t−1})` is the **outcome surprise**, the practice arc's value-currency delta. Xiang's feeling is reported when
the offer arrives, which in the ultimatum game is event and outcome at once; here the two are separated by the
horizon and both are readouts.

1. **Across worlds.** Raise critics in worlds that differ only in expected cost. On identical held-out rows, does
   the norm calibrate to the world, and is the calibration a shift or a rescaling? Does the response to the
   identical event scale with the world's expectation, and does the outcome surprise?
2. **Within a world.** Xiang's contrast inside one critic: does the response to the same violation depend on what
   was expected just before it? Regression to the mean confounds this directly, so use the parent's same-prefix
   twins, which share `s_{t−1}` by construction.
3. **Scope.** Does the norm, or only the response, switch on with the absorbed levels — the value side's Part 2a.

## 1. The norm calibrates to the world, and training turns a shift into a rescaling

`V(s_{t−1})` on the fixed violation rows, `a1` `t_v` 64k:

| diet | ℓ=1 | ℓ=2 | ℓ=3 | ℓ=4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `out_lo` | 0.902 | 0.813 | 0.627 | 0.343 |
| `out_mid` | 0.927 | 0.863 | 0.729 | 0.485 |
| `out_hi` | 0.940 | 0.897 | 0.799 | 0.621 |

Spearman ρ against the diet's E[outcome] is **+1.000 inside every family, at every level, checkpoint and anchor**,
and +0.92 to +0.99 over all eleven diets; the spread grows with level (0.037 at ℓ = 1, 0.278 at ℓ = 4).
Regressing each diet's norm on `full`'s across the identical rows (ℓ = 1, `a1` `t_v`): **at 64k** slopes 1.16
(`out_lo`) → 0.82 (`out_hi`), sd ratios 1.18 → 0.84, R² 0.95–0.98; **at step 0** slopes 0.97–1.01 and sd ratios
1.00–1.02 with the same mean shifts. A random-init trunk's norm shifts as a pure intercept; the trained trunk's
norm also rescales — a low-outcome world gives the critic a wider dynamic range on the same states, a
high-outcome world a compressed one. R² < 1 says the map is not exactly affine either.

> **Re-read 2026-09-17, held as the epistemically uncertain reading.** The rank order and the slopes reproduce to
> three decimals on two more trajectory seeds ([`results/tables_s43.md`](results/tables_s43.md) §0b,
> [`tables_s44.md`](results/tables_s44.md) §0b: `out_lo` slopes 1.161 / 1.176 / 1.164, `out_hi` 0.816 / 0.787 / 0.792)
> and on the practice learner's own judge with its trunk held fixed ([`tessitura`](../../../practice/voicing/tessitura/README.md)'s
> refit: ρ = +1.000 in every family, level within ±0.03 across a fivefold range). But that judge emits a logit through a
> sigmoid, and on it a **random-init trunk rescales by the same amount as the trained one** (0.557 / 0.871 / 1.153
> against 0.552 / 0.993 / 1.188): the level's calibration is a property of the link function on any feature map,
> and what the trained representation buys there is discrimination (AUC 0.618–0.652 against 0.572–0.581). Whether
> the shift-only random-trunk control above is the general case or the ridge's, is open; read this section's
> calibration beside a discrimination claim. The level is also *public*: a critic refit on the model's output
> distribution alone reproduces these rank orders and slopes ([`orbitofrontal/projection/`](../../orbitofrontal/projection/README.md)),
> while the response of §2–§3 is not.
>
> **Re-read 2026-09-19, the discrimination control run** ([`results/tables_discrim_20260919.md`](results/tables_discrim_20260919.md),
> [`discrim.py`](discrim.py), CPU on the banked cells, three trajectory seeds). Within cells (matched on `k*`, `j` and
> position, sign-balanced on surprisal) the post-event level's ranking of realised damage at the violation token goes from
> 0.50–0.63 on the step-0 trunk to 0.54–0.72 at 64k, a gain of +0.05 to +0.14 at ℓ = 2–4 on both venues and mixed at
> ℓ = 1, on all three seeds; at the edit onset +0.06 to +0.19. So the ridge's random-trunk control is not the sigmoid's
> shift-only case in discrimination either: the trained representation buys the readout a modest, seed-stable ranking
> gain, and the random trunk sits near chance within cells. Two limits: the outcome label is the frozen actor's own hit,
> whose base rate moves with the checkpoint (ℓ = 1: 0.40 → 0.68), so the control holds the rows fixed and not the label;
> and pooled over cells the pre-event level reads the outcome at 0.66–0.73 at the edit onset from a state that precedes
> any edited token, so pooled gains carry the query's cell. The grammar's consequence label, which is fixed, reads at or
> below chance at the violation token at 64k. Motivated by
> [`practice/voicing/sotto_voce/aliquot/`](../../../practice/voicing/sotto_voce/aliquot/README.md) §2, where a random
> 2-layer map plus the link function read the practice verdict within 0.03 of the live plant.

## 2. The response to the identical event does not order with the world

On the same fixed rows with `k*`, `j`, position and surprisal pinned (guards 0.497–0.503 on every table; matched
n 224–352 at `a1` `t_v`, 2100–2776 at `swap65k` `t_v`, 384–836 at the `a1` onset):

| response in each diet's own units, ℓ=3, `a1` `t_v` 64k | low | mid | high | ρ vs E[outcome] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `out` family | −0.500 | −0.611 | −0.635 | −1 |
| `dmg` family | −0.525 | −0.583 | −0.614 | +1 |
| `mix` family | −0.617 | −0.600 | −0.565 | +1 |

Raw `R`'s ρ against E[outcome] runs −0.73 to +0.32 across levels with the sign flipping; `R − Ro` is weakly
positive overall (+0.13 to +0.64); the scaled response is monotone inside every family and reverses between
families, and since E[outcome] and E[damage] are anti-correlated inside every family the same reversal holds
against E[damage]. `R`'s realised-damage AUC is diet-independent: 0.553–0.579 / 0.544–0.569 / 0.537–0.574 /
0.525–0.534 for ℓ = 1–4 across all eleven diets, `V`'s 0.648–0.672 / 0.674–0.694 / 0.616–0.630 / 0.541–0.556,
the clean-only critic inside those spans. The full ordering matrix is in the tables (§4b of each cell).

**The outcome surprise.** On fixed rows the outcome is identical for every critic (`a1` `t_v` 64k, ℓ = 2:
0.5913), so `δ = outcome − V(s_{t−1})` orders inversely with the world's expectation: −0.221 (`out_lo`) / −0.272 /
−0.306 (`out_hi`), ℓ = 1 −0.219 / −0.243 / −0.256, and the ordering persists to offsets 4 and 8. This is §1
restated in the currency Xiang's reported feeling tracks — the identical-outcome, different-history contrast —
and it is flagged as such in the table.

> **The norm is a learned expectation over outcomes and it adapts to the world it was fed; what the world sets is
> where the critic's zero is and how wide its scale opens, not how hard it reacts to an event. Xiang's headline —
> the same offer feeling worse to the group that learned to expect more — reproduces in the outcome surprise,
> where it is the calibration of the norm restated, and does not reproduce in the anticipatory response.**

## 3. Within a world: the same-prefix twins

`swap65k` 64k: 45,000 eligible violations, 15,266 pairs at the wide caliper, 4,700 at the parent's 0.30 (2,619
token-balanced, 1,191 on the test split), mean `|Δs|` 0.135; coverage is `k*`-skewed by the caliper (pairs at 0.30
by `k*` = 1…6: 7 / 138 / 404 / 1563 / 2487 / 101 — the matchable violations are the ones the model barely
notices). `a1` 64k gives 713 pairs at 0.30.

**The confound is real and `dR` cancels it exactly.** `R(violator) ~ V_pre` has slope −0.19 / −0.35 / −0.54 / −0.54
(ℓ = 1…4) and `R(twin) ~ V_pre` the same, −0.39 / −0.44 / −0.51 / −0.52: shared by construction.

**`dR` is positive, not token identity, absent at random init, and grows with training.** Token-balanced, critic
`full`, `swap65k`:

| step | ℓ=1 | ℓ=2 | ℓ=3 | ℓ=4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | −0.0008 ± 0.0011 | −0.0003 ± 0.0004 | −0.0001 ± 0.0002 | −0.0001 ± 0.0001 |
| 8 000 | +0.0085 ± 0.0023 | +0.0132 ± 0.0023 | +0.0028 ± 0.0022 | −0.0028 ± 0.0014 |
| 64 000 | **+0.0077 ± 0.0028** | **+0.0124 ± 0.0028** | **+0.0127 ± 0.0026** | +0.0043 ± 0.0021 |

`V` predicts the actor being **right**, so positive is better news: after an illegal token the critic is *more*
optimistic than after a surprisal-matched legal twin sharing the identical prefix. The marginal (not
token-balanced) values are roughly double and sit beside these in the tables.

**Does `dR` scale with the pre-event expectation?** Token-balanced 64k slopes `dR ~ V_pre` +0.176 (ρ 0.100) /
+0.081 (ρ 0.069) / −0.018 / −0.012 for ℓ = 1…4, the same on the test split and on all pairs; residualised on
`(k*, j, position, violator surprisal, |Δs|)` +0.188 / +0.079 / −0.053 / −0.050; inside `k*` strata at ℓ = 1
+0.557 (`k*` = 3), +0.273 (4), +0.128 (5), +0.064 (6), −0.212 (2). A weak positive dependence at ℓ = 1–2, flat to
slightly negative above — and **at step 0 the ℓ = 1 slope is already +0.106**, so ℓ = 1's dependence is not a
training effect; ℓ = 2's is (+0.0006 → +0.081). Quintile bins are in the tables and figure.

**Matched persistence** (token-balanced, ℓ = 3): `dV` = 0.013 at offset 0, then 0.085 / 0.069 / 0.087 / 0.047 /
0.082 / 0.030 / 0.029 out to offset 8, on a two-position sawtooth (the constituent-phase artefact of striatum §4,
which cancels in `dV` and is visible in the levels). Only offset 0 is a clean event reading: after `t_v` both
streams carry the same *edited* continuation, the swap's legally regrown subtree, coherent with the violator's
token and not with the twin's, so the later offsets are not a decay of the event.

> **Token-balanced, at matched surprisal and an identical prefix, an outcome-trained reader that was never told
> what a rule is prices the illegal token as better news than its legal twin, from nil at random init to three
> to five standard errors at 64k. That is the value-side echo of the parent's §2e and of coeruleus's
> reinterpretation reading: the swap regrows the subtree legally, the violator's token is the one the
> continuation belongs to, and the value channel has learned to bet on it.**

> **Tested 2026-09-17 (§5).** The bet reading is half right. On a glitch world where re-parsing is the wrong
> policy the event-level optimism is the same size, so it is a property of an impossible token arriving, not of
> what the token turns out to have been right about. What belongs to the continuation is the *persistence*:
> eight tokens of held advantage in the edit world, none in the glitch world, from an identical starting point.

> **Re-read 2026-09-17 on three trajectory seeds** ([`results/tables_s44.md`](results/tables_s44.md) §0b). The ℓ = 1–3
> rows reproduce on all three seeds, every seed past 2σ at every level. **The ℓ = 4 row has no stable sign** (`−++`
> at 8k and `+−−` at 64k across `s42` / `s43` / `s44`, the majority sign flipping between checkpoints) and is retired.
> The `dR ~ V_pre` question above was answered within-subject in [`orbitofrontal/adaptation/`](../../orbitofrontal/adaptation/README.md):
> the response carries the running history and re-calibrates on the norm's own clock, at two order seeds.

## 4. Scope

`swap65k` `t_v`, identical rows at every checkpoint, critic `full`. The response is level-scoped and the scope
extends with training; the norm is only weakly `k*`-dependent, as it should be for an event that has not happened
yet.

| ℓ=3 `R` by the violation's `k*` | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| step 8 000 | −0.116 | −0.107 | −0.072 | +0.004 | +0.007 |
| step 64 000 | −0.119 | −0.108 | −0.114 | −0.031 | +0.003 |

At a level-ℓ query the value revision is a drop only for violations at `k*` ≲ ℓ, and the `k*` = 3 and 4 cells

## 5. Addendum (2026-09-17) — the glitch world beside the edit world

The "does not establish" list below named this arm; it was run on the same machinery. `basalis/`'s glitch
construction on the **same windows at the same `t_v`**: the flagged token is the token impossible under the
*clean* prefix whose model surprisal is nearest the edit's own violator (`glitch_s`), its twin the legal token
nearest it, caliper 0.30, token-balanced; the continuation is the untouched original stream, which belongs to a
legal token, where the edit world's is the swap's regrown subtree, which belongs to the violator. The clean-prefix
legal law was not on file for `swap65k` (built with `with_reference=False`) and was rebuilt with `stimuli.py`'s own
call, validated on `a1` where it is on file; at `t_v` the clean prefix leaves 5.17 of 16 tokens legal on average.
Within-pair guards are exact in the glitch world (max `|ΔV_pre|` = 0.0, max `|Δ log q_{t−1}|` = 0.0). **The
across-world guard did not hold**: the illegal tokens available under a clean prefix are systematically more
surprising than an edit's violator (mean surprisal 9.38 vs 6.79, mean `|Δs|` between the worlds 2.69 nats), and
the grammar gives no way to close that; the worlds are matched on window, position and `(k*, j)`, and each within
its pairs, but not to each other in surprisal.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes** (one grammar, one trunk, steps 0 / 8k / 64k, two venues, a frozen trunk and a linear critic, one
seed):

- The critic's pre-event level is a learned expectation over outcomes that calibrates to the world it was fed,
  in perfect rank order within each diet family on identical rows; a random-init trunk can only shift it, the
  trained trunk also rescales it.
- The anticipatory response to the identical event does not order with the world's expected cost: monotone
  inside each family, reversed between families, damage reading diet-independent. The outcome surprise orders
  with the world, as the norm's calibration restated.
- Within a world, after the regression-to-the-mean confound is cancelled by same-prefix twins, the response's
  dependence on the pre-event expectation is weak, confined to ℓ = 1–2, and present at ℓ = 1 in a random-init
  trunk.
- After an illegal token the critic is more optimistic than after its surprisal-matched legal twin, absent at
  random init and growing with training — and, by §5, the same on a glitch world where re-parsing is the wrong
  policy: the event reading is world-independent, and only its persistence belongs to the continuation.
- The value response is scoped to the absorbed levels and the scope extends with training.

**Does not establish:**

- Anything about a trunk shaped by value; the readout's expressivity bounds every row.
- The two worlds matched to each other in surprisal: a glitch under a clean prefix is ~2.7 nats more surprising
  than an edit's violator and the grammar gives no way to close that (§5); a selection rule that places the glitch
  where legal tokens live is a new pass.
- The twin trends across checkpoints as strict comparisons: the pair population is re-selected per checkpoint by
  the model's own surprisal and is `k*`-skewed by the caliper, phasic's own caveat.
- The `mix` family's reading apart from its edited-exposure confound, or the `out` family's at ℓ ≥ 4.
- Transfer off `v16 s2 L6 m4`, off this actor's goal, or to a value reader with a variance term (Xiang's insula
  row, the variance prediction error, has no analogue in a ridge).
