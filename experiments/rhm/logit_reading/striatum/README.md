§5. Everything the three earlier rounds measured is the *structural* prediction error — "the world did
not match my model". The biological picture has a second error, "the outcome was worse than I expected
for my goals", learned by neurons that read a broadcast copy of cortex and are trained by outcome; an
emotion sits at their junction. The model's only objective was next-token loss, so no goal was ever
thwarted and no valence could attach. Jasper's three decisions for this first cut (2026-09-16): the
goal is a **structural query task with a fixed actor** on the frozen trunk, not a horizon bet and not a
practice-arc learner; **frozen trunk**, a pure readout as `coeruleus/` did; **no commit/abstain choice**.
**Attribution**: the question, the constraint that consequence be independent of `k*` and of surprisal
by construction, the two pre-launch adjustments (both anchors first-class; a clean-only critic as the
exposure floor) and the horizon-excess correction in the addendum are the orchestrator's; the three
design decisions are Jasper's; the exact parse replay, the onset anchor, the per-level per-horizon
critic, the across-level contrast, the 2×2 from rare edits, the local re-matching and the
conditional / out-of-fold addendum are the implementer's (one Opus session).

## Goal

Four questions, on the same trunk, stimuli and observer family as the parent:

1. Does a violation of a learned norm cost anything at a goal that is not prediction, and does the
   cost inherit the model's altitude the way detection did (Part 2a)?
2. Does a readout trained on *outcomes alone* — no violation label, no consequence label — come to
   respond to violations, and if so, to their **structure** (was the answer changed; was the token
   illegal) or to their **cost** (did the goal fail)?
3. How much of that response is bought by exposure to damage, against a critic that has never seen an
   edit?
4. Does the repo's directional-not-scalar pattern have a value-side twin: does the state carry *which*
   query a violation compromised, beyond a scalar "how much"?

## Instruments

- **The actor** ([`task.py`](task.py)). A 16-way MLP head on the frozen trunk's final residual stream
  (`post_block7`, pinned across checkpoints; `post_embed` carried as the floor) that, at window index
  `t`, reports the feature of the **level-ℓ constituent containing `t`**, `ℓ = 1…6`. Trained on fresh
  clean windows only (`altitude.units.windows_with_parse`), then frozen. Its clean accuracy per `ℓ` is
  itself a reading of which levels the state supports.
- **The parse of the edited stream** ([`parse.py`](parse.py)). Replays `stimuli.make_edits`'s RNG with a
  latent-recording `realise`; the reconstruction is bit-identical to `stimuli_{a1,swap65k}.npz` on all
  eight stored arrays, and the recovered leaves reproduce both window sets exactly. Cached as
  `parse_<tag>.npz`: the level-ℓ answer at every window index for the original and the edited stream.
- **Two labels, kept apart.** *Consequence* `c[ℓ,a] = 1` iff the level-ℓ answer at the query differs
  between the original and edited generating trees — purely structural, verified to lie inside
  geometric containment (`0 ≤ t_q − e < 2^j`) with zero exceptions, and independent of `k*` and of the
  model's surprisal by construction. *Realised damage*: the actor was **right on the original window
  and wrong on the edited one** at the query. The first is what the world did; the second is what it
  cost.
- **Two anchors.** The **edit onset** (`first_diff`, the token is legal under `p_L` in 82.9% of swaps,
  so the structural readouts are blind there by construction; the unedited window is bit-identical up
  to the token before, max |Δstate| = 0.0 over 16,373 test windows) and **`t_v`** (the first
  exactly-impossible token, the parent's anchor). Query = anchor + `a`, `a ∈ {0, 1, 2, 4, 8}`.
- **The critic.** `V[ℓ,δ](s_t) ≈ E[o_ℓ(t+δ)]`, `δ = 0…12`, trained on realised 0/1 outcomes only:
  exact closed-form ridge from a streamed Gram matrix over every block and every penalty
  (`coeruleus/`'s recipe), an MLP beside it, a shuffled-outcome head beside every one. Two diets:
  **trained** (the stimulus windows' 60% train split — `a1` is 49% swap / 25% rare / 26% none, `swap65k`
  all swap) and **clean** (8192 fresh clean windows, never an edit; held-out fit comparable to the
  trained critic's, so a fair floor rather than a broken head). The **value revision** is
  `R[ℓ,a] = V[ℓ,a](s_t0) − V[ℓ,a+1](s_{t0−1})`, both terms predicting the same outcome.
- **Matching.** Exact strata on `(k*, j, anchor-position bucket)` plus 1:1 nearest-neighbour on the
  model's surprisal at the anchor, sign-balanced inside `|Δs|` bands (the parent's gotcha; a caliper
  alone left the surprisal guard at 0.375 and the position guard at 0.06–0.34). Every guard is
  reported; `k*` and `j` are pinned at exactly 0.500. Window-level train / val / test split
  0.60 / 0.15 / 0.25; every contrast is on test windows.
- **References on the identical rows**: the model's surprisal, `ΔH`, the single-position excess, the
  banked `coeruleus/` horizon-excess head, the model's *realised* horizon excess (addendum), an oracle
  illegality probe and an oracle **consequence** probe (the ceiling for "is consequence in the state").
- **The across-level contrast.** At a fixed `(window, anchor, a)` the state is identical across query
  levels, so comparing the revision at the levels the edit changed against the levels it did not is
  matched on everything a state-only readout can see; the structural references cannot move across `ℓ`.

## 1. The actor, and what an edit costs at the goal

Clean held-out accuracy (chance 0.0625). The trunk's climb is monotone at every level and runs out at
the root; the embedding floor is flat across training.

| ℓ | step 0 | 8k | 24k | 64k | ε-trained 64k | `post_embed` floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.517 | 0.893 | 0.901 | 0.904 | 0.905 | 0.394 |
| 2 | 0.282 | 0.776 | 0.802 | 0.815 | 0.816 | 0.219 |
| 3 | 0.149 | 0.623 | 0.677 | 0.705 | 0.707 | 0.145 |
| 4 | 0.103 | 0.399 | 0.491 | 0.524 | 0.530 | 0.134 |
| 5 | 0.086 | 0.218 | 0.281 | 0.325 | 0.321 | 0.130 |
| 6 | 0.063 | 0.091 | 0.116 | 0.141 | 0.135 | 0.062 |

**An edit costs at the goal, and the cost switches on with the absorbed levels.** Accuracy drop from
the unedited to the edited window at the onset (`a1`, `a = 0`):

| ℓ | swap, step 0 | 8k | 24k | 64k | rare, 64k | none, every checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.159 | 0.326 | 0.334 | 0.345 | 0.112 | 0.000 |
| 2 | 0.097 | 0.320 | 0.352 | 0.371 | 0.132 | 0.000 |
| 3 | 0.037 | 0.217 | 0.242 | 0.260 | 0.139 | 0.000 |
| 4 | −0.001 | 0.107 | 0.143 | 0.160 | 0.078 | 0.000 |
| 5 | −0.003 | 0.028 | 0.033 | 0.045 | 0.020 | 0.000 |

Level-4 damage goes from nothing to 0.16 as level 4 is absorbed; unedited windows read exactly zero.
This is the goal-side echo of the parent's Part 2a, with no separate mechanism. The critic fits: held-out
`R²` of `V[ℓ, 0]` at 64k is 0.218 / 0.249 / 0.191 / 0.135 / 0.063 / 0.010 for `ℓ = 1…6`, against a
shuffled-outcome floor of 0.001 and an embedding floor of 0.059.

**Consequence is confounded with structure and still varies inside every cell.** At the onset the
base rate of consequence for a level-1 query is 0 at `k* = 1`, 0.75 at `k* = 2`, 0.95 at `k* = 5`, and
0.43 / 0.71 / 0.71 / 0.77 for `j = 1…4`; the matching is what removes that. The rare edits hand over a
free 2×2: they are **legal** (no `t_v` at all) and consequential below their level with probability
0.90–0.96, and exactly inconsequential at it; swaps above their level are illegal and inconsequential.

## 2. At the violation, the revision reads what it cost, not what was changed

All rows re-matched on `(k*, j, position)` with sign-balanced surprisal pairs; `swap65k`, `a = 0`; the
model's own surprisal reads 0.500 on every row by construction.

**Realised damage** (right on the original, wrong on the edited window), AUC:

| readout at `t_v`, 64k | ℓ=1 | ℓ=2 | ℓ=3 | ℓ=4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| n matched | 2100 | 2776 | 2544 | 1756 |
| **critic revision `R`, linear** | **0.601** | **0.606** | **0.569** | **0.541** |
| critic revision, MLP | 0.677 | 0.657 | 0.585 | 0.540 |
| clean-only critic's revision | 0.583 | 0.543 | 0.529 | 0.527 |
| shuffled-outcome floor | 0.474 | 0.519 | 0.513 | 0.527 |
| **the critic's level `V`** | **0.694** | **0.691** | **0.656** | **0.609** |
| model surprisal (guard) | 0.500 | 0.500 | 0.500 | 0.500 |
| `ΔH` | 0.583 | 0.501 | 0.509 | 0.498 |
| banked `coeruleus/` excess head | 0.511 | 0.564 | 0.569 | 0.553 |
| oracle consequence probe | 0.515 | 0.524 | 0.482 | 0.462 |

The random-init trunk sits at its floor (0.51–0.54 across the same rows) and the effect appears between
step 0 and 8k (0.60 / 0.61 at `ℓ = 1, 2` by 8k). The ε-trained checkpoint reproduces it at `ℓ = 2–4`: `R` 0.60 /
0.56 / 0.55 against shuffles of 0.48–0.51 (at `ℓ = 1` it sits at its shuffle), `V` 0.60–0.68.

**Structural consequence** (the true answer changed), the same rows and readouts:

| readout at `t_v`, 64k | ℓ=1 | ℓ=2 | ℓ=3 | ℓ=4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| critic revision `R` | 0.525 | 0.545 | 0.517 | 0.562 |
| shuffled-outcome floor | 0.501 | 0.479 | 0.476 | 0.474 |
| the critic's level `V` | 0.511 | 0.505 | 0.445 | 0.384 |
| **oracle consequence probe** (same states) | **0.581** | **0.565** | **0.574** | **0.593** |

Consequence *is* in the state — the oracle probe finds it at every level — and the outcome-trained
readout does not point at it. And at **matched consequence the critic is indifferent to legality**: on
consequential episodes at the onset (`a1`, the only anchor where a legal rare edit exists), swap vs rare
reads `R` = 0.495 / 0.504 / 0.524 for `ℓ = 1…3` while the model's own surprisal on the same rows reads
0.574 / 0.532 / 0.550.

> **The outcome teacher finds the damage and not the structure. Matched on level, position and
> surprisal, the value revision reads whether the goal was missed at 0.54–0.61 and the critic's level at
> 0.61–0.69, while the structural label the same state carries at 0.57–0.59 is a null for both, and an
> illegal edit and a legal one with the same consequence are the same to it.**

## 3. How much exposure to damage buys

The clean-only critic, which has never seen an edit, reads most of the damage the trained one reads
(0.53–0.58 against 0.54–0.61 on the revision). What exposure adds to the *revision* is a few
hundredths. What it adds to the *level* is a different matter (§5).

## 4. The onset anchor, and the value trace

At the onset the consequence contrast is **not identifiable** once position is in the strata, by
construction: containment is `0 ≤ t_q − e < 2^j`, so `(j, t_q − e)` determines the label and the
matched cells collapse (n = 60–200). The contrast survives only at `t_v`, where the detection delay
decouples the flag from the edit's start. What the onset anchor does carry is the level and the
trace: `V` reads realised damage at 0.60–0.67 at every level there, the excess head is at chance
(0.49–0.52, consistent with a token that is legal in 83% of swaps), and in the unmatched trace
(`figs/striatum.png`, from the JSON) `V` at a level-3 query drops by about 0.57 across the onset on
consequential episodes against 0.17 on inconsequential ones, and barely moves at `t_v`. Read the trace
as suggestive only: it is unmatched, and a two-position sawtooth from averaging over constituent phase
sits on top of it.

## 5. Directionality does not transfer; the level subsumes the surprise readout

**The across-level contrast is a null, slightly backwards.** With the state held identical and only the
query level varied, the per-level-ranked revision is no larger at the levels the edit changed: win rate
0.507 (trained) / 0.518 (clean) at the onset and **0.424 / 0.426 at `t_v`** at 64k, with the same
picture at 8k and 24k and for both critics. The scalar level `V` carries far more than the per-`ℓ`
pattern of its revision. On a frozen trunk with a linear critic, the value-side twin of the repo's
directional-not-scalar pattern is negative.

**Addendum (2026-09-17): the right comparator, and conditional readouts.** The `excess` row above is
the *single-position* excess at the anchor, `−log q_{t0−1}(x_{t0}) − H(q_{t0−1})`. The banked
`coeruleus/` head predicts the *horizon* sum `Σ_{u=t}^{t+8}(NLL_u − H(q_u))`, a different object;
[`addendum.py`](addendum.py) adds its realised value. On the matched rows the single-position excess is
a null for damage (0.47–0.50) while the realised horizon excess reads it at 0.55–0.61 and the head's
prediction of it at 0.50–0.57: the head reads damage *through* its own target, and worse than the target
does. Conditional AUCs inside five quantile bins of a second score, identical rows, `swap65k` `t_v` 64k:

| | ℓ=1 | ℓ=2 | ℓ=3 | ℓ=4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `V`, marginal | 0.693 | 0.692 | 0.655 | 0.619 |
| `V` given the excess head | 0.688 | 0.686 | 0.648 | 0.602 |
| excess head, marginal | 0.504 | 0.563 | 0.557 | 0.567 |
| **excess head given `V`** | **0.463** | **0.490** | **0.481** | **0.499** |
| excess head given the clean-only critic's revision | 0.508 | 0.560 | 0.551 | 0.564 |
| `R` given the excess head / excess head given `R` | 0.617 / 0.475 | 0.592 / 0.523 | 0.551 / 0.530 | 0.517 / 0.555 |

Out of fold (5-fold), adding the excess head to `V` buys +0.003 / +0.001 / +0.002 / −0.003; adding `V`
to the head buys +0.05 to +0.23; adding the head to the **clean-only** critic buys +0.03 to +0.04.

> **Conditioned on the critic's value level, the surprise-shaped readout carries nothing; conditioned
> on the surprise readout, the value level is untouched. The clean-only critic does not subsume it, so
> the subsumption is what exposure to damage bought — small on the revision, complete on the level.**

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes** (one grammar, one architecture, the clean trajectory at four checkpoints plus the
ε-trained checkpoint, two stimulus venues, one goal):

- A goal makes a violation of a learned norm cost something, and the cost inherits the model's
  altitude: level-`ℓ` damage switches on as level `ℓ` is absorbed, unedited windows cost exactly nothing.
- A readout trained on outcomes alone, reading the same frozen state as the structural readouts, reads
  the violation's **cost** and not its **structure**: above floor on realised damage, a null on the
  structural consequence label the state demonstrably carries, and indifferent to legality at matched
  consequence.
- Most of that reading is generalisation from clean data; exposure to damage adds a few hundredths to
  the revision and makes the critic's level subsume the noradrenergic readout entirely.
- The state does not carry which query was compromised in a way the per-level revisions expose; the
  scalar level carries the most. Directionality does not transfer to the value side on this cut.
- A structural readout can read realised damage, but only through its own target: the model's
  realised future surprise tracks the goal's failure where the constituent the query depended on was
  destroyed. One object read twice, not two channels agreeing.

**Does not establish:**

- Anything about a trunk that has the goal. The frozen-trunk readout is the cortex-to-value-system
  broadcast copy; a trunk fine-tuned on the task, whose state is shaped by value, is the deferred arm.
- Anything about a choice. The outcome is right or wrong on a fixed actor; the value function never
  decides anything, so "the response changes what I do" is untested here.
- Whether a larger critic changes the directional result. The MLP beats the linear head by up to 0.08
  on the revision; the across-level contrast was run on the linear one.
- Whether the onset trace survives matching. It cannot be matched at this design, by construction.
- Transfer off `v16 s2 L6 m4`, off this architecture, or to a goal that is not a structural query.

## Gotchas worth not rediscovering

- **Keep the two labels apart.** "The answer changed" and "the actor got it wrong" are different
  objects and the readouts sort themselves by which one they track; a table that reports one as the
  other reads the opposite conclusion.
- **The matcher's strata have to contain the anchor position.** On `(k*, j)` alone, consequential
  rows at `t_v` are systematically earlier in the window (position guard 0.06–0.34), because
  consequence there means the violation was detected while still inside the edited span. A position
  bucket pins it; sign-balancing inside `|Δs|` bands pins the surprisal guard — the parent's gotcha,
  which a caliper alone does not fix.
- **At the edit onset the consequence contrast is unidentifiable once position is controlled.**
  `(j, t_q − e)` determines the label. Use `t_v`, where the detection delay decouples the two.
- **The single-position excess is not the horizon excess.** They behave differently on the damage
  label (0.47–0.50 vs 0.55–0.61); do not read one for the other, and compare a head against its own
  target.
- **Outcome arrays are `uint8`**; `o_orig − o_edit` underflows to 255. Cast before differencing.
- **`modal run --detach` returns before the run does.** Its client exits on disconnect; wait on
  `modal app list` or on the expected artefacts (`results/wait_app.sh`), not on the local launch log.
- **A sibling node writing its launch log breaks the image build.** Declare the app's image with an
  `ignore` that skips `results/` trees and `.log` files.
