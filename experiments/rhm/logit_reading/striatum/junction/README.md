§10.4 reads 4 and 5, the tentative read written after #120[^private]. The
parent found an outcome-trained critic indifferent to legality at matched consequence, on a design that
decorrelated cost from legality by construction. Read 5 proposed that a value channel's norm-sensitivity is
inherited from the world's correlation between violations and cost; read 4 proposed that the surprise channel
flags the events value learns from. Both are claims about the critic's *diet*, and the diet is a knob.
**Attribution**: the two questions are the orchestrator's; the three diet families, the width-free positive
control, the identifiability finding on the surprisal-matched contrast, the single-container design (one pair of
forward passes per cell, a diet as a mask over the Gram's rows) and the missing 8k excess column are the
implementer's (one Opus session); the reading agreed in discussion — detection intact, valuation indifferent,
and neither learned from the correlation — is Jasper's and the orchestrator's.

## Goal

1. **The junction.** Does the critic's sensitivity to legality at matched consequence track the training diet's
   correlation between violation and cost? Build diets from one window pool that span that correlation, hold the
   held-out rows fixed, and read the same matched contrast on every one, with the structural readouts beside it as
   the row the diet cannot move.
2. **The surprise-gated diet.** Train the critic only on the rows the model's own realised horizon excess flags,
   against matched-size random subsets, across a ladder of training-set sizes, and read the containment the
   parent found — the banked excess head carrying nothing once the critic's level is known — as a function of
   rows.

## Instruments

- **The cell is a function of the edit.** A swap of width `j` changes the true level-ℓ answer for every ℓ ≤ j
  and a rare edit for every ℓ < j, and neither changes anything outside its span (checked on `parse_a1.npz`:
  P(change) 0.92–1.00 inside the profile, 0.000 outside). So (legal / illegal, consequential / inconsequential)
  is determined by `(etype, j)`, a swap of width `c` and a rare edit of width `c + 1` are a consequence-matched
  pair, and a diet is a re-weighting over `(etype, j)`.
- **The diets** ([`diets.py`](diets.py)). Three families over the `a1` training windows, plus the banked `full`
  diet and a size-matched `natural_sized` anchor, plus the parent's clean-only critic (never saw an edit). `c`:
  depth-matched — P(illegal) = 0.5 and the consequence-depth marginal pinned identical across `aligned` /
  `decorrelated` / `reversed`. `x`: the extreme cells, where costly / costless is exact. `r`: cells cut on the
  critic's own teacher, realised damage. `assoc` = P(costly | illegal) − P(costly | legal) on realised damage in
  the training diet; `c gap` = E[consequence depth | illegal] − E[· | legal].

| diet | swap / rare / none windows | `c` given swap / rare | `c gap` | damage given swap / rare | `assoc` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `full` (banked) | 4839 / 2488 / 2503 | 1.99 / 1.50 | +0.49 | 0.110 / 0.052 | +0.40 |
| `aligned` | 1230 / 1230 / 844 | 2.50 / 0.50 | +2.00 | 0.132 / 0.021 | +0.77 |
| `decorrelated` | 1232 / 1232 / 844 | 1.50 / 1.50 | 0.00 | 0.112 / 0.052 | +0.41 |
| `reversed` | 1230 / 1230 / 844 | 0.50 / 2.50 | −2.00 | 0.090 / 0.083 | +0.05 |
| `aligned_x` | 615 / 615 / 844 | 4.00 / 0.00 | +4.00 | 0.108 / 0.011 | +0.60 |
| `reversed_x` | 615 / 615 / 844 | 0.00 / 3.00 | −3.00 | 0.069 / 0.102 | −0.28 |
| `aligned_r` | 556 / 556 / 844 | 2.26 / 1.14 | +1.12 | 0.148 / 0.029 | +1.00 |
| `reversed_r` | 556 / 556 / 844 | 1.56 / 2.60 | −1.04 | 0.049 / 0.126 | −1.00 |

  The grammar will not let both cost marginals be pinned at once (at a matched consequence depth a rare edit is
  twice as wide as a swap), which is why the families' total cost content runs *with* the association in one and
  *against* it in another.
- **One container per cell** ([`task.py`](task.py)). One pair of forward passes, one frozen clean-trained actor,
  one clean-only critic; every diet is a boolean mask (or weight) over the rows entering the streamed Gram, so the
  actor, the split and the held-out rows are bit-identical across diets by construction. The matcher sees the
  label and the guards, never the diet.
- **The contrasts.** Q1a, primary: swap vs rare among consequential episodes at the onset anchor, exact strata on
  `(j, anchor-position bucket)`, random 1:1 pairing, `j` and position pinned at 0.500, the model's surprisal left
  in as the input-level cue a state readout could use. Q1a′: the parent's unmatched recipe. Q1a″: with surprisal
  matched too — 12–140 rows at full scale, reported with its `n` as a footnote. The positive control: the
  unconditional swap-vs-rare reading of the critic's level over all edited rows, nothing matched. Ceiling: an
  oracle legality probe on the same anchor states. Beside every row: the model's surprisal, the banked
  `coeruleus/` head, the realised horizon excess.
- **Row diets for Q2.** `gate_top` (highest realised horizon excess), `gate_bot`, `rand`, `randpos`
  (position-matched random), `oracle` (highest realised damage), at 0.5% to 50% of the 1.73M training rows;
  containment = AUC of the banked excess head for realised damage inside five quantile bins of `V`. The 8k
  checkpoint carries no banked head, so containment is a 64k readout; its missing `_hexcess` column was built.
- **Reproduction gate.** The `full` diet refits the banked critic from scratch and reproduces the banked run:
  actor accuracy 0.904 / 0.815 / 0.705 / 0.524 / 0.325 / 0.141; `swap65k` `t_v` matched cells 2100 / 2776 /
  2544 / 1756; damage `R` 0.611 / 0.603 / 0.569 / 0.550 (banked 0.601 / 0.606 / 0.569 / 0.541), `V` 0.693 /
  0.693 / 0.655 / 0.619 (banked 0.694 / 0.691 / 0.656 / 0.609); the unmatched legality contrast on `a1` 0.495 /
  0.504 / 0.524 with surprisal 0.574 / 0.532 / 0.550, identical to the banked table.

## 2. The diet does reach the critic — through width, not legality

The positive control, unconditional swap vs rare on the critic's level `V` over all 3050 edited held-out rows,
nothing matched, ℓ = 1:

| diet | `aligned_x` (`c gap` +4) | `aligned` (+2) | `decorrelated` (0) | `reversed` (−2) | `reversed_x` (−3) | ρ vs `c gap` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AUC(−`V`, swap) | 0.450 | 0.452 | 0.460 | 0.470 | 0.472 | **−0.86** |
| AUC(−`V`, consequence depth ≥ 2) | 0.742 | 0.729 | 0.719 | 0.706 | 0.714 | **+0.65** |

Monotone, replicated at 8k (ρ = −0.89 / +0.73), and backwards with respect to legality: a diet that made the
illegal edits the costly ones makes the critic score swaps *less* badly. At a matched consequence depth a rare
edit spans twice the leaves a swap does, so what the critic learns from the diet is "wide edit → damage", and
width sides with `rare` in `aligned` and with `swap` in `reversed`. The effect vanishes the moment `j` is matched.

**Everything else is flat too.** Realised damage at the onset, matched on `(k*, j, position)` and surprisal with
every guard at 0.500: `V` 0.615–0.688 across all diets and levels, the clean-only critic level with the trained
ones (0.672 / 0.644 / 0.623 / 0.654 against `full`'s 0.684 / 0.653 / 0.623 / 0.669); the revision reads damage at
ℓ = 1 (0.67) and sits at or below chance at ℓ = 3–4 for every diet. The structural consequence label is a null for
every diet (matched n 60–200, the parent's onset unidentifiability) while the oracle consequence probe reads it at
0.51–0.60.

> **Across a training world whose violation–cost correlation runs from −1 to +1, the outcome reader's reading of
> whether an edit broke a rule, at matched cost, moves by at most 0.02 while an oracle probe on the same states
> has 0.05–0.11 of room, and the structural readouts read legality diet-independently. The diet reshapes the
> critic's reading of how much was damaged, not of whether a norm was broken.**

## 3. The surprise-gated diet is a worse curriculum, not a better one

`swap65k`, `t_v`, 64k; matched rows 2100 / 2776 / 2544 / 1756. Rows each arm needs before the banked excess head's
conditional AUC inside bins of `V` falls to 0.50:

| ℓ | head marginal | `gate_top` | `rand` | `randpos` | `gate_bot` | `oracle` (damage) | `full` (1.73M rows) | clean-only |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.504 (nothing to contain) | 8,651 | 8,651 | 8,651 | 8,651 | 8,651 | 0.463 | 0.518 |
| 2 | 0.563 | **432,531** | **8,651** | **8,651** | never | 432,531 | 0.492 | 0.553 |
| 3 | 0.557 | **432,531** | **8,651** | **8,651** | 25,952 | 432,531 | 0.481 | 0.530 |
| 4 | 0.567 | never | 69,205 | 432,531 | 69,205 | never | 0.499 | 0.516 |

At ℓ = 2 `gate_top` runs 0.543 → 0.538 → 0.530 → 0.524 → 0.472 → 0.481 across the ladder while `rand` is at 0.499
on its first rung; the position-matched control matches or beats plain random, so the gate's deficit is not a
position artefact. The damage reading tells the same story: `rand` and `randpos` are flat at 0.679–0.695 from
8,651 rows while `gate_top` starts at 0.643 and overtakes only near 432k (ending 0.703–0.721); the gated critic's
held-out R² is 0.13–0.23 against `rand`'s 0.23–0.25, and the oracle-damage gate is badly miscalibrated at small n
(R² −2.76) though its ranking is usable. The surprise-selected rows are where damage is densest (mean damage per
row 0.43 against 0.27 for random), so the gate does what it was built to do; the critic it trains is a poorer
reader of the state. The smaller `a1` `t_v` cell (n = 224–352) points the same way at ℓ = 2: `rand` 6,488 rows,
`randpos` 17,301, `gate_top` 43,252. The clean-only critic never contains the head at any size, reproducing the
parent's "exposure bought the containment".

> **A diet of the model's own most surprising moments reaches the containment about fifty times later than a
> random diet of the same size, and an oracle selecting on realised damage is no better. Surprise-gating the
> value learner's diet slows the subsumption it was proposed to cause.**

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes** (one grammar, one trunk, the 64k and 8k checkpoints, two venues, a frozen trunk and a linear
critic with the MLP critic tracking it throughout):

- The outcome reader's indifference to legality at matched consequence is not a property of the diet it was fed:
  it survives diets spanning the full range of violation–cost association, with a working positive control
  showing the diet does reshape the critic, monotonically, along the width axis.
- What the world's correlation buys the value reader is a sharper reading of cost; the structural readouts read
  legality on the same rows regardless of diet.
- Selecting the value reader's training rows by the model's own surprise, or by an oracle's damage label, reaches
  the containment of the surprise readout far later than an unselected diet of the same size, and fits the
  outcome worse throughout.

**Does not establish:**

- Anything about a trunk shaped by value. A frozen trunk and a linear readout bound what the *readout* can
  express, not what a value-shaped representation could carry about legality.
- The surprisal-matched legality contrast. It is not identifiable at scale on these stimuli (12–140 rows); the
  primary contrast leaves surprisal in as a cue, and the cue is one an outcome reader could have used and did
  not.
- Whether a curriculum built on *quiet* rows, or on surprise with the base rate of quiet rows preserved, changes
  the Q2 ordering; only top, bottom, random, position-matched random and oracle-damage selection were run.
- Transfer off `v16 s2 L6 m4`, off this actor's goal, or to a corruption model other than the parent's edits.

## Gotchas worth not rediscovering

- **The (legality, consequence) cell is `(etype, j)`.** A consequence-matched pair is a swap of width `c` against
  a rare edit of width `c + 1`, so any legality contrast at matched consequence is a width contrast unless `j` is
  in the strata; and the two cost marginals (consequence depth and damaged area) cannot both be pinned.
- **Realised damage is not structural consequence here.** An illegal single-leaf swap changes no true answer and
  still costs at the goal, because the state the actor reads is perturbed downstream. Selection on structure alone
  cannot push the realised violation–cost association below about zero; cut on the teacher itself to span it.
- **The surprisal-matched legality contrast is not identifiable at scale** (12–140 rows). Report the `(j,
  position)`-stratified contrast as primary, the unmatched one for comparability, and the surprisal-matched one
  with its `n`.
- **8k carries no banked `coeruleus` head**, so containment is a 64k-only readout; the `_hexcess` column for 8k
  had to be built before the surprise reference row existed there.
- **A sibling node's launch log inside the tree races Modal's source mount** on roughly half of all builds, the
  documented `ignore` notwithstanding. Keep launch logs in the scratchpad and let the launcher retry.
