# Files — logit_reading/altitude

**Up**: [README.md](README.md) · **Parent**: [../FILES.md](../FILES.md)

Every module imports the parent's `flat_oracle`, `calibration`, `violation`, `phasic`, `grammar`
and `stimuli` unchanged. Q1–Q3 read the parent's per-checkpoint `*_calibration.npz` arrays and need
no GPU.

## Code files

| file | purpose |
|---|---|
| `identity.py` | **Q1.** The `CE − H(q) = ⟨q − p, z⟩` identity checked pointwise per checkpoint; the temperature trace (gap vs `α`, its root `α*`, what rescaling does to ECE, `KL(p_L‖q)`, every `KL(p_k‖q)` and `best_k`); per-class gaps and `α*` by window position, token level and entropy decile; the `R²` second-moment decomposition. The continuous altitude: convex `p_κ` on a 0.05 grid, the rejected log-linear form, and the `(k, ε)` and `(κ, α)` two-parameter references. Modal `identity_sweep` (CPU). |
| `frontier.py` | **Q2.** A: boundary offset recovered from the model's per-position entropy (template detector against `E[H(p_k) | leaf level]`, and argmax-of-offset-profile), per candidate period `s^k`, against chance. B: `KL(p_L‖q)`, `KL(p_{k+1}‖p_k)` and every `H` by leaf level and window-position bucket. C: `phase_identifiability` — each observer's own phase posterior mod `s^k` (GPU, model-independent, once). `residual_sweep` (addendum): per-checkpoint `KL(p_L‖q)` and `KL(p_L‖p_κ*)` by leaf level, the share ratio and the L1 distance between share vectors. |
| `units.py` | **Q3.** Candidate next-level units as adjacent depth-`j` constituent pairs, enumerated with the oracle's parse and split into true / chance; the feature-level candidate likelihood closed at the token level (`P̂(f | f_A, parity)` from a disjoint corpus × exact upward pass); `Δ` with the model as null, with each exact observer as null, the model-free candidate gain and PMI, and `−log q(B)` alone (the second-child confound). Modal `units_sweep` (CPU). |
| `controls.py` | **Q4.** `tonic_ckpt`/`tonic_sweep`: the parent's 2b pairs rebuilt bit-for-bit with observer `k`'s running surprisal as the readout. `observer_cache_sweep`: ε-noise observer predictives on the `a1` stimuli. `entropy_direction_ckpt`/`_sweep`: the parent's same-prefix pairs against observer `k` with `noise_eps`, paired win rates by `k*`. `persistence_at_k`: 2e's unmatched output-level `dH` persistence with the ε-observers at every `k`. |
| `whither.py` | **Q4b addendum.** The same-prefix pairs rebuilt on CPU; `KL(q_next ‖ p_{k,next})` after the violation and after the twin for every observer in both families, with `defined_frac` for the clean family; argmin `k` by `k*`. `whither_ckpt`/`whither_sweep`. |
| `train_noisy.py` | **Q5 trajectory.** The parent's `train_trajectory` recipe with per-token uniform replacement at `eps_train` applied before the `(x, y)` split; `eps_train = 0` is bit-identical to the parent. 13 checkpoints to 64k, ~55 min on an L4. |
| `dual_ladder.py` | **Q5 readout.** Part 1 against both observer families (clean and ε, `k = 0…6`) on two window sets (clean, and the same windows corrupted at ε); `ladder_cache_sweep` computes each `(window set, family)` once, `dual_ckpt`/`dual_sweep` fit every checkpoint; reports the family-relative gap and `defined_frac`. |
| `analyze.py` | Local reduction over the JSONs pulled off the volume: every table in [`results/tables.md`](results/tables.md) and the figures in `figs/`. Reads only JSON — no Modal, no GPU. |
