**Prompt**: the conversation reading [`../altitude/`](../altitude/README.md): after an off-grammar token the

## Goal

The parent and [`altitude/`](../altitude/README.md) established what a next-token model's state
contains after a violation and what its output does instead. This round asks whether anything can be
built that reads the state and changes what happens next, in the model's own terms:

1. **Bound the prize.** After a corrupted token, how much loss does the model leave on the table
   against the best member of its own observer family, and how much of that could a single gain knob
   reach?
2. **A self-supervised readout.** Can a head on the frozen state learn, with no labels, to predict
   the model's own *excess* surprise over the next few positions — realised loss minus the
   uncertainty it stated — and does that head fire on the right things?
3. **Close the gain loop.** Let that head set the temperature at the position it reads. Does the
   post-violation entropy sign flip, how much of the prize comes back, and what does it cost where
   nothing happened?
4. **Close the plasticity loop.** Let the same head gate the per-position learning rate during
   continued training on the corrupted stream, from a checkpoint where the frontier is still live.

Venue: the ε = 0.01-corrupted stream the [`altitude/train_noisy.py`](../altitude/train_noisy.py)
trajectory was trained on, where hedging after a corrupted token is the Bayes-optimal response and
the ε-noise observer at the model's own fitted `κ*` is the ceiling. Two models: the ε-trained one on
its own distribution, and the clean-trained one dropped onto the corrupted stream — a world it never
saw. Two event types: natural events (the known corrupted positions in held-out corrupted windows)
and controlled ones (the parent's `swap65k` same-prefix pairs).

## Instruments

- **Events and the ceiling** ([`events.py`](events.py)). The corrupted held-out windows are the ones
  [`altitude/dual_ladder.py`](../altitude/dual_ladder.py) cached (eval seed 4242); the corruption
  mask is reconstructed from that module's RNG and asserted bit-identical to the cache. 1577 isolated
  events (no second corruption within ±H, H = 8); the un-isolated 1825 give the same numbers. Excess
  at offset τ is `CE(p_L^ε, q) − CE(p_L^ε, p_κ*^ε)`, the model's loss against the best member of its
  family, so "it is coarse" is already subtracted.
- **One-knob consumers.** A temperature `q_α = softmax(αz)` and a uniform noise floor
  `q_w = (1−w)q + w/v`, both convex in their knob and optimised exactly, pooled or per row.
- **The excess-surprise target** ([`readout.py`](readout.py)). `y_t = Σ_{u=t}^{t+8} (NLL_u − H(q_u))`
  over the predictions made at `u`. By the `altitude/` Q1 identity this is zero-mean once the model
  is trained, so the head is asked "am I about to be surprised beyond my stated uncertainty" rather
  than "is this a hard position": at the frontier the entropy is high and the loss matches it; after
  a fallback the entropy is low and the loss does not. The realised target has sd 2.6 nats while the
  predictable part moves ~0.05 nats, so the primary head is the exact minimum-MSE linear map solved in
  closed form from a streamed Gram matrix (every block, every ridge penalty), with an MLP beside it.
  An Adam-fit linear probe gave negative held-out R² where the closed-form solve gives positive.
  Floors carried from the parent: a random-init trunk and the input embedding. Contrasts: the same
  heads on the raw future NLL, and the model's own entropy.
- **The gain loop** ([`gainloop.py`](gainloop.py)). Every arm is a per-position gate and a monotone
  map `α = f(gate)`, an 18-bin quantile step function refined to 0.1% in both tails (a corrupted token
  is in context at ~1.5% of positions), each bin's `α` the exact minimiser of the model's own realised
  NLL on held-out corrupted windows at `t ≥ 8` — no oracle in the fit. Gates: constant, the Q2 head,
  a probe trained *with* the corruption label, and the exact offset since the last corrupted token.
- **The plasticity loop** ([`plasticity.py`](plasticity.py)). Continued training on the corrupted
  stream from ε-trained step 8000 (`κ* = 3.60`), 16k steps, five arms in parallel. Every gated arm
  uses rank-normalised weights, so the weight multiset is bit-identical and only the assignment
  moves — [`endogenous_teacher`](../../endogenous_teacher/README.md)'s control. `head_shuf` is the
  same multiset permuted; `oracle_down` / `oracle_up` weight by the exact "a corrupted token lies in
  `[t−8, t]`" indicator in each direction, bounding what a perfectly accurate gate could do. The gate
  reads the live model's state through the frozen head; its drift is logged.

> **On the distribution the model was trained on, the prize after a corrupted token is real (8.6×
> the quiet background at τ = 0, decaying over ~8 tokens) and a temperature reaches essentially none
> of it — 0.4%, and a third even with an oracle choosing per prediction. The correction that does
> reach it is a replacement by a *finer* observer, not a rescaling of the coarser one the output fell
> to. On a world the model never saw the prize is 22× larger and a gain knob reaches half, of which
> half again is one global softening.**

## Q2 — the self-supervised readout

| readout | ε-trained 64k | ε-trained 8k | clean-trained 64k on noise |
|---|---|---|---|
| excess head, held-out R² (random-trunk floor) | +6e−5 (−4e−5) | +4e−4 (+3e−4) | **+3.5e−3** (+1.4e−4) |
| excess head, AUC(corrupted position vs quiet) | 0.462 | 0.583 | **0.832** (MLP 0.846) |
| — input-embedding / random-trunk floors | 0.516 / 0.492 | 0.541 / 0.508 | 0.592 / 0.580 |
| — the model's own summed `H(q)`, unfitted | 0.668 | 0.691 | 0.563 |
| raw-future-NLL head, AUC(event vs quiet) | 0.550 | 0.558 | 0.595 |
| **calibrated-hard vs overconfident-wrong**, excess head | 0.499 | 0.545 | **0.724** |
| — raw-NLL head / summed `H(q)` on the same contrast | 0.048 / 0.000 | 0.049 / 0.000 | 0.071 / 0.000 |
| `swap65k` same-prefix pairs, excess head (paired) | 0.512 | 0.476 | 0.449 |
| — oracle-label probe on the *same* states (linear / MLP) | 0.717 / **0.885** | 0.665 / 0.786 | 0.706 / **0.862** |
| — `H(q)` after the token (the parent's 2e) | 0.440 | 0.420 | 0.433 |

(3.67M unlabelled training positions per venue; the pairs reproduce the parent — 1570 held-out at
ε 64k, prefix-identity check 4e−06, and the oracle MLP's pooled 0.904 is `altitude/` Q5b's 0.904.)

Three facts, separately:

- **On its own distribution the excess target is a null.** The head does not beat its random-trunk
  floor, and the model's own entropy, a scalar it already emits, separates events from quiet better
  than any head does. The per-event consequence is ~0.08 nats against a target sd of 2.6.
- **On a world it never saw the head works, and in the way the excess form was chosen for**: 0.83
  detection against a 0.58 floor, and 0.72 on overconfident-wrong vs calibrated-hard where a head on
  the raw future loss reads 0.07 — the two targets point opposite ways on that contrast.
- **No self-supervised readout finds the `swap65k` violations in any venue** (0.45–0.51), while an
  oracle-label probe on the very same states reads 0.86–0.89. The information is in the state; "am I
  about to be surprised beyond my stated uncertainty" is a different direction in it than "was that
  token illegal".

> **An outcome-trained readout of "my model is failing" is learnable exactly when the model is
> failing a lot — under change — and not on a distribution whose consequences it has already
> absorbed.**

## Q3 — the gain loop

Δ realised NLL is on held-out corrupted windows; negative is an improvement.

| arm | α at τ=0 | prize recovered vs ceiling | Δ NLL, event window | Δ NLL, quiet | Δ NLL, whole stream | `H(q)` after event |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ε-trained 64k** (base `H` 1.577) | | | | | | |
| const | 1.004 | −0.001 | +0.0008 | −0.0003 | −0.00001 | 1.574 |
| head (ridge / MLP, bin or log-linear) | 1.003–1.006 | −0.002…0.000 | +0.0008…+0.0010 | −0.0004…−0.0005 | −0.00005…−0.00008 | 1.573 |
| oracle_probe (AUC 0.814) | 0.998 | +0.000 | +0.0005 | −0.0002 | −0.00002 | 1.579 |
| oracle_offset (exact binary gate) | **0.342** | −1.105 | **−0.0808** | −0.0020 | **−0.0071** | 2.539 |
| **clean-trained 64k on noise** (base `H` 1.177) | | | | | | |
| const | 0.764 | +0.248 | −0.241 | +0.019 | −0.017 | 1.320 |
| head_ridge | 0.593 | +0.431 | −0.374 | +0.015 | −0.030 | 1.531 |
| head_mlp | 0.568 | **+0.486** | −0.406 | +0.013 | **−0.032** | 1.659 |
| oracle_probe (AUC 0.873) | 0.502 | +0.427 | −0.370 | +0.016 | −0.026 | 1.724 |
| oracle_offset | 0.068 | +0.406 | −0.618 | +0.006 | −0.051 | 2.744 |

**The loop does nothing on the ε-trained model and a great deal on the clean-trained one.** On the
unseen world the self-supervised head beats the constant temperature (+0.25) and the supervised
corruption probe (+0.43), recovers 48.6% of a 0.58-nat prize, and improves the whole corrupted stream
by 0.032 nats per token at a cost of +0.013 nats at quiet positions. On the trained world every head
arm's fitted map sits at α ≈ 1.00 in all 18 bins.

**The `oracle_offset` row is the one to read carefully.** Its two loss columns disagree in sign on
the ε-trained model, and the reason is not noise: the post-event population is selected on a latent
— whether the token at `t` was corrupted — that no observer of the window can see, so the conditional
law of the realised token there is not `p_L^ε`. Against the ceiling a window-observer could reach, the
prize column is honest; on realised tokens given side information about the latent, the NLL column
is. A gate that is *told* a corrupted token just arrived softens to α = 0.34 and is worth 0.081 nats
in the event window and 0.007 nats per token on the stream. Nothing readable from the window captures
any of it — not the state head, and not a 0.814-AUC supervised probe of the very same fact, whose
18-bin map stays within 0.98–1.03 including its top 0.1% bin. At a ~1.5% base rate a detector that
good still fires mostly on quiet positions, and softening those costs more than the hits return.
Window-position and isolation confounds were checked (fit restricted to `t ≥ 8`, bins refined; `α(τ=0)`
stayed at 0.342 and the isolated / un-isolated recoveries agree to three decimals).

**The entropy sign does not flip in either venue.** Paired win rate that the forecast after the
illegal token is *less* confident than after its matched legal twin: ε-trained 0.440 → 0.433–0.441
across every arm; clean-trained 0.433 → 0.378–0.401, i.e. *further* from 0.5, because the head fires
slightly harder on the twin than on the violator (α viol / twin 0.66 / 0.63).

> **A gain loop is a "which world am I in" organ. On a world the model was trained for it has nothing
> to do that the fixed policy does not already do as well as any window-observer can; on a world it
> was not, a label-free head plus one scalar knob recovers half the loss.**

## Q4 — the plasticity loop

Continued training on the corrupted stream from ε-trained step 8000 to 24 000, five arms
(584–613 s each), identical weight multisets except `uniform`:

| arm | `κ*` (ε, corrupted) | CE corrupted | CE clean | `KL(p_L‖q)` clean | post-event excess vs ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| uniform | 4.50 | **1.3077** | 1.2396 | 0.0502 | 0.0801 |
| head_down | 4.45 | 1.3136 | 1.2448 | 0.0554 | 0.0929 |
| head_shuf | 4.45 | 1.3130 | 1.2444 | 0.0551 | 0.0885 |
| oracle_down | 4.45 | 1.3181 | **1.2393** | **0.0500** | 0.1478 |
| oracle_up | 4.45 | 1.3146 | 1.2497 | 0.0604 | **0.0799** |

Holding the multiset fixed, the assignment matters and the two oracle arms straddle the random one in
opposite directions. Clean `KL(p_L‖q)` runs 0.0500 (oracle_down) < 0.0551 (shuf) < 0.0554 (head_down)
< 0.0604 (oracle_up); post-event excess runs the other way, 0.0799 (oracle_up) < 0.0885 (shuf) <
0.0929 (head_down) < 0.1478 (oracle_down). **Down-weighting the positions after a corruption buys
clean-grammar accuracy — enough for a high-variance gated arm to match `uniform` — and pays for it in
post-event behaviour; up-weighting them does the reverse.** Per-leaf-level CE has the same ordering,
largest at level 0 (0.509 oracle_down vs 0.524 oracle_up).

**`head_down` is indistinguishable from `head_shuf` on every readout** (1.3136 vs 1.3130, 1.2448 vs
1.2444, 0.0554 vs 0.0551): the `endogenous_teacher` null replicating exactly, and here attributable,
because the oracle arms with the same multiset do separate. The head's correlation with the realised
horizon excess during training ran +0.03 → −0.04, the same low-signal fact Q2 measured on this
distribution. All arms landed at `best_k = 4`, `κ*` 4.45–4.50: nothing changed which rung the model
sat on, only how well.

> **The plasticity intervention has teeth and a tradeoff. A perfectly informed gate trades post-event
> behaviour for clean-grammar accuracy in whichever direction it is pointed; the label-free readout
> cannot use it on this distribution because it carries nothing here.**

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes** (one grammar, one architecture, the ε-trained and clean-trained trajectories, one
noise dose, single seed per treatment):

- After a corrupted token the ε-trained model leaves a real, decaying excess over its own family
  ceiling that a temperature cannot reach and a replacement by a finer observer can. Where the nats
  live is in restoring the finer reading the state still holds, not in softening the coarser one the
  output fell to.
- A head trained on the model's own excess surprise, with no labels, detects the model's failures
  and separates overconfident-wrong from calibrated-hard positions on a world the model was not
  trained for, and is a null on the world it was.
- Closing the gain loop with that head recovers about half the prize on the unseen world, beats a
  supervised corruption detector and a global recalibration, and does nothing on the trained world,
  where only side information no window-observer has licenses softening.
- Gating plasticity by "what surprised me" is an intervention with measurable consequences in both
  directions when the gate is exact, and the label-free head is not exact enough here to move it.
- **The reading agreed in discussion.** The loop's value is conditional on the world differing from
  the one the model was trained on. That is the relevant case: production data always differs from
  training data to some degree, and a biological learner never stops seeing new data and having to
  generalise from it. The organ is not for the world you already know.
- **A reinterpretation of the parent's Part 2e, supported but not tested by an arm.** The `swap`
  stimuli regrow the edited subtree legally under the new feature, so after the violating token the
  continuation is what the model's one-rung-down reading predicts; the model is genuinely less
  surprised after the violator than after a rare-but-legal twin, which is why the head fires harder
  on the twin. The ε-observer's world contains only single-token glitches, so it keeps expecting the
  old structure and keeps being surprised — the four-token persistence the parent measured. The
  model's policy (reinterpret) is right after a structural edit; the observer's (hold and discount)
  is right after a glitch; each is wrong in the other world, and neither is a loop.

**Does not establish:**

- The other consumer. "Hold the fine reading and discount this token" — the precision-weighting seat
  — was bounded (Q1's mixing table) and not built. The cheap form is a head that outputs a mixing
  weight between the model's forecast and its forecast with the suspicious token masked, a second
  forward pass; the excess head is its natural gate, and the swap stimuli against natural corruptions
  are the two worlds it would have to tell apart.
- Whether a higher noise dose makes the trained-world readout learnable or the fallback give way to
  widening; ε = 0.01 is the regime `altitude/` already flagged as having little gradient pressure
  either way.
- Whether the plasticity tradeoff survives a readout good enough to use it, or a seed. The Q4 spread
  (0.0104 nats on clean KL between the oracle arms) is the smallest effect leaned on; the four gated
  arms share a seed, data stream and noise stream, so the comparison is within-run.
- Anything off `v16 s2 L6 m4`, off this architecture, or on natural language.

## Gotchas worth not rediscovering

- **Fit a noisy-target linear head in closed form.** With a target sd of 2.6 nats and a predictable
  part of ~0.05, SGD on a linear probe gave negative held-out R² where the exact Gram solve gives
  positive. Any "the state does not carry X" claim from a fitted head needs the closed-form check.
- **Two loss columns, both honest, can disagree in sign.** A population selected on a latent no
  window-observer sees (was this token corrupted) has a conditional law that is not the observer
  family's predictive. Report the prize against the reachable ceiling *and* the realised NLL, and say
  which is which; do not reconcile them.
- **Detector AUC is not enough at a low base rate.** A 0.81-AUC probe of "a corruption just happened"
  cannot license softening when the event rate is ~1.5%, because its top bins are still mostly quiet
  positions. The exact gate pays; the good detector does not.
- **Keep the weight multiset fixed and the oracle arms in.** Rank-normalised weights make every gated
  arm's multiset identical; the shuffled arm says whether the assignment did anything, and the two
  oracle arms say whether the intervention *can* do anything, which is what makes a head-equals-
  shuffle null attributable rather than ambiguous.
- **The cached corrupted windows do not store their mask.** Reconstruct it from `dual_ladder`'s RNG
  and assert bit-identity against the cache before trusting an event list; 1/v of the flips redraw
  the same token and are not events.
- **The `swap` stimuli's continuation is legal under the swapped feature.** A readout that fires on
  future surprise will fire *less* after the violator than after a rare legal twin, and that is
  correct, not a failure; the parent's entropy-direction sign should be read with this in mind.
