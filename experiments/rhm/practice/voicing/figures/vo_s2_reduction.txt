====================================================================================================
[M] THE CORRIDOR UNDER A MOVED WRITE — misfire with the treatment's own draws removed
====================================================================================================
    `n_misfire` counts FIRED rows (the head executed) whose span did not match
    `dp_features` exactly. Where the treatment RE-DECIDED the write, a mismatch with the
    DP's row IS the treatment and not a defect — so the raw rate is not the corridor's
    health on a treated arm.

    WHAT CANNOT BE DECOMPOSED FROM THIS LOG, said rather than fudged. `n_sampled` /
    `n_xp_shift` count every re-decided call, OPEN and CLOSED alike; `n_fired` counts
    only calls the head executed. The recorder does not tally the shift separately for
    the two paths, so on an arm whose corridor is shut most re-decisions happen on
    closed slots — where there is no head and so no misfire to explain — and
    `shift - misfire` is not a subtraction that means anything. `excess` is therefore
    printed ONLY where `shift <= n_misfire`, i.e. where the shift could in principle
    account for the misfire; elsewhere it reads `n/a`. Splitting the shift by path is a
    one-line recorder change and belongs to the next round, not to a reducer.
