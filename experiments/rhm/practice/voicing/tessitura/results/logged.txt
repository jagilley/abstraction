[B] THE RING BUFFER'S OWN LAG — what the critic is shown, against what the world just did
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    The audit's `base_rate` is the mean verdict over the held-out slice of a slot's ring
    buffer (cap 8192 rows per slot, the last 1024 held-out rows scored). It is therefore
    a TRAILING estimate of the world. Fitting an exponential moving average of the
    cycle's own rate to it gives the diet's effective window in cycles. ANY measurement
    of the judge's own lag has to be read against this floor: a judge that tracked its
    buffer instantly would still lag the world by this much.

    TWO CAVEATS ON THE PROBE COLUMN, said before it is read. (i) The probe solve rate is
    nearly flat over a run, so its EMA fit is ill-conditioned and the tau it returns is
    not interpretable as a window; the filed column is the one to read. (ii) On the
    `sotto_voce` arms the probe buffer's `base_rate` is the MIRROR's mean predicted
    PROBABILITY, filed as a soft label (`sotto_voce.py` ~6168: `ym = pm.clone()`), not a
    verdict rate — a different object, and not comparable across the grader column. The
    WORLD's verdict on those same probes is the [W] table's probe row, which is computed
    on every probe as an instrument no arm consumes.
