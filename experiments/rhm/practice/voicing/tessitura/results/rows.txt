--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE DERIVATION IS A CHOICE AND IS REPORTED AS THREE. The judge is Q-shaped,
  `critic(context, candidate) -> P(solve)`; a state value has to be built from it.
    V_w    the judge's probability at the candidate that WAS written — the expectation
           at the write it will make, and the only one of the three the run itself ever
           consults on a governed slot;
    V_max  its max over the slot's realised candidate set — the value of the slot under
           a judge-optimal write;
    V_mean its mean over that set — the value of the slot under a blind write.
  `base` is the mean verdict over the SAME rows. On filed rows V_w is the judge's
  expectation of what it chose; on probe rows it is its expectation of a substitution
  drawn off-policy, so the two are different objects and never pooled.

  a DIFFERENT on-table class (gate V-4d), and a verdict on the SAME trajectory's final

  disagree with each other, which is the pairing's own noise and is printed, not hidden.
  The filed buffer is shorter than the probe buffer in cycles, so only the overlap pairs.
