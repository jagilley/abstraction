[X] THE TWO SEEDS, AS SIGNS — ts_s0, ts_s2
========================================================================================================
  Each of §8's headlines restated as a sign. Magnitudes are printed so the reader can see
  how far apart they are, but the CLAIM is the sign, and a magnitude that moves between
  seeds is reported as moving rather than averaged away.

  AN EXCLUSION CRITERION, STATED BEFORE THE TABLES AND APPLIED MECHANICALLY. A critic
  begins governing only once a slot holds `vo_critic_min = 256` filed rows, so an era
  early enough can contain little but the readout's own INITIALISATION. `build_critic`
  zeroes every bias and draws the weights at N(0, 0.02), so an untrained critic emits a
  ~0 logit and reads 0.500 at EVERY candidate — which makes the signature exact and
  checkable rather than a matter of judgement: a cell is flagged `init` iff
  |V_w - 0.5| < 0.01 AND V_max - V_tab < 0.005, i.e. the judge cannot tell its
  candidates apart at all. The separator is not marginal: at seed 2's era 1 that gap is
  0.000292 on 671 rows, and at its era 2 it is 0.106531 on 309,334. Flagged cells are
  printed with a `*` and the sign patterns are given BOTH WAYS, with and without them.
  Nothing is dropped.
