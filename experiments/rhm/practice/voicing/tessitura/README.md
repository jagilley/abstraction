The three questions [`logit_reading/striatum/norm/`](../../../logit_reading/striatum/norm/README.md) asked of a
critic on a frozen trunk, asked of the judge `voicing` trained on the practice learner's own graded attempts:
does its norm calibrate to the world it was fed and on what clock, does it read cost or structure at the write,
and how does it price a substituted class against the written one at an identical context. A zero-cost pass over
every banked arm with a critic, a CPU pass over the two arms that banked their heads, one paid yoked arm per seed
with three default-off knobs (`# [tessitura]`-marked in `../voicing.py`, G-F 0.000e+00, the dumps byte-identical
to the banked arm's), and a CPU refit of the critic under outcome diets on the fixed final trunk. This folder
carries the code and the facts; the interpretation is the super-node's, alongside its siblings there, because
this is that round's instrument at work on the practice learner, as [`../../perception/`](../../perception/README.md)
was the frontier's.
