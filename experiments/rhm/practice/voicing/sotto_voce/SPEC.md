## The background, so you hold the same picture I do

Voicing built a critic: a small judge that reads a situation and a candidate spelling and predicts the grader's verdict, trained on the learner's own filed writes, gradient confined. Its clean result was that a judge fed only what the learner chose to write ranks alternatives at chance (held-out AUC 0.49 on counterfactual rows vs 0.65 on filed rows). The learner writes one class per slot, so its experience carries no information about the untaken class. Voicing's fix is the probe channel (`vo_run_probes`, ~line 5618): for a filed context, substitute a different on-table class into the trajectory's final configuration, render it, pay the world's grader for one verdict, and file the row into the critic's buffer (`pbuf`) and nowhere else. Priced and off-stream, both gated (V-4B, V-5, V-4d). That lifted counterfactual AUC to 0.80 at about half a percent of priced time, and the composed chooser's L2/L3 class accuracy replicated across three seeds.

The conceptual point this node is built on. The judge cannot be identified off the policy's support. There are exactly three ways to get labels for the roads not taken: vary the behavior (Q1 showed 9% write variation at L2 costs the L3 commit window), pay the world (the probe channel, cheap on RHM, a real trial elsewhere), or use a model of outcomes trained on experience and applied to sentences the learner never wrote. This last is the forward model's seat in the practice arc, and the question is whether it works and where it fails. RHM is where to measure it because the world's verdict is exact and cheap, so the world can grade every model-graded probe in the background as an instrument, never consumed, and we can see exactly where the model was wrong. The neuroscience note behind this is `reading/emotion_in_cortex.md` §4, the compilation problem: how a value readout gets trained on situations for which no real outcome ever arrived. The committee half comes from `experiments/mjc/committee_head/README.md` (disagreement is the best reward-free reader of "is this learnable," 0.716) and `experiments/mjc/ballistic/directed/README.md` (disagreement finds where you lack data, not where data went stale). A counterfactual is by construction where you lack data. And the hazard on record is `experiments/mjc/practice/span/README.md`: a single model's belief about its own error off the practiced corridor was uncorrelated with its true error. Also read `ideas/heterogeneous_graders.md` §8 on the mirror grader, since an outcome model trained on the learner's own experience is the same grader in a mirror, and this node measures how far a mirror extends before it goes blind.

## The arms

All yoked to the anchor's clock (Y-1), seeds 0 and 2 (the draws whose ladders reached L4; seed 0 reached L5). The floor and ceiling are banked and not re-run: `vo_s3b:voi3b_comp_yk` (composed chooser, filed diet) and `vo_s3b:voi3b_comp_pr_yk` (composed chooser, world-graded probes) at seed 0, and their seed-2 twins in `vo_s3e`. Three new arms, all composed chooser, all with the probe channel on:

1. **model-graded.** Same substitutions, but the verdict filed into `pbuf` comes from an outcome model: a separate small classifier from the rendered final configuration (the `x2` tensor at the grade call) to P(solved), trained on the learner's own experienced sentences and their world verdicts (the filed rows' `fin` and `y`; you will need to persist `fin` per filed row, the parts in `rows` already carry it). The world is not consulted for filing. The world's verdict is still computed for every probe and logged as an instrument only.
2. **committee-graded.** As 1 with a K-member committee of outcome models (K around 4 to 5 as in `committee_head`; differ by seed and bootstrap of the training rows). File a probe only where the members agree; size the agreement threshold offline from the committee's spread on held-out filed rows and report the fraction filed. Log the mean and the disagreement per probe.
3. **hybrid.** As 2, but where the committee disagrees, pay the world for the verdict and file that. The world-graded disagreeing probes also train the outcome models. The bill is the world-graded fraction only.

Two things to keep straight. The outcome model must be a separate object trained on outcomes, not the surface model's NLL or the DP's score, because the composed chooser already uses the surface model as its prior and a critic trained on the prior's opinion would just be learning the prior. Report the surface model's NLL as a comparison row in the reduction if it is cheap, but it is not an arm. And in arms 1 and 2 the outcome models train on experience only, never on any probe verdict from the world; the leak would make the arm a slower version of the ceiling.

## Gates

Every existing gate must still hold on the new arms: G-F at 0.000e+00 with every new knob off (a shared path is touched, so this is mandatory), V-1, V-4A/B in the form that fits, V-5 off-stream, V-6/V-6b that the model-graded diet is live and changes the run, Y-1 with 0 cancelled, V-4d on the probe path. New gates, each shown to fail on a deliberate perturbation before it is reported, per the arc's rule:

- **M-1.** On a model-graded arm the world's verdict exists for every probe, enters no loss, no buffer the critic reads, and no bill. Perturbation: wire it into `pbuf`.
- **M-2.** The outcome models train only on their stated sources (experience; plus world-graded disagreeing probes in hybrid only). Perturbation: feed them a model-graded probe row.
- **M-3.** The bill equals exactly (world-graded probe count) × `d_fb`: zero on arms 1 and 2, the disagreeing fraction on 3.
- **M-4.** The committee's agreement rule is what files: a probe with disagreement above threshold is not in `pbuf` on arm 2 and is world-graded on arm 3. Perturbation: flip the threshold.

Add them to `gates/falsify.py` and `vo_gates_cpu` in the same style.

## Readouts

Per arm per seed, beside the banked floor and ceiling, in the reduction: the critic's held-out AUC on the probe diet scored against the world's verdict (for model-graded arms this must be the instrument verdict, not the filed one), and on the filed diet; the composed chooser's class accuracy against the repair set at L2/L3 (the replicated cell) and at L4/L5 where the seed has them; the era-3 and era-5 error gaps; the probe bill as a share of priced time. Plus the readouts that are new here: the outcome model's accuracy against the world on probes and on held-out filed rows; whether committee disagreement predicts outcome-model error on probes (AUC of disagreement as a detector of wrongness); the fraction of probes each committee arm filed; the hybrid's world-query fraction; and where the outcome model and the world disagree, broken down by level and by whether the substituted class was in the book's frequently-written set, so the mirror's blind region is located rather than summarized.
