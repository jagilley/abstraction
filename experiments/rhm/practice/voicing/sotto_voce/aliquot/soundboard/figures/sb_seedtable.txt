  Every arm is the COMPOSED chooser with the probe channel on, clock-yoked to its seed's banked
  anchor. The FLOOR is the composed chooser on the FILED diet, the CEILING the same chooser on
  WORLD-graded probes, and the MIRROR / committee / hybrid are `sotto_voce`'s three arms. All of
  those are banked and NOT re-run, and so are `aliquot`'s two PROJECTION arms — a ridge-logistic
  readout of `SN.trunk`'s pooled hiddens over the substituted configuration, per root, over the
  LIVE trunk and over a NEVER-TRAINED one. THE THREE NEW ARMS are that same readout in the same
  seat with ONE thing added: an outcome head over the plant's pooled hiddens whose gradient
  reaches the TRUNK in the same optimizer step as the plant's own infill training — on the
  world's VERDICT, on NEXT-LEVEL YIELD from the learner's own miner, and on the verdict with the
  plant's own infill term OFF. THE SEEDS ARE NOT AVERAGED.
