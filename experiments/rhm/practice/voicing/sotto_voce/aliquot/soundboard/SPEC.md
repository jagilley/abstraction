## Where this sits

Read, in this order: `experiments/rhm/practice/voicing/sotto_voce/aliquot/DESIGN.md` (§1–§3, §7) and `figures/al_seedtable.txt`; then `aliquot/duplex/DESIGN.md` (all of it, §2.5–§2.6 and §7 especially) and `duplex/figures/du1_reduction.txt` sections (a), (c) and `du2_reduction.txt` section (f); then `sotto_voce/README.md` and `voicing/README.md` for the stack; and `ideas/calibration_and_violation_are_one_object.md` §12 for the motivation.

The short version. Aliquot put a linear readout of the practice plant's state in the grader's seat for the probe channel, in place of sotto_voce's mirror, and found the plant's pooled hiddens do not linearly carry the world's verdict: the live trunk read at 0.73–0.76 held-out on experience against 0.72–0.74 for a never-trained trunk, where the mirror reads 0.99. The reading is that a plant trained only by masked infilling represents what prediction needs, so the outcome error has to reach the trunk's weights. Duplex tested that offline on overtone's banked dumps: fine-tuning the plant on an outcome head with the infill loss kept on raised the linear read on the duplicate-free filed column from 0.63 to 0.73 and 0.78 on the two seeds and on seed 0's probe rows from 0.80 to 0.83, at +0.008 nats of held-out infill loss against a matched infill-only control and a 0.05–0.07 cost in block-parse accuracy; fine-tuning on the outcome alone destroyed the plant within 100 steps and bought nothing for the read. Most of the filed gain was context rather than candidate: the pre-write read rose almost as much as the post-write one, so shaping writes "how solvable is this state" into the global pool more than it writes a candidate ranking. Restricting the outcome to L2/L3 slots retained 62–82% of the gain at L4/L5. All of that was offline on rows written by an unshaped plant. This round puts it in the loop.

## What to build

On `aliquot.py`'s stack (fork it as the lineage does; every addition tagged, every knob default off, G-F bit-identical with knobs off), arms that shape the plant during the run and read it with aliquot's projection in the grader's seat. All arms are the composed chooser with the probe channel on, clock-yoked to the seed's banked anchor exactly as aliquot's are, the world's verdict computed for every probe as an instrument no arm consumes, zero bill on every projection arm. Seeds 0 and 2, authorised, no more. Floor, ceiling, the mirror arm (`so_s1`/`so_s2`) and aliquot's frozen-plant projection arm (`al_s1`/`al_s2`, `al_pj_yk` and `al_rt_yk`) are banked and not re-run; they come in through `--bank`.

- **Shaped on the solve verdict, both losses.** An outcome head over the plant's pooled hiddens (duplex's form is a reasonable default; say what you choose) trained by BCE on the world's verdict of the learner's own experienced configurations, the mirror's src-0 diet, with its gradient reaching the plant's trunk in the same optimizer step as the plant's own infill training, and never the emission head (voicing Q1 is why). Aliquot's readout unchanged in the grader's seat, so the only difference from the banked `al_pj_yk` is that the plant has heard outcomes. Duplex's weights, rate and the run's own `gen_steps` are the defaults; state any departure.
- **Shaped on next-level yield.** Same wiring, with the head's target the currency the abstraction grader needs: a per-configuration label derived from the learner's own miner, for instance how many level-(ℓ+1) keys a solved piece contains that are at support, or whether it contributed to the next level's build, computed from `macros.Miner`'s own counts at observe time and nothing from the oracle. Define it in DESIGN before the paid arms and say why. Add a per-probe instrument in the same currency, endogenous (the reader's parse of the substituted configuration counted against the miner) and, as a second column, the oracle's (counted against the true table), so this arm's projection can be scored against the currency it was trained on as well as against the solve verdict. `ROADMAP.md` §6's glossary and `practice/perception/` have the vocabulary and the recorder for mined pieces.
- **Shaped on the solve verdict, outcome only.** The infill loss off during the run. Offline this wrecked the plant; in the loop the question is what the run does when its executor's DP scores through a plant that has left the observer family. Run it, and if it collapses that is the number.

Instruments every arm logs per cycle or at checkpoints, cheap: held-out infill loss on a fixed set of fresh corpus windows; the block head's level-1 parse accuracy against the exact features (an oracle instrument, fine); duplex's nested DP parse at L2–L5 at a few checkpoints; the executor's misfire and corridor parity, since the DP scores through the shaped generator; the projection's held-out AUC on experience and its drift between refits, with the unmasked variant beside it as aliquot logged; and the projection's level on the learner's own filed configurations against the world's verdict, both calibration and ranking, since duplex says the level is what shaping writes.

Two spec lines from the offline round. Bank the trajectory id in the recorder so a leave-one-trajectory-out split is constructible afterwards; the banked dumps carry none. Keep the bank's coin-flip hold draw for comparability with aliquot, but measure the cross-cycle duplicate share, the fraction of held-out bank rows whose (configuration, root) also appears among training rows, so the channel duplex §2.6 named gets a number. And dump `vo_heads.pt`, the rows and the bank's buffer at the end of every arm, so later audits are CPU jobs; aliquot did not and paid for it.

Gates: G-F; Y-1 exact with zero cancelled; a liveness gate that the outcome gradient reaches the trunk (with shaping on, the plant's parameters must differ from a no-shaping twin's after one cycle, and the falsification harness must go red when the gradient is detached); aliquot's M-gates and P-gates on every projection arm; a GPU-side gate block, since aliquot's second preflight defect was invisible to CPU gates. A gate is not reported until shown to fail on a deliberate perturbation.

Budget: about 1.3 GPU-hours per yoked arm, three arms, two seeds, plus preflights. Size memory from the smoke, not from the donor.
