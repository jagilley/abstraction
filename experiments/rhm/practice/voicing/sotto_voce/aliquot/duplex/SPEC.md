## What to build

An offline script that, per seed, reproduces overtone's post-write probe numbers as its gate, then inserts a shaping step and re-reads:

- **Shaped, both losses.** Fine-tune the banked trunk on an outcome head (an MLP over the same pooled features the linear probe reads, or the critic's own form, your call, stated) with BCE against the verdict on the critic's training rows only, while the masked-infill loss on fresh corpus windows stays on, for a modest number of steps at a small learning rate. Then fit the linear probe on the shaped trunk's hiddens and score it on the audit's held-out rows, beside the frozen-trunk and random-trunk numbers on identical rows.
- **Shaped, outcome only.** The same with the infill loss off.
- **Infill only, matched steps.** The same number of steps on the infill loss alone, so "the trunk moved" is separated from "the trunk moved toward the outcome".
- **A world-model diagnostic** on every trunk before and after: held-out masked-infill loss on fresh corpus windows, and the block head's parse accuracy against the level-1 features (an oracle instrument, fine here). If it is cheap, `logit_reading/frontier/common.py::nested_phase` in its template-free mode on corpus windows as well; `practice/perception/` shows how it was run on this grammar. This is the practice-side analogue of shaped's "does it leave the observer family".
- **The transfer split**, on every trunk: fit the linear readout on rows from L2 and L3 slots only, score it on rows from L4 and L5 slots (seed 0's ladder reaches L5, seed 2's reaches L4). Rows are keyed per slot in `vo_rows_meta.json`.
