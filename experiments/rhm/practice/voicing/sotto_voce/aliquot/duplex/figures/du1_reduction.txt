[duplex] du1 — THE OUTCOME ERROR IN THE TRUNK'S WEIGHTS, OFFLINE ON BANKED CHECKPOINTS
====================================================================================================
  TAG STATUS. The `dedup` flag in (G2) is DESIGN 2.5's exact-duplicate filter. It is
  OFF in `du0`, which is therefore the tag that reproduces overtone and the tag
  comparable to the loop's own audit, and ON in `du1`, whose FILED column is the one
  with no exact train/test duplicate in it. `du1`'s (G1) section is EXPECTED to differ
  from the banked dump; the size of that difference is what the channel was worth.

  Facts only. The protocol is `overtone/analyze_dump.py::post_write_probe`'s: a linear
  probe on the trunk's pooled hiddens over the POST-WRITE configuration, fit on the
  critic's TRAINING rows (`hold_code >= 100*vo_critic_hold`) and scored on the rows the
  critic was AUDITED on. Gate G1 asserts that the two trunks overtone already read —
  the banked one and a never-trained one at overtone's own init seed — come back cell
  for cell; every other trunk is that same protocol after a shaping step.

  READS.  pre   [pooled(obs).mean ; pooled(obs)[span].mean]    — the write NOT in the
                input, so whatever it reaches is context difficulty
          pmean  pooled(obs+write).mean                        (overtone's `post_mean`)
          post  [pooled(obs+write).mean ; span mean]           THE READ OF RECORD
                                                               (overtone's `post_slot`)
          postm  the same with one block outside the span masked (`macros.parse_features`'
                `mask_block` idiom; the plant has never seen a fully unmasked input)
          mlp    the run's own critic, recomputed from `vo_heads.pt`, through THIS trunk
          dp     the free prior, from the dump; identical on every trunk by construction

  TRUNKS. frozen = the banked trunk. rand = never trained, overtone's `manual_seed(20260915)`.
          both@N / out@N / infill@N = N steps of (outcome BCE + infill CE) / (outcome BCE
          alone) / (infill CE alone) at lr 1e-4, the loop's own `gen_lr`. The outcome
          rows are the critic's own TRAINING rows (filed and probe both, per slot); the
          held-out rows enter nothing. `both` and `out` see identical outcome rows;
          `both` and `infill` see identical corpus windows and identical masks.
