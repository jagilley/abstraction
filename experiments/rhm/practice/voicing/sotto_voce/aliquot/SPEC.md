## Why this experiment exists

The value-side arc in `experiments/rhm/logit_reading/` (super-writeup: `orbitofrontal/README.md`) established that an outcome-trained value reader is a *projection of the world model*: a linear readout of the trunk's state, weights set by the goal's outcome history, whose level gates action and whose error teaches. It is sighted where the world model is and blind where it is. `ideas/calibration_and_violation_are_one_object.md` §12 (merged yesterday as PRs #125 and #126) synthesises this and adds a note at the end of §12.4 on how a candidate gets priced before it is committed: a linear readout of state can only price what is on the wire, so a candidate has to be *fired* (the world model put into the state it implies) to have a price; replay of taken roads trains the projection, preplay of untaken roads queries it; and preplay alone inherits the projection's two blindnesses (input blindness: `orbitofrontal/regime/`; weight blindness: `practice/maestro/`). The session record is `conversations/one_world_model_many_projections_2026-09-18.md`. Read §12 and that record first; they are the motivation.

The practice arc has a node that already isolates the grading half of this. `experiments/rhm/practice/voicing/sotto_voce/` (README, DESIGN, SPEC, FILES, CONVERSATION; read all of them) asks whether the world has to be paid for verdicts on counterfactual probes, the substituted spellings the learner never wrote. Its *mirror* is an outcome model trained on the learner's own experienced configurations and their world verdicts, applied to the substituted configurations. Finding: the mirror recovers about half the value of paying the world at zero bill, is near-perfect on its own support, and is blind exactly at the frontier, degrading monotonically with level; a committee's disagreement locates the blind region but deletes the frontier as a filter; the hybrid pays the world where the committee disagrees. Two seeds (0 and 2), every arm clock-yoked to its seed's banked anchor, the world's verdict computed for every probe as an instrument no arm consumes (gate M-1).

The mirror is a *separate* net with its own representation, learned from the learner's experience alone. §12.4's object is different: the verdict read off the *world model's own state* through a linear readout. The trunk's representation comes from the corpus and from continued training, not from what the learner chose to write, so its altitude is set by a different history than the mirror's. Whether that makes it sighted where the mirror is blind, blind in a different place (the input-blindness pattern `regime/` measured, over-optimistic after what the trunk re-parses around), or no different, is what this run asks. No outcome is pre-registered; the run says what it says.

`voicing/overtone/` already fitted the same object post hoc and offline: `overtone/analyze_dump.py::post_write_probe` reads the trunk's pooled hiddens over the post-write configuration through `SN.trunk(core, x2)` and fits a linear probe, with two controls that matter here: `pre_slot` (the write not in the input, so context difficulty only) and `rand_slot` (the same architecture never trained, which reached 0.735–0.754 on probe rows and is the floor the trained trunk has to beat). Read `overtone/README.md` and that function. Its numbers on uniform probe rows were roughly pre-write 0.57, random trunk 0.74, trained trunk 0.80, the MLP critic 0.85. That was offline, on banked dumps, fit on probe rows. This run puts the projection in the loop as the grader of record, fit on experience only.

## What to build

On `sotto_voce/sotto_voce.py`'s stack, a new `vo_om_mode` in which the grader of record for probes is a linear readout of the learner's own trunk over the substituted configuration, in place of the mirror. Concretely:

- The readout reads the trunk's hiddens over the substituted final configuration (the same rendered object the mirror grades and the world grades), pooled in some form, plus whatever the mirror gets (the root). Linear or logistic; a closed-form ridge exists in `logit_reading/coeruleus/readout.py` and the practice critic uses BCE. Your call, stated in DESIGN.
- It trains on the mirror's experience diet and nothing else (src 0 in `VoOutcomeBank`: the filed rows' final configurations and the world's verdicts), on the mirror's refresh schedule, and files its probability as the verdict, the way the model-graded arm does. The world's verdict is computed for every probe as an instrument only (M-1 holds unchanged).
- The trunk is the *live* generator the loop trains every cycle. So the readout's features move under it. Decide whether to recompute features of the buffered rows at each refresh or cache them, and log the choice; `practice/tuning/` and `orbitofrontal/adaptation/` are the record on readers fed by a moving learner. Two gotchas from the code: the generator is a masked-infill plant that has never seen a fully unmasked input (`ratchet/macros.py::parse_features` docstring explains and shows the `mask_block` idiom), and `SN.trunk` is how overtone read it.
- Controls, all yoked to the seed's anchor as every sotto_voce arm is: the banked floor (filed diet), ceiling (world-graded probes) and model-graded arm (`so_s1`/`so_s2`) are not re-run. Add the random-init-trunk twin of the projection (overtone's control two, in the loop): same readout, same diet, features from a never-trained trunk of the same architecture with a stated seed. That twin is what says whether the trained representation carries the reading or the link function does. If budget allows after those, a hybrid form (pay the world where the projection and the mirror disagree, or where a K-committee of projections disagrees) is the natural second arm; the plain projection arm and its random twin come first.
- Seeds 0 and 2, to match sotto_voce's two-seed table. You are authorised for these two seeds and no more. Each yoked arm cost sotto_voce about 1.3 GPU-h; budget accordingly and parallelise arms, not seeds beyond two.
