## One-liner

Rubato found that on the practice loop's own substrate no grader in the value seat, the oracle's included, beats the
ungated arm at depth, because the miner mints vocabulary only from the learner's solved answers and so already
filters on payoff: frequency-in-solves and value are nearly one number, and the seat has nothing to buy. This node
removes that filter with one knob. The miner's solved draw is kept exactly as the reference arm makes it, and the
cycle's **unsolved** chosen answers are appended after it at the batch's own proportion, about three failures for
every success; the observation panel that defines a key's consumers reads the same rows. Because the miner only
ever observes the span around the damaged cell, a failed answer contributes precisely its wrong patch, so what
enters is **the learner's own recurring mistakes, memorised as vocabulary**. On both seeds the regime is
quality-limited: the ungated table is three to nine times the reference's size at a third of its precision, and
the loop loses about a quarter of its task accuracy at the last era. **The oracle in the seat now buys something,
for the first time in the lineage**: the world's grade beats the junk floor on three or four of the four deep eras
at both seeds, never revokes a true key across 542 revocations, and rebuilds the reference's L2 table (fourteen true
keys at 0.70 precision) from a junk-fed miner, at about five thousand world queries a cycle. **The read carries the
seat at seed 2 era for era at zero queries**, matching the world's error curve to within a hundredth at eras 4 and
5, and half-carries it at seed 0. Its deficit is located and it is the diet again, now with a cost: the read cannot
price a key's consumers until the readout has been fit on rows at their span, which comes at c105 at seed 0 and c80
at seed 2 exactly as on the clean substrate, and until then the read arm is the floor plus the count rule, while the
junk enters fastest. Once priced it catches about half the junk passes the world catches and wrongly revokes a
tenth to a fifth of the passes the world keeps. Both grades lose the reference at the last era on both seeds; the
seed-0 era-4 loss is the window's timer, the same +0.18 rubato's clean world arm showed, and era 5's mechanism is
open.

## The question

PR 135's two takeaways were that the confidence scalar was the most important finding and that the most important
next step was junk in the substrate, so the value seat could have a function. The orchestrator agreed on both and
added a reading of why the second follows from the record: the seat was **starved, not polluted**. Every candidate
the loop offered had come out of a success, so quality was free and key arrival was the only budget, an
arrival-limited regime in which a value organ is idle whatever grader sits in it. Real goal-directed behaviour is
quality-limited almost everywhere: candidates are unlimited and the constraint is knowing which are worth keeping.
Habit formation runs on repetition and not on outcome, so practised errors become skills unless a separate organ
withholds reinforcement from the repeated things that do not pay. A learner that memorises only from successes has
that organ built into memory itself. The question this node asks: with the filter removed, does a value seat let the
learner mine from everything, failures included, and still reach the depth the solve-filtered miner reaches; and
does the read do it at zero world queries where the world's grade is billed.

Vocabulary, inherited. The **table** is the learner's phrasebook of chunks, a **key** an entry pairing two chunks one
level down; the **miner** counts the span around the era's damaged cell in the learner's own chosen answers and
offers any pairing seen `mine_support` (3) times; the **panel** is the same counter at every level, and a key's
**consumers** are the keys one level up that hold it as a half; the **walk** admits every offered key (the `none`
gate, eight offers a pass); **admit-then-grade** (rubato) grades a served key by firing its consumers single-entry
one level up and revokes it for want of a consumer or a non-positive best gain, re-offering on a new consumer; the
**world** grade prices a fire by the grader's success and is billed, the **read** grade by the shaped projection's
level and is not; the **diet** is the rows the readout has been fit on at the consumers' span. New here: a key is
**junk-only** if it is at support in the miner but not in a **shadow** miner fed only the solved rows of the same
arm's own history; **true / false** is the grammar's mask on the key's spellings; a **single-entry fire** puts the
key alone as a table at its own level's cell on a fresh pool, the same pool graded unfired as the base.

## What was built

`scordatura.py` is `rubato.py` at its `rb_g1d` head plus one substrate knob, `mine_junk`, default off, and with it
off the file is `rubato.py` to the bit (G-F 0.000e+00 at smoke scale, and on the preflight's grade path every series,
event and admission record equal). The draw (DESIGN §1.1–§1.2): the donor's `mine_src`, permutation and cap
untouched, then indices of the cycle's unsolved instances drawn without replacement on a generator of the junk's
own, `n_take · n_uns / n_sol` of them rounded half up, appended after the solved rows; the committable miners, the
panel and the G-Y instrument read the one parse; every piece of bookkeeping that indexes solved instances reads the
first rows. A shadow miner beside every miner and every panel level (`_mj`, a new state name; R-0 over 76) is fed
the solved rows only, so every walk step, graded key, fired consumer, revocation and cycle carries the key's count,
its solved-sourced count, its junk-only flag and its true fraction. `adm_grade_persist` makes rubato's post-hoc
persistence rule a knob (k = 1 is rubato's rule exactly). `sc_fire_keys` runs the single-entry survey on every
at-support key at an arm's end, in an RNG sandbox with everything the reads touch put back. Four in-run checks
(J1–J4: the shared stream untouched, every junk row unsolved, the solved rows first, the shadow fed only solved rows)
assert every cycle; gates G-J and G-P cover the dose, the draw, the shadow and persistence on designed cases. Arms,
built from the donors' specs so the knob is the only difference: `sc_gn_yk` (the ungated junk floor), `sc_gw_yk`
(`rb_gw_yk` + junk: the ceiling as built), `sc_grd_yk` (`rb_grd_yk` + junk + persistence 2). Every gate falsified
before it was reported: G-J / G-P 10 of 10 red; J1–J4's red knobs 4 of 4 on R-1's vehicle, each on its own check.
`reduce_scordatura.py` writes the dose read ([D1]–[D4]), the per-seed grade record ([G1]–[G10]) and the two-seed
table, with rubato's arms as columns.

## 5. On the same fires: half the world's catches, a tenth to a fifth wrong; persistence blunt; the era-4 loss is the window's

**The two currencies** ([G6]). On the read arm's priced L2 key-passes, where the world revokes (its gain exactly 0)
the read revokes 127 of 300 (seed 0) and 111 of 248 (seed 2); where the world keeps, the read revokes 34 of 347 and
72 of 390. By class: on junk-only false keys the read revoked 123 of 262 world-zero passes at seed 0 and kept 139,
its own gain positive on 177 of 300 such passes; on solved-sourced true keys it wrongly revoked 25 of 218 and 33 of
241. No priced L2 pass had negative world gain. On the world arm, the read's counterfactual (no diet) would have
revoked 37 of the 114 passes the world revoked and 51 of the 660 it kept.

**Persistence** ([G8]). Of the 128 / 133 held passes, the world revoked 106 / 85 on the same fires; the key's next
priced pass was worthless (revoked at run 2) on 61 / 76, kept on 30 / 22, absent on 37 / 35. At k = 1 every held pass
would have been a revocation. The hold delayed more correct junk revocations than it prevented wrong ones.

**The window.** The world grade's seed-0 era-4 loss against the reference is +.180 here and +.176 on rubato's clean
substrate, where rubato traced it to one no-consumer revocation on the window's timer blocking five of nine L4 keys.
The same loss at the same seed on two substrates is the window's, not the junk's; the window now elapses four times
faster in cycles and is a known defect kept as built.

## The update

1. **The junk is the learner's own recurring mistakes, and it is mostly value-junk.** The miner observes the span
   around the damaged cell, so a failed answer contributes its wrong patch; what reaches support from the failures
   is a pairing the learner keeps writing at the same damage, which given a table of its own solves nothing (28 of 29
   and 30 of 30 at c40, 40 of 42 and 36 of 38 at c201) and in a quarter to a third of offers harms the growing table.
   A passenger minority is wrong by the grammar and useful in practice, pp3's kind. The learner fails about three
   times for every success, so its most-repeated pairings are its errors, and the walk offers in count order:
   frequency and value have come apart, and the seat has work on both seeds.
2. **The value seat's utility is a property of the producer's filter, not of the seat.** Rubato's "no grader helps"
   and this node's "the oracle helps" are the same seat on two substrates, one where the miner already filtered on
   payoff and one where it does not. The world's grade beats the junk floor on three or four of the four deep eras at
   both seeds, never revokes a true key across 542 revocations, and reconstructs the reference's L2 table from a
   junk-fed miner. The arrival-limited regime idles a value organ; the quality-limited one needs it.
3. **The read is bound by its diet, and in a quality-limited regime its silence is the cost.** Its first priced
   L2 pass is the same cycle as on the clean substrate; the junk changes what is in the table but not when the readout
   has rows at the consumers' span. Before that the read arm is the floor plus the count rule, and that is when junk
   enters fastest. Once priced it makes about half the world's catches at a tenth to a fifth wrong, and at seed 2
   that is enough to match the world's error era for era at zero queries. The same readout prices junk correctly at
   the key's own level by the end, where its diet is the learner's own writes; the grade asks it one level up, where
   the diet is thin. This is rubato's diet finding with a consequence attached.
4. **Both grades lose the reference at the last era on both seeds, and the mechanism is open.** The world arm's
   L2 table is the reference's and its L3 and L4 precision match, yet era 5 sits +.17 to +.24 above the reference.
   Candidates the record does not separate: the frontier level has no consumers and is ungraded by construction,
   its served table two to three times the reference's; the churn, 271 revocations and 148 re-offers against
   rubato's 14 and 6; the learner's first two eras on a polluted table. The seed-0 era-4 loss is separately the
   window's.
5. **Persistence is blunt where junk is abundant.** Holding for two passes delayed about a hundred correct
   revocations to prevent twenty to fifty wrong ones; the dial's right setting depends on the junk rate.
6. **The dose is endogenous and the survey's cell has a floor.** A cleaner table solves more and appends less; a
   single-entry fire at the key's own cell cannot read harm, only worthlessness, so harm is the walk's base-plus-one
   fire's to read.

## What this does not show

The draw holds the reinforced diet fixed and appends the failures; the fully outcome-blind draw, in which the solved
rows would also thin, is a flag away and was not run. Which of the candidates in update 4 carries era 5 is not
separated; no arm grades the frontier level, and the window is the timer rubato built, elapsing four times faster in
cycles. Eras 4 and 5 are twelve and nine cycles. The persistence and margin dials were not run as arms against each
other, and a revoked key is not graded again until re-offered, so the counterfactuals are read off the record. After
the L2 merges most L3 and L4 keys carry a mixed true fraction, so the true / false counts above L2 are of L2 keys
and of the unmerged minority. The read's positive gains on junk consumers' fires (177 of 300 at seed 0) are located
at a span the readout had few rows at and are not explained beyond that. Nothing off the RHM practice substrate.
