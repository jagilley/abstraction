## Why

Read `experiments/rhm/practice/voicing/sotto_voce/aliquot/rubato/README.md` (the update, items 4–6, and "What this does not show") and its `DESIGN.md` §10–§11 first; `CONVERSATION.md` §5 there carries the side chat that motivates this node. The short form: rubato put pp5's value seat into the practice loop as admit-then-grade, and no grader, the oracle's included, beat the ungated arm at depth on either seed. The reason is structural. The loop mints vocabulary by mining pairings that recur in the learner's own *solved* configurations above a support threshold (`mine_from="chosen"` over `ps > 0.5`, around `rubato.py:14640`; `mine_cap` 8 of the cycle's solved instances, in `SB_FLAGS`), and the observation panel that defines a key's consumers reads the same parse `pf` of the same rows (`obs_miners`, `rubato.py:14768`). So the miner already filters on payoff, frequency-in-solves and value are nearly one number, almost no value-junk enters, and key arrival is the budget. The seat had nothing to buy. In the language we have been using: the miner is habit by repetition and the value seat is the organ that withholds reinforcement from repeated things that do not pay, and on this substrate the two cannot come apart because only successes are repeated into the miner.

This node changes the substrate so they can. The question is whether a value seat lets the learner mine from everything, failures included, and still reach the depth the solve-filtered miner reaches, and whether the read does it at zero world queries where the world's grade bills about 2,300 a cycle. Jasper and I have discussed this as moving the loop from an arrival-limited regime, where every candidate is good and the bottleneck is getting enough of them, to a quality-limited one, where candidates are abundant and the bottleneck is telling which are worth keeping, which is the regime a value organ exists for. The results may of course say something else; read them on their own terms.

## The substrate change

One knob, default off, with the fork bit-identical to `rubato.py` with it off (the lineage's G-F gate; rubato's `fidelity_smoke` shows how it is done and gated). Fork `rubato.py` into a new sibling child of `aliquot/`. `scordatura/`, the instrument deliberately retuned while the piece stays the same, would fit; choose your own name if another fits better.

The draw I would start from, and why: keep the solved draw exactly as `st_gn_yk` makes it (the same 8 rows from the same permutation on the same shared stream) and *append* `mine_junk` rows drawn from the cycle's unsolved chosen answers on a new generator of their own, so the reinforced diet is held fixed and every difference from `st_gn_yk` is the junk. A fully outcome-blind draw (8 random rows of all chosen answers) would also cut the true rows to a fraction and confound junk with slower arrival, which no seat can fix; that version is a flag away if the record wants it later. The dose I would start at is the outcome-blind proportion, unsolved to solved as the batch has them, so about 26 junk rows beside the 8 if roughly a quarter of instances solve; read `st_gn_yk`'s own solve rate per cycle off its record rather than trusting that number. Both the committable miners and the panel must see the junk rows, so that "no consumer" cannot do the seat's work for free; since both read `pf` from `mine_src`, one site should carry it, but check that the bookkeeping which indexes solved instances (`mine_take`, `_mine_idx`, `grec["mine_*"]`, the `tutti` dose ledger) stays over the solved rows; appending the junk after them is the simplest way. `sb_yield_label` is zero on unsolved by design and `st_gn_yk` shapes on `vo_sh_target="solve"`, so the shaping target should not move; confirm it. The miner's own comment says an unsolved answer is not a valid derivation; for the miner's mechanics that is exactly the junk we want, but check nothing downstream assumes a mined row solved.

Instruments, all zero GPU: per cycle the junk rows mined; per key a shadow count of its solved-sourced observations beside its total, so "at support here but not under `st_gn_yk`'s miner" is a column on every admitted, graded and revoked key; the true-table mask on every such key (pp3 and the tables' logged precision already use it); and on the graded arms the world's and the read's value on every fire as rubato already records them.

## The arms and the order

1. **The knob, its gates, and the pre-check, seed 0.** Fork, G-F, a designed gate on the stratified draw and the shadow count (the G-D pattern in `rb_grade_gates`, falsified before it is reported), then run the ungated junk arm (`admit="none"`, no grade, `mine_junk` on, states saved at every era boundary and every tenth cycle as the lineage now requires) from cycle 0 with `rb_stop_cycle` around cycle 40, where the record shows L2 keys admitted and their L3 consumers forming. Then fire the junk-only keys single-entry against the world offline (pp1's fire, which the grade reuses one level up: the key alone as a table forced as the macro on a fresh pool of its level's cell, the same pool graded unfired as the base), and the solved-sourced keys beside them for scale, so we know how much of what enters has world gain at or below zero, how much is strictly harmful, and how much is pp3-style passenger junk. **Report here with the dose read.** If the dose looks wrong (nothing junk-only reaches support, or everything does and the cap has become the filter), say what you would change and why, and continue with the change you judge right.
2. **The floor, the ceiling and the claim, both seeds.** Resume the seed-0 junk arm to 201 cycles as the ungated floor, start seed 2's from cycle 0 with states, and restore two graded arms per seed from the floor's latest save before a grade can fire (rubato read that cycle off the reference's saves; do the same): the world grade exactly as `rb_gw_yk` (margin 0, window 160, four consumers; the ceiling as built), and the read grade as `rb_grd_yk` (diet 512) plus one dial rubato only re-applied post hoc: revoke only after two consecutive non-positive priced passes (`adm_grade_persist`, default 1, gated). Everything else, the window and the re-offer rule included, stays as rubato built it, so what changes against `rb_g1`/`rb_g2` is the substrate and one persistence knob; the window and re-offer are known defects to read the record against, not to fix here.
