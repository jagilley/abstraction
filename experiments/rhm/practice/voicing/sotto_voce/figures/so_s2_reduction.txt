====================================================================================================
    The held-out split is by the parity gate's own BIJECTIVE row code, not a coin flip,
    so a context recurring across cycles cannot sit on both sides. `auc` is None where
    one class is absent on the held-out rows — an AUC is undefined then, and saying so
    beats reporting 0.5. DENOMINATORS AND BASE RATES ARE PART OF THE READING: the
    preflight's AUCs sat on 16-26 rows at base rate 0.045-0.08 and were a path check.

    [sotto] `auc_world` is the probe AUC scored against the WORLD'S verdict on the
    same rows rather than against the verdict that was FILED. On a world-graded arm the
    two columns are the same number; on a model-graded arm they are not, and the one
    the question is about is `auc_world` — can the critic rank what the world would have
    said, having been taught by a mirror. `filed==world` is the share of the filed
    probe rows whose verdict the world agreed with, i.e. the mirror's own accuracy on
    the rows it actually put into the critic.

    [Q3] THE TWO DIETS ARE READ APART and are NEVER pooled. The filed rows are the
    practice beam's own writes — a narrow, self-selected candidate set (Q0 finding 3:
    at the frontier almost every write falls in one token class) — and the probe rows
    are the babbler's substitutions, a DIFFERENT class by construction. A pooled AUC
    would read that candidate-set difference as discrimination. Both columns carry

    `dp_features` exactly. Where the treatment RE-DECIDED the write, a mismatch with the

    re-decided call, OPEN and CLOSED alike, and `n_fired` counts only calls the head
    executed — so on an arm whose corridor is mostly shut, Q2's `shift - misfire` was

    explain. A negative value means the head matched the DP on rows the treatment had
    moved — possible, and printed rather than clipped. Q2's undecomposable form is kept

[N] THE COMPOSED CHOICE — how often the critic moved the write off the DP's row
====================================================================================================
    On a governed slot the composed chooser is  argmax_t  z(dp_t / span) + w * z(critic_t)
    over the on-table candidates, each z-scored over the candidate SET. `moved` is the
    share of governed calls where that argmax is not the DP's own argmax — MEASURED at
    the choice, never inferred from the write.

    `dp_vs_head` is printed beside it and is a DIFFERENT quantity: on an OPEN slot the
    executor's own would-be write is the head's emission, not the DP's row, so the two
    priors already disagree before the critic is consulted. Reading `moved` without it
    would attribute that standing disagreement to the critic. It is 0 on a closed slot
    by construction (there the DP IS the writer).

    A `moved` share near 0 says the critic is decorative at this w; near 1 says the DP
    is. Neither is a defect and neither is read as one here.

[P] THE MIRROR — the outcome model against the world
====================================================================================================
    The outcome model reads a RENDERED CONFIGURATION and its root — what the world's
    grader reads — and is trained by BCE against the world's verdict on the
    configurations the learner actually produced (the filed rows' `fin` and `y`,
    deduplicated by (configuration, root) within the cycle). It is a separate object:
    its own parameters, its own optimizer, its own numpy streams, never the plant's.

    TWO NUMBERS, and the gap between them is the whole question. On HELD-OUT
    EXPERIENCE the mirror is scored on rows drawn from the same distribution it was
    polished on. On PROBES it is scored on substitutions the learner never wrote — off
    its own support by construction — against the world's verdict, which every arm
    computes as an instrument and no arm consumes (gate M-1).

[Y] THE CLOCK YOKES — realised against planned (gate Y-1's check, post hoc)
====================================================================================================
    A clock yoke replays its source's realised commit and advance CYCLES and decides
    nothing else. That is the round's whole control: the chooser is read at L4 and L5
    with the same slots open at the same cycles as the anchor, so a level-resolved
    readout is not silently comparing two different ladders. Gate Y-1 asserts the match
    in `preflight`; this is the same check on the paid tag.

    `cancelled` actions are EXCLUDED from the match and printed separately: a replayed
    commit whose build is empty is cancelled by the substrate, not by the clock. Those
    are a finding about the arm's BOOK — it had nothing to commit at a cycle the anchor
    did — and are the one place a yoked arm's ladder can still fall short of its source.
