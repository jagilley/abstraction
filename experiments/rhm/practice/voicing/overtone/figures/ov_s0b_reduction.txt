    Every column here is read on the SAME held-out rows as the critic's own AUC, and
    none of them has ever seen a verdict:

      dpz    the DP's per-entry score of the candidate, z-scored over the candidate set
             — EXACTLY the prior term the composed chooser sums, in its own units
      dp     the same before the z-score (per-block, i.e. divided by the span)
      conf   the negative mean entropy of the trunk's next-feature distribution over the
             slot's blocks — confidence WHERE it is about to write, candidate-blind
      marg   the mean top1-top2 logit margin over the same blocks
      crit   the critic, restricted to the rows where the prior is defined, so the
             comparison and the increment below share ONE denominator
      comb   an OUT-OF-FOLD logistic combination of (dpz, critic logit); `prior` is the

             BEYOND-PRIOR increment, which is the Steenwyk-shaped question

    written candidate is usually the DP's own argmax, so `dpz` there reads the prior's

    class. They are not the same question and are never pooled.

[M] THE CORRIDOR UNDER A MOVED WRITE — misfire with the treatment's own draws removed
====================================================================================================
    `n_misfire` counts FIRED rows (the head executed) whose span did not match
    `dp_features` exactly. Where the treatment RE-DECIDED the write, a mismatch with the
    DP's row IS the treatment and not a defect — so the raw rate is not the corridor's
    health on a treated arm.

    [Q3] THE SPLIT Q2 COULD NOT MAKE. `n_sampled` / `n_xp_shift` count every
    re-decided call, OPEN and CLOSED alike, and `n_fired` counts only calls the head
    executed — so on an arm whose corridor is mostly shut, Q2's `shift - misfire` was
    a subtraction across two different denominators and `excess` had to be withheld
    wherever `shift > n_misfire`. The recorder now tallies the shift BY PATH
    (`n_shift_fired` / `n_shift_closed`), so `excess` below is
        (n_misfire - shift_FIRED) / n_fired
    on the same denominator throughout. It is the misfire the moved write does NOT
    explain. A negative value means the head matched the DP on rows the treatment had
    moved — possible, and printed rather than clipped. Q2's undecomposable form is kept
    in the last two columns so a Q2 tag reduces to the same numbers it did.

[N] THE COMPOSED CHOICE — how often the critic moved the write off the DP's row
====================================================================================================
    On a governed slot the composed chooser is  argmax_t  z(dp_t / span) + w * z(critic_t)
    over the on-table candidates, each z-scored over the candidate SET. `moved` is the
    share of governed calls where that argmax is not the DP's own argmax — MEASURED at
    the choice, never inferred from the write.

    `dp_vs_head` is printed beside it and is a DIFFERENT quantity: on an OPEN slot the
    executor's own would-be write is the head's emission, not the DP's row, so the two
    priors already disagree before the critic is consulted. Reading `moved` without it
    would attribute that standing disagreement to the critic. It is 0 on a closed slot
    by construction (there the DP IS the writer).

    A `moved` share near 0 says the critic is decorative at this w; near 1 says the DP
    is. Neither is a defect and neither is read as one here.

[O] BABBLING OFF-STREAM — the probe's counts, what it graded, and its share of the bill
====================================================================================================
    Per governed slot per cycle, up to `vo_probe_n` filed contexts: the trajectory's
    FINAL configuration with a DIFFERENT on-table class substituted at that slot,
    rendered and graded once. The verdict answers 'would this write ALONE have been
    right, holding the rest of the trajectory fixed' — not 'would the beam have
    solved it'; the rest of the trajectory is the beam's own, unchanged. Said here
    because the probe's solve rate is only comparable to the FILED rate under that
    reading.

    PRICED: every probe grading is a grounding on the meter at `d_fb`, inside the
    cycle's own ledger. `share` below is of the WHOLE cycle's priced ledger, the
    probe's own cost included — the number the design's ~5% cap is stated against.
    Gate V-4B asserts the bill moved by exactly the probe count and gate V-5 that

[Y] THE CLOCK YOKES — realised against planned (gate Y-1's check, post hoc)
====================================================================================================
    A clock yoke replays its source's realised commit and advance CYCLES and decides
    nothing else. That is the round's whole control: the chooser is read at L4 and L5
    with the same slots open at the same cycles as the anchor, so a level-resolved
    readout is not silently comparing two different ladders. Gate Y-1 asserts the match
    in `preflight`; this is the same check on the paid tag.

    `cancelled` actions are EXCLUDED from the match and printed separately: a replayed
    commit whose build is empty is cancelled by the substrate, not by the clock. Those
    are a finding about the arm's BOOK — it had nothing to commit at a cycle the anchor
    did — and are the one place a yoked arm's ladder can still fall short of its source.
