    THE PRIOR'S OWN RANKING OF THE VERDICT, recomputed here from the stored DP score
    through the FINAL core, over the WHOLE buffer. Two things make it not the same
    number as the audit's `dp_auc` and both are stated rather than glossed:
      - the audit reads the 10% held-out slice once per CYCLE and the reduction takes a
        median over those reads; this is ONE read over every row the buffer still holds
        at the end, scored through the core as it finished;
      - a row whose candidate the final operative table no longer holds has no DP score
        (`n` below counts only the rows that do).
    Agreement between the two is therefore a consistency check on the machinery, not an
    identity, and the denominators are printed so the comparison is honest.
