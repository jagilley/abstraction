woodshed / F1 part 1 — the trust-formation-rate instrument, read off existing logs
====================================================================================================
pi per-level proposal mass = sum of `pi.mean_mass` over that level's (level,node)
slots (level-major layout, prop_net.slot_layout). It is a normalised distribution
over the live action set, averaged over the probe batch. Slots not in the live set
are masked to -inf and read exactly 0.0, so a pre-arrival level's 0.000 is a
construction, not a measurement. tau = probe cycle - arrival cycle.

(1) POST-ARRIVAL GROWTH — one row per (arm, level) with an arrival
====================================================================================================
  m0/mend  : mass at the first post-arrival probe / at the last probe
  u0/uend  : the UNIFORM reference — this level's open slots / all open slots, i.e.
             the mass a flat pi would put here. m0 is not zero at arrival because
             newly unmasked slots enter the softmax with untrained logits.
  lift     : mend / uend. 1.0 = pi is indifferent to the level; >1 = it favours it.
  d16/d32  : mass gained per 10 cycles over tau in [0,16] and [0,32] — the
             censoring-safe rate, since every arm is measured on the same window.
  d/10cyc  : OLS slope of mass on tau over the WHOLE post-arrival window (r2)
  up       : fraction of consecutive post-arrival probe steps that increase
  rho      : Spearman rank correlation of mass with tau
  t1/2     : cycles after arrival to reach half the arm's own final mass
  T, d0    : saturating fit m = M(1-exp(-tau/T)); d0 = M/T is the initial rate and
             is identified even when T is not. `*` marks T outside the window or a
             fit with r2 < 0.5 — read d0/d32 there, not T.
  nav      : open (level,node) slots at the last probe
  win      : tau at the last probe (the observation window; censoring matters)

====================================================================================================
(6) INSTRUMENT — what this readout can and cannot resolve
====================================================================================================
  1. Probe cadence is `probe_every` = 8 cycles, plus the first and last cycle of every era, so any time constant below ~8 cycles is at the sampling limit and T/t1/2 for such cells is reported but not resolved. There is no per-cycle pi mass series in `results.json`; the only per-cycle pi artifact in the arc is audiation's `snapshots/probe_trace.npz` (raw, unmasked logits), which lives on the volume and is not in the fetched mirror.
  2. m0 is NOT zero. A level's slots are masked out of the softmax until its table is committed, and they re-enter carrying whatever the untrained logits give — near the uniform share of the live action set. So the number to read is the rise above the uniform reference (`lift`), not the raw mass; `m0` differences across arms are partly the head's arbitrary state at the moment of unmasking.
  3. Right-censoring is severe and uneven: L4 arrivals leave 21-46 cycles of window while L2 arrivals leave 60-180. `M` (the saturating asymptote) is extrapolation wherever `win` is short relative to `T`; `m@tau` at matched tau is the censoring-safe comparison and is why section (2) exists.
  4. Mass is a normalised distribution over the live action set: L1's 32 primitive slots always compete, and every level's mass is jointly constrained. A rise at L3 is arithmetically a fall somewhere else.
  5. A level's mass is spread over only the (level,node) slots that hold a table entry, and extension/recert opens more slots mid-run. `navail` and `m/slot` separate the two, but the split is only read at the last probe here.
  6. Arrival for gifted arms is cycle 1 by construction, which is also the cycle the probe series starts, so their m0 is the untrained head's mass, whereas an earned arm's m0 is measured after 18-129 cycles of unrelated training. The two m0s are not the same kind of number; m@tau is.
  7. Single seed everywhere. The stream-displaced twins (`ma_s1`, `cr3_s1`, `as_s1`) bound draw-luck only. Census finding 7's floors apply to error-rate readings, not directly to mass; no floor for mass has been measured, and the displaced pairs here are the only handle on one.
  8. The exposure column exists only where `entry` recording was on; census's gifted arms have no `entry` and are absent from section (5).
