## Findings

### 1. The second extension is an arrival problem in flat coordinates and a constant-size problem in the grammar's own — the wall belongs to the key (`sizing/`)

Offline, over 40 banked arms across nine tags, the substrate's own `Miner.build` replayed
entry-for-entry against every logged commit (101/101, the L4 rung for the first time):

- **Levels, exactly**: |T| = 14 / 56 / 816 / 205,824 / 1.31e10 at L2…L6, the ratio
  `m^(s^(ℓ−1))` — doubly exponential, independent of v. The r² law is exact at L5
  (measured/predicted 0.91–1.05).
- **Arrival binds, not the ratchet.** Over the arms' own frozen L4 books the L5 ratchet admits
  1–15 true entries (`census_extend` moves it 1→4, 2→7); E[true L5 keys at support 3] is
  **0.000** at the run's ~1,600 L5-node observations. A 22-key L5 book needs ~158k observations
  (≈58 GPU-h/arm). The arrival cut at L5 is ~5.9e9× against the ratchet's 1.2–21× at L2–L4.
- **No admissible world helps; the rule draw does.** Every (v, s, m) with a cheap L5 sits at the
  occupancy `tall/` measured inadmissible. Junk mass is a property of the *draw*: over twelve
  draws of the same grammar L4 junk spans 0.000–0.905, seeds 6 and 10 are collision-free at
  every level, and at seed 10 the same book costs ~9,866 observations (≈3.6 GPU-h/arm) — at the
  price of the arc's native aleatoric channel.
- **The L5 yield gauge is derivable and sub-floor.** `tol_yield_l5` = 0.0639 over 3,658
  null-ABBA windows; signal/floor **0.26** at L5 and **0.08** at L6 against 1.38/1.53 at L3/L4;
  A1's thermostat replayed on the L5 series fires in 14/29 arms on sub-floor motion. At that rung
  only the reads from *behind* the frontier clear their floors.
- **Under a parent-feature key every level has `v·m = 16` rules** — a 12,864× shrink at L5.
  This is `fourwall`'s re-key basis, and it puts F3's index ops on the critical path as a
  *level-size* lever, not an r²-wall lever.
- **Grip is depth-invariant at exactly (s−1)/s and dies at the level cap.** `antiphon`'s era-4/5
  dose collapse was `max_macro_level = 4` meeting era levels 4–5, not depth: at `maxl = 6`
  structural grip is 0.500 in every era. What falls with depth is the whole-key dose, the half
  the ask cannot author: dose ≈ κ/|T_(ℓ−1)| with κ ≈ 2–3.5 (extrapolated 3.1e-3 at L5). Node
  choice buys 1.17×/1.04× at L3/L4 and nothing at L5, so the demand-support norm need not be
  broken; a rehearsal-shaped question (re-pose keys seen exactly twice) would ~1.9× the L4 book
  in one op.
- Correction to the round's own briefing: the world is m = 2, not m = 4 (`tall/` measured m = 4
  inadmissible); every banked arm and every number here is at m = 2.

### 3. Worthless answers exist natively, no judge is nerdsniped at any dose, and value goes to the judges in the band (`tr_s0`)

**What a worthless answer can be here.** Credit is the world's exact grader, and every move
writes a world-legal constituent, so a foreign grammar cannot mint keys: its instances are
either never solved or solved as world-legal derivations that mint *true* keys. Rejected with
reasons. What remains is **non-convertibility**, and it is exact: every true level-L flat's
halves are true level-(L−1) flats (0 exceptions at L3/L4/L5), so a candidate whose clean half
parses to a junk key can never contribute a true key at the target level. **The world already
serves 54.6% such candidates at the era-3 mining node.** The trap knob is the fraction `f` of
the K = 2048 menu built from them, d\*-matched (TV 0.039), drawn from the world's own cell.

**The bound that reframed the round, offline.** The novelty selector is breadth-first, so its
junk intake is capped by the *pool ratio* (96 junk : 44 true halves = 0.686) whatever the
menu serves; raising `f` cannot strengthen the nerdsnipe, lowering it can (4.57× the null's
junk rate at f = 0.15). This is why `antiphon` finding 4 read mild. Two guards die offline: the
delivery ledger's credit rule is "not yet at support," so its junk/true weight ratio is 1.04 at
era 3 and up to 8.7 at era 2 — **it is not a noisy-TV guard** (a correction to `antiphon`'s
machinery description); and a ratchet guard that prefers halves already in the learner's own
lower table inherits that table's precision (0.25–0.50). **The learner's own use record does
not die**: the beam's per-entry selection mass on committed rows is 0.73–0.93 true at L3
against table precision 0.25–0.50 (lift 1.8–3.2×; up to 5.8× at L4), and a guard that
use-weights the rows one level below the clean half cuts the judge's junk share from 0.686 to
0.43–0.54 (ceiling 0.154).

**In vivo, at f = 0.90** (five 201-cycle arms; era-1 twins bit-identical to their `an_s0`
counterparts at 0.000e+00, diverging at c61 = the first cycle the trap exists; `t90_trust` ≡
`t90_novel` through c110, so the guard's effect is isolated to era 3):

| oracle | 0.448 | **33** / 29 | — | −0.052 | −0.005 |
| **trust-guarded** | **0.728** | **28** / 27 | **11 at 0.364** | **+0.276** (9.2× floor) | **+1.022** (2.5×) |
| guard-free novelty | 0.864 | 27 / 24 | 7 at 0.286 | +0.055 | +0.268 |

No judge over-takes the null (over-take 0.81/0.96/0.85), the volume pin holds (fill 0.945–0.980
against `an_s0`'s 0.955–0.985), and the trust arm's take sits on the offline pool-share bound.
Trust over novelty is a rank claim (+1 end, +3 matched spend) on identical frozen L2/L3 tables,
with the larger and cleaner L4 book. **The oracle's premium over the null fell from 28 keys at
the native dose to 16** — against the offline prediction that it would rise — because at
f = 0.90 only 205 of 2048 candidates carry true halves and the difficulty quota forces even the
oracle to take 45% distractors. And deep-era value inverts the mining order: the incumbent
judge banks the fewest true keys of the three judges and posts the largest value in the tag;
the oracle banks the most and posts none.

## Joint interpretation (discussed with Jasper 2026-09-02 — argued, not measured)

- **(a) The loop's three actions now have measured owners, and the assignment is not the one
  the QUEUE guessed.** The one-level-up yield gauge owns *what to freeze* — the type law in its
  level-scoped form, exactly as `teacher_slot` measured; δ-silence as commit owner was a
  floor-sensitive result. The execution currency owns the within-level decision it can actually
  make, *when this era's practice has plateaued*, and giving it the advance is the assignment
  that beat the ceiling. When the advance clock beats the commit clock, a level stays live —
  which is the extension. `two_deltas` (b)'s "currency-scoped rather than level-scoped"
  sharpening reads, after this round, as: the execution currency has a seat, and it is the
  curriculum, not the crossing.
- **(b) The question judge that carries value is a learning-progress band-pass, and value is
  trust-carried.** Across `an_s0`, `an_s2`, and `tr_s0` the mining order tracks the novelty
  edge (oracle, then novelty) and the value order tracks the band (the incumbent, then trust),
  with novelty and the oracle at zero. The two winning selectors both prefer questions the
  learner has already shown it can solve the intended way — halves that landed, rows the beam
  has used — and π learns from solved trajectories (`woodshed`). So a judge that poses
  solvable-as-designed questions trains π on successful macro use and forms trust; a judge that
  poses the needy keys grows the table and starves π. This is the SPEC's band-pass, measured,
  and `census`'s concentration-over-coverage law arriving in the question port. The delivery
  ledger is the solvability half of that band-pass, not a noisy-TV guard. The one endogenous
  guard that discriminates worthless questions is the use record — trust — which ties the trap
  to Track F.
- **(c) The corruption payoff on this substrate is bounded by an exact ratio, and a junk-rich
  world shrinks even the oracle's coverage.** Breadth-first novelty is itself a passive guard
  capped by the grammar's ambiguity ratio at the mining node; the nerdsnipe is reached by
  cleaning the menu, not dirtying it. In the noisy world, at pinned difficulty, coverage stops
  being purchasable and what remains purchasable is the band — which is where the value went.
  Jasper's real-world framing survives in that form: the noisier the world, the more the value
  system's job is *which* questions, but the job is solvability-weighted novelty, not junk
  avoidance.
- **(d) The meter keeps the reward per right question and raises the cost per wrong one — and
  this round measured the second without being able to measure the first.** The solve tax is
  real and uniform. The reward side never arrived because the converter, π's imitation on rows
  per cycle, was cut six-fold on a ladder whose deep eras are 21 cycles long. So "no selector
  beats the null on value" is a statement about the ladder and the current selectors, not
  about metering: none of the selectors weights solvability, because all were designed where it
  was free. The selector the meter asks for is the band-pass with its lower edge priced.
- **(e) The second rung is a change of key.** Arrival in flat coordinates cannot be bought on
  any admissible world at any affordable budget, and no lever in this round — pacing,
  selection, the guard — touches it. Under the grammar's own key every level is sixteen rules
  and the climb is constant-cost. The practice learner's keys are surface tuples; climbing past
  L4 means discovering that several of its tuples are one thing. That is the merge op of
  `recurrence_manufactures_confounds` §5, the quotient whose limit that doc calls a belief,
  specced as [`../enharmonic/`](../enharmonic/SPEC.md) with the sizing's facts as its premises
  and the mirror loop as the loop it sits under.

- `an_m0`'s consumption eras are sized for abundance's rows; the reward side of the meter is
  untested until a matched-row ladder runs. Its value handle is the null's own displacement,
  not a twinned floor.
- `tr_s0`'s trust-over-novelty mining edge is +1–3 keys on a null-to-oracle span of 16: a rank
  claim. The trust selector has no native-dose twin. The era-5 floors are thin (0.410).
- The sizing's L5 statements about *which* keys are modelled from the DGP marginal (the L5 key
  stream is not logged); the counts are measured. Everything downstream of junk mass is at one
  rule draw.
- `sizing/` and `tutti/DESIGN.md` §8 disagree on the record: §8 was written before the sizing
  and is corrected in place with a note; the sizing is authoritative.
