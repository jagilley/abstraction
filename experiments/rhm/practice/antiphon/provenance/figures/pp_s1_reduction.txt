  over-predicts here because the conjunction it assumes is not independent:

  the term that misses: within a trajectory, the sources of its L3 applications
  are POSITIVELY CORRELATED — a trajectory that explores into one L3 macro tends
  to explore into the others — so 'every L3 application was pi's own ask' is
  rarer than an independent model predicts. Recorded as an apparatus fact; it is
  the same class of error as the `wd_s0` sizing miss, caught this time before the
  round rather than after.
