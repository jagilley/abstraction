(4) CIRCULARITY CHECK — content or composition?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The concern: `exaff_prop` conditions on pi-PROPOSED executions and trust is pi's
  mass, so both move with pi's behaviour. Three ways the conditioning could
  manufacture the ordering, and each has its own cut below:
    (i)  ASK-CONDITIONING — the statistic only looks where pi asked.  -> (4a)
    (ii) VOLUME — credit makes pi propose L3 ~1.9x more than `exact`. -> (4b)
    (iii) RAMP — the per-cycle rate DECAYS after arrival, and the arms' proposal
          volume ramps at different speeds (tau to prop_share>=0.90: reh 14, none
          18, exp 22), so an execution-weighted run mean puts different weight on
          the high early part of the same curve.                     -> (4b)/(4c)

  Every column below is L3 (rehearsed); `handle` is the identical statistic on L2,
  where rehearsal never fired (n = 3). `mult` = L3 spread / L2 handle.

       The beam phase is clean (0 everywhere). Size-matching does not PROVE a probe
       row is the committed table's — it only fails to detect the mixing — so the
       surviving probe cycles are ambiguous rather than clean, and no probe number
       is reported. This is a property of the DONOR's `entry.hist` too (gate (c)
       shows the join reproduces it element-wise), so it demotes the `probe@end` /
       `probearr` columns of `figures/reduction.txt` section (2). Nothing in
       sections (3)-(4) of that file used the probe phase.

  (4b) VOLUME-MATCHED AND RAMP-FREE READS

       Uniform subsampling to a matched execution count leaves a cycle's exafference
       RATE unbiased, so the matched-count statistic is exactly a re-weighting of the
       per-cycle rates with weights COMMON to all arms. Two such weightings:
         equal-cycle   every post-arrival cycle counts once
         matched-count cycle c weighted by min over the trio of that cycle's count
       Neither can be moved by an arm proposing more than another.

  (4c) DECOMPOSITION of each pairwise run-mean gap into RATE and WEIGHT

       run-mean = sum_c w_c r_c, with w_c the arm's own share of proposed L3
       executions in cycle c and r_c that cycle's exafference rate. For arms X, Y:
         gap = sum_c wbar_c (rX_c - rY_c)   [RATE: same weights, different content]
             + sum_c (wX_c - wY_c) rbar_c   [WEIGHT: same content, different ramp]
       wbar = (wX+wY)/2, rbar = (rX+rY)/2. A gap that is mostly WEIGHT is the
       composition artifact the concern names.
