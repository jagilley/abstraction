(5) CAVEATS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * The efference log is DOWNSTREAM of use: an entry enters E_l only by being
    served often enough that the agent's own solved rollouts spell it out at
    support >= 3. Self-tagging is therefore a near-deterministic consequence of
    an entry being used, not an independent variable — section (2)'s concentration
    column is mechanism, not effect.
  * No floor for pi mass exists anywhere in the arc (woodshed FILES.md), and no
    floor for ANY provenance statistic exists either. The only handles are the
    as_s1 stream twins (n=1 pair per family) and wd_s1's unrehearsed L2 (n=3).
  * Single seed everywhere; rehearsal is not compute-matched (~2x practice compute).
  * mine_support = 3 is a free parameter of the match rule (a key must be emitted
    three times before it counts as posed) and it is NOT sweepable offline: the
    banked logs carry `keys_at_support` only at that one threshold. `n_at_support`
    below shows how much the log SIZE would move; the membership does not exist on
    disk at any other threshold. A machinery limit, not a measured invariance.
