[1b] THE BREADTH-FIRST TAKE SHARE — exact, no mining model needed.
     `_round_robin` takes one candidate per DISTINCT key in priority order before it
     takes a second, and every uncovered half ties at novelty 1.0. So a novelty
     selector's distractor share is set by the POOL RATIO, not by the menu's mass:
       era       junk halves  true halves  pool share  over-take vs the null at native f
       L2n12               9           13       0.409                               1.23x
       L3n6               96           44       0.686                               1.25x
     => a breadth-first novelty judge OVER-takes the trap while f < the pool share
        and UNDER-takes it above; the nerdsnipe it can produce is bounded by that
        ratio, and the ratio is a property of the grammar's ambiguity at the node.
     The knob's useful direction is therefore BOTH ways. Predicted over-take
     (novelty judge's junk intake / the null's) across the menu's worthless rate f:
       era         f=0.15     f=0.3   f=0.546     f=0.7     f=0.9
       L2n12         2.73      1.36      0.75      0.58      0.45
       L3n6          4.57      2.29      1.26      0.98      0.76
     (a ratio above 1 is a judge doing WORSE than not selecting at all — the
      nerdsnipe; below 1 is breadth capping its own junk intake.)

     A channel is 'unlearnable' only if its keys never recur, i.e. if its pool is
     larger than the era's observation budget divided by mine_support. On this
     grammar the reachable junk-half pool is SMALLER, so every junk half closes —

[8] THE REJECTED CHANNELS
     (i) A FOREIGN GRAMMAR (a second RHM at the same shape, independent rules).
         `crystallize.units.apply_move` materialises every span through a max-sum DP
         over THIS world's `rules_t` and renders it through `canon`, so the agent
         cannot produce a foreign-legal answer; and `run_arm` block (b) mines only
         `out['x'][ps > 0.5]`, i.e. answers the WORLD's exact grader passed. A
         foreign instance is therefore never mined and the channel degenerates into
         'burn priced budget, bank nothing'. Grading it under a foreign grader would
         put foreign-legal answers into the value buffer and the generator's
         fine-tune set — moving the plant, not the question distribution.
     (ii) AN UNLEARNABLE CHANNEL AT MATCHED DIFFICULTY. d* small => near a legal
         derivation => solved => mined; and [6] shows the reachable junk-half pool is
         smaller than budget/support, so whatever is posed recurs and closes. An
         unlearnable arm therefore has to give up the difficulty pin, exactly as
         `q_comp_free` gives it up to be the honest comfort pole.
