(Q2) THE VALUE STEP: HOW MUCH OF IT IS THE ANALYTIC delta?
==============================================================================
  BCE's logit gradient is delta_t = sigmoid(v0_t) - y, so if `Delta v` at the datum's
  OWN states were simply -eta*delta_t, then `a forecaster without the grade leaves a
  residual that IS the vector delta` would be an IDENTITY about the loss, not a finding
  about self-models. WHAT THIS CELL CAN MEAN: a high R^2 from delta alone means the
  value component is wiring and nothing built on it is evidence about forecasting.
  WHAT IT CANNOT MEAN: it says nothing about the pi component or about spillover, which
  are where the generalization geometry lives. Adam normalises per-parameter, and one
  step is shared by all 9 states, so the identity is not guaranteed a priori.
    source                          R^2(dv_t)
    delta_t = sigmoid(v0)-y  [1]        0.588
    (v0, y)                  [2]        0.612
    all 9 deltas of the event [9]       0.596
    + z                       [.]       0.530
    raw corr(dv_t, -delta_t) = +0.696; sign agreement 94.8%

(Q3) INCREMENTAL DECODE OVER THE SCALAR delta THAT IS ALREADY FREE ON THE WIRE
==============================================================================
  Every cell below is R^2 with a COVARIATE BASELINE already in it: the per-state
  analytic delta = sigmoid(v0)-y, its mean, the grade, and log|Delta_i| -- i.e. every
  SCALAR that is already free on the wire, INCLUDING the update's own magnitude. The
  first row IS that baseline; every later row is the same fit with a VECTOR source
  added, so the gap between them is what the vector form buys.
  WHAT THESE CELLS CAN MEAN: an increment over the baseline row means the VECTOR update
  carries spillover content the free SCALARS do not. WHAT THEY CANNOT MEAN: nothing here
  licenses a claim about privacy (that is Q4's ladder), and the RAW columns are not the
  evidence -- only the [cs] columns are, because a gain that vanishes under cycle x
  update-order residualisation was the calendar and not the datum.
  The earlier `ref rms / own rms` ratio target was DROPPED: `own_rms` sat in both the
  target and every update-derived source, so those cells scored by tautology. It is now
  a covariate instead, which is what makes the remaining increments mean anything.
