     of the head's execution against the DP's intention).  `misfire` =

     WHAT THIS WINDOW CAN AND CANNOT SAY ABOUT THE OPEN BIT. An arm that never
     committed L4 is anchored on its L2 commit, and in `fb_s0` the open arms are
     BIT-IDENTICAL to their frozen twins through c31 — the operative table first

     that ends at c31 (L2@c12 + 20) is entirely inside the identical stretch. Rows

     open bit. Read the per-slot b(s) series instead, and see the note in [C].
