**Prompt**: [`ideas/sparse_one_rung_up.md`](../../../../ideas/sparse_one_rung_up.md) §4 and §8. Track A climbed
with no introspective organ because the outer loop reads the *output face* of the learner's trajectory — a count,
over its own solved pieces, of the next level's raw material — and RHM supplied the reader that parses it for
free. §8 asked whether the count still paces the crank when the reader is the learner's own climbing perception.
The code says what "for free" is: the miner keys tables by flat leaf tuples and segments solved pieces on the
grammar's **grid** — spans of `2^ℓ` at multiples of `2^ℓ`, aligned because the piece is a whole sequence — and the
endo read's positions depend only on `(era.level, era.node, s, depth)`. `frontier/` showed a next-token reader
recovers that grid from its own entropy period without knowing the phase. So the question became concrete: does
`at_support` survive having its grid recovered rather than given?
**Attribution**: the question, its reduction to the grid, and the three-part design are the orchestrator's; the
pure recorder and its bit-identity gates, the reader window change, the feature-keyed second pass and its gate,
the leaf-token control that turned out to matter, the per-level decomposition of grid errors, the own-production
reader, and the dead zones re-measured per series are the implementer's (one Opus session); the call to run it
now, in parallel, and the reading that the own-production reader's overfit may be the point rather than the
defect are Jasper's and the implementer's respectively, both recorded in discussion.

## Goal

1. **Gauge fidelity.** Recover the grid from a next-token reader's entropy on windows in which each mined piece
   sits at an offset the reader is not told; recompute `at_support` on it, in the thermostat's own coordinates;
   compare with the exact count cycle by cycle and era by era.
2. **The reader's climb against the gauge's reach.** At which eras does the recovered grid resolve the level
   above the era's, as a function of the reader's checkpoint, for a reader of the grammar's corpus and a reader
   of the learner's own productions?
3. **Offline replay.** `conductor`'s yield thermostat, with its dead zones, replayed on the endogenous series
   yoked cycle for cycle to the realised run: where and by how much would the decisions have diverged?

## Instruments

- **The recorder** ([`record.py`](record.py)). `cd_s0` saved miner state but not the solved configurations, so
  `conductor`'s `outer_yield` arm was re-run at `cd_s0`'s literals with a pure recorder wrapping
  `macros.parse_features` and `finetune_generator` — copies of tensors already in flight, no RNG, no allocation,
  no gradient; `conductor.py` and its imports untouched. **Gates**: F-1 the recorded arm is bit-identical to the
  banked run on 13 series (max |Δ| 0.000e+00); F-2 the realised loop actions are equal; F-3 recorded mine rows
  equal the run's own `n_mined` every cycle. 139 cycles, 1093 mined pieces, 37,271 solved beam tips; 2163 s,
  0.60 GPU-h.
- **The readers** ([`reader.py`](reader.py)). `train_trajectory`'s recipe line for line (8L/8H/256D, AdamW 3e−4,
  batch 64, fresh data per chunk, the same checkpoint ladder) with one change: the window is a parameter, 128
  tokens (two periods), because the parent hard-wires it to `s^depth` = 64, one period, which gives the detector
  one column per candidate offset at `k = 6`. `corpus`: the practice grammar's stream (`v8 s2 L6 m2`,
  `generate_rules_distinct(seed=0)`), 32k steps, re-read ×4.1. `own`: the learner's solved beam tips concatenated,
  16k steps, re-read ×54.9 on 2.4M tokens.
- **The detector.** `frontier/common.py::nested_phase` in its template-free `meanprof` mode (the template mode
  needs the observer family's entropy templates on this grammar, a new oracle computation), so every reach
  reported here is a lower bound; `frontier/` measured `meanprof` 0.00–0.09 below the template.
- **Presentation and keying** ([`evaluate.py`](evaluate.py)). Each mined piece sits at slot 1 of a four-piece
  stream of the learner's own productions, in a 129-token window cut at a uniform offset in 1…64; one window per
  piece, fixed by seed and identical across every checkpoint and arm, so the endogenous miner sees what the real
  miner saw, once. The first pass keyed by leaf tokens; that control matters and is reported (§1). The
  load-bearing pass keys by level-1 features as the thermostat does: the detector recovers the `k = 1` block grid
  at 1.000 from step 250, so the recorded parse shifted to the recovered grid *is* the parse at that grid, and
  pieces whose recovered block parity is wrong are counted and dropped (0 at every checkpoint ≥ 1000). **Gate
  P-1**: the feature recomputation on the true grid equals the run's own `obs_hist` exactly, every level, every
  cycle.
- **The series.** `A`: the run's own `obs_hist`, what the thermostat read. `Bf`: `A` recomputed on this window
  set (identity check). `Cf`: the endogenous gauge. `Df`: a shuffled offset, a wrong grid at the right period.
  `B / C / D`: the same three keyed by leaf tokens. Floors: the random-init reader and `Df`.
- **Grid quality.** Every recovered span decomposed as *exact* (the era's own span), *right level, wrong node*
  (a genuine constituent of that level at another node), or *off grid*. The exact column is the `k = 6` boundary
  accuracy by construction.
- **The replay** ([`replay.py`](replay.py)). `conductor`'s rule on each series, yoked to the realised era
  timeline (an early advance is recorded as having left and takes no further action until the next realised
  boundary — `floors.py::replay_rule`'s scope). **Gate R-1**: on the exact series it reproduces the realised
  chosen actions exactly. Dead zones re-measured on each series by the same null-ABBA method.

## 1. Gauge fidelity: the count survives the recovered grid, in level and not only in shape

The read the thermostat consumes, `at_support` at the read level, per era, mean → final:

| era (read level) | `A` exact | `Cf` corpus reader @32k | `Cf` own-production reader @16k | `Df` shuffled grid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (L3) | 28.6 → 50 | 28.8 → 62 | 28.4 → 52 | 2.1 → 6 |
| 2 (L4) | 40.2 → 66 | 23.0 → 40 | 36.0 → 60 | 0.3 → 1 |
| 3 (L4) | 73.0 → 79 | 47.6 → 53 | 66.5 → 71 | 1.1 → 2 |
| 4 (L4) | 81.1 → 83 | 56.7 → 60 | 72.8 → 73 | 2.4 → 3 |
| 5 (L4) | 85.3 → 87 | 62.4 → 64 | 74.8 → 77 | 3.0 → 3 |

Over all 139 cycles against `A`: corpus reader @32k Pearson 0.983 / 0.992 and Spearman 0.996 / 0.992 at L3 / L4;
own-production reader @16k **0.997 / 0.999** and 0.996 / **1.000**; shuffled grid 0.771 / 0.826 and 0.994 / 0.839;
random-init reader 0.513 / – and 0.759 / –. `Bf` equals `A` at 1.000, so the windowing costs nothing. The bias has
a sign per level: at L3 the endogenous count *over*counts (right-level-wrong-node observations add genuine L3
tuples from other nodes), at L4 it *under*counts (a wrong node at L4 pushes the span out of the piece).

**The leaf-token control.** Keyed by leaf tokens on the *true* grid, the L4 count reads a mean of 5.8 against the
thermostat's 34.5: the keying, not the grid, is what kills the L4 read in that form. The feature-keyed pass is the
one the thermostat's coordinates require and the one every number above is in.

## 3. The reader's climb against the gauge's reach

Nested period accuracy on the mined-piece windows (chance 0.5 / 0.25 / 0.125 / 0.063 / 0.031 / 0.016):

| reader, step | k=1 | k=2 | k=3 | k=4 | k=5 | k=6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| corpus, 250 | 1.00 | 0.92 | 0.52 | 0.25 | 0.10 | 0.05 |
| corpus, 1 000 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.97 | 0.59 | 0.21 | 0.08 |
| corpus, 2 000 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.93 | 0.37 | 0.15 |
| corpus, 8 000 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.99 | 0.57 | 0.30 |
| corpus, 32 000 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.99 | 0.70 | **0.37** |
| own, 2 000 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.90 | 0.72 | 0.34 |
| own, 8 000 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.97 | 0.88 | 0.86 | 0.86 |
| own, 16 000 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.97 | 0.90 | 0.89 | **0.89** |

The level above the era's level is resolved *as a level* at era 1 from step 1000, era 2 from 2000, era 3 from
4–8k, and never fully at eras 4–5 for the corpus reader (aligned 0.75, 0.36); the *node* is the binding constraint
and sits at 0.35–0.46. The own-production reader resolves both to 0.82–0.91 by 8–16k, and its price is visible on
the grammar venue: its held-out loss on the corpus rises from 0.89 at 2k to 2.61 at 16k while its loss on the
practice pieces keeps falling, and its period on corpus windows **peaks at step 2000 and goes backwards**
(`k = 3` 1.00 → 0.84, `k = 4` 0.86 → 0.54, `k = 5` 0.61 → 0.38, `k = 6` 0.29 → 0.20) — `frontier/` Q2's wall
signature, at a re-read factor of 55. Its grid comes from recognising its own pieces, not from having absorbed the
grammar.

## 4. The replay: within a cycle on the own-production reader, and why the corpus reader loses a commit

Realised chosen actions: `commit L2 @ 49`, `advance @ 56`, `commit L3 @ 92`, `advance @ 130`. The rule on `A`
and on `Bf` reproduces all four exactly.

| driving series | replayed actions | Δ cycles vs exact |
|---|---|---|
| own-production reader @16k | commit L2 @ 49, advance @ 55, commit L3 @ 91, advance @ 121 | **0, −1, −1, −9** |
| own-production reader @8k | commit L2 @ 50, commit L3 @ 90, advance @ 102, advance @ 120 | +1, −2, era-1 advance missed, −10 |
| corpus reader @2k | commit L2 @ 38, advance @ 51, commit L3 @ 97, advance @ 115 | −11, −5, +5, −15 |
| corpus reader @32k | commit L3 @ 63, advance @ 74 | **L2 commit never fires**, −29, +18 |
| shuffled grid | none | never quiets inside the caps |
| random-init reader | none | – |

Divergence is not monotone in reader quality for the corpus reader: as `k = 3` and `k = 4` sharpen while `k = 6`
stays near 0.35, more observations land right-level-wrong-node, the L3 count inflates and never quiets inside era
1, so the L2 commit is lost. **Dead zones** re-measured on each series by the same null-ABBA method: on the exact
series L3 0.489 / L4 0.464 against the governing `cd_ef` values 0.461 / 0.517 (the method checking itself to 6% /
10%); the feature-keyed endogenous series' floors are the same order (L3 0.35–0.53, L4 0.30–0.47); the shuffled
series' floor is 3–5× smaller (0.148 / 0.092), and at its *own* floor the shuffled grid does act (commit @ 14,
advance @ 31, commit @ 98) — so "the count survives a wrong grid" is ruled out at the governing dead zone but not
at a dead zone re-measured on the wrong grid.

> **The output-face counter has an affirmative instance without the exact reader, with a sharp condition. RHM's
> gift is the grid, and a next-token reader's entropy period buys most of it back for free — but it buys two
> things at two prices. The level grid is cheap, at ceiling by 2k steps of an ordinary reader. The node needs the
> depth-6 boundary, where a reader of the world's grammar tops out at 0.37. What closes that gap is the learner's
> own stream: a reader of its own productions reaches 0.87 and drives the thermostat to within one cycle of both
> commits — and it is, by `frontier/`'s own diagnostic, overfit to those productions.**

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes** (one practice arm, one grammar, one seed; the reader recipe of the parent trajectory with a
two-period window; the template-free detector, so every reach is a lower bound):

- `at_support` recomputed in the thermostat's coordinates on a grid recovered from a next-token reader's own
  entropy tracks the exact series at Pearson 0.98–0.99 (corpus reader) and 0.997–0.999 (own-production reader)
  over 139 cycles, clears the shuffled-grid and random-init floors in level and not only in shape, and its biases
  have a mechanism per level.
- The recovered grid is right-level at L2–L4 with essentially nothing off grid; the node is the binding
  constraint, pinned at the `k = 6` boundary accuracy: 0.37 for a reader of the grammar, 0.87 for a reader of the
  learner's own productions.
- `conductor`'s rule replayed on the own-production reader's series lands within one cycle of both commits and
  the first advance; on the corpus reader's series the L2 commit is lost to right-level-wrong-node inflation.
- The own-production reader shows the corpus wall's signature on the grammar venue while it resolves the
  learner's own pieces: the organ that makes the output face readable on a stream is one `frontier/`'s diagnostic
  calls overfit.

**Does not establish:**

- **A causal reader.** The own-production reader was trained on the pooled productions of the whole run, so at
  cycle `c` it has seen productions from cycles after `c`, and its corpus is a superset of the very pieces it is
  asked to locate. Its Q2 and Q3 numbers are inflated by that; the corpus reader has neither problem. The honest
  form is a reader trained online as the productions arrive.
- **A live loop.** Every replay here is yoked to the realised timeline; an action changes everything downstream.
  The endogenous read is nearly free per cycle (one forward pass of the reader over ≤ 8 windows plus a numpy
  detector, under 10 ms against 10.4 s per cycle); the cost is the reader. One live arm with a grammar reader is
  ≈ 1.3 GPU-h; with an online own-production reader ≈ +0.4–0.7 GPU-h; a minimal round (one endogenous arm, its
  yoked-clock control, a shuffled-grid control, against the banked `outer_yield`) ≈ 2.5–3.5 GPU-h, three
  containers, one wave.
- The template detector's reach on this grammar, or the reach at levels 5–6 for any reader of the corpus.
- Transfer off the RHM grid: the period is this substrate's fixed constituent length. The generic form of the
  same instrument is segmentation at entropy peaks, of which the period detector is the special case.

## Gotchas worth not rediscovering

- **Key by what the thermostat keys by.** Leaf-token keying on the true grid reads L4 at a sixth of the feature-
  keyed count; a grid experiment run in the wrong coordinates reads the keying's failure as the grid's.
- **`train_trajectory` hard-wires the window to one period.** A detector needs at least two; make the window a
  parameter and leave everything else in the recipe alone.
- **A reader of the learner's own productions is a re-read reader.** Report its held-out loss and its period on
  the grammar venue beside its reach on the pieces, or the wall goes unnoticed.
- **Pool nothing across cycles a live learner could not have seen.** The pooled own-production reader is a
  ceiling, not a learner; say so in the table caption.
- **Record by wrapping, never by editing.** A recorder that copies tensors already in flight and touches no RNG
  can be gated bit-identical to the banked run; anything else cannot.
