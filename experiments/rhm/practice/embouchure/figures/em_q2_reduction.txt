(2) GAUGE vs YOKE — the mandatory timing control (census finding 4)
==============================================================================
  The yoke replays the gauge arm's realised action cycles by clock and reads
  nothing. A divergence would mean the reads are not inert; identity means the
  criterion contributed nothing beyond a cycle number, which is a finding, and the
  priced-time column is then what the read cost for a trajectory a clock reproduces.
  [tacet] In this tag the yokes ARE the treatments (each carries one gate knob), so
  a non-zero delta below is the round's variable and `first divergence` reads as
  WHEN THE GATE FIRST BOUND. What must still hold is commits/advances EQUAL — that
  is what makes the arms lifetime- and pacing-identical.
  (no yoked arms in this tag)
