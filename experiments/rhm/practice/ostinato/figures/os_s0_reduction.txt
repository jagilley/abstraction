(5) THE LEDGER — what each arm read, and what it cost
==============================================================================
  ear's convention: the learner's own experience is free, a counterfactual read is
  priced, and instruments logged in every arm are free. Only the POLICY's input

==============================================================================
(R1) THE RUNG, MEASURED — how much repetition the benchmark actually got
==============================================================================
  rho = `perf_bench_frac`: the fraction of each execution's rows b(s) integrates.
  Every row still receives a delta and still carries credit; only the BENCHMARK's
  own evidence is thinned, so total volume, difficulty, the diet and the EWMA's
  update INSTANTS (hence its staleness) are identical across rungs and only its
  sampling jitter moves. alpha = 0.05 -> an EWMA read averages ~39
  updates, so eff_n = (benched rows/update) x that, and SE(b) = sd(e)/sqrt(eff_n).
  `|b-e|` is the arm's own mean ungated signal (sum_bme / rows) — the thing SE(b)
  has to be small against for the benchmark to be carrying anything.
