# RHM: A History

RHM (the Random Hierarchy Model, Cagnetta & Wyart 2024) entered this repo as a fix for a specific frustration in the natural-language work: vocab reduction there turned out to be a channel intervention (moves β, not γ), and the researchers couldn't find a clean lever on the actual data-generating process. RHM supplies one — a hierarchy of composition rules with known ground truth at every level — and nearly every RHM thread since has been either "use the known ground truth to build a sharper instrument" or "use the known ground truth to build a genuine control task." Reading the ~seven weeks of work below (2026-06-20 → present) less as a list of experiments and more as a sequence of belief revisions, four throughlines dominate.

## Throughlines

**1. Every scalar instrument this program builds eventually turns out to be measuring something narrower than advertised, and each correction is a real prior update, not a bug fix.** Residual *rank* was first read as tracking DGP complexity, then shown to be dominated by FM-capacity/head-mismatch artifacts ([RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md](RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md)); its replacement, β + `R_res_participation`, explained that failure and two others ([residual_decomposition/README.md](residual_decomposition/README.md)) — but a later trajectory re-measurement found β's own capacity-invariance holds only in the narrow regime it was validated in, so even the fix needed re-scoping, not just adoption ([residual_decomposition/trajectory/README.md](residual_decomposition/trajectory/README.md)). The confabulation thread's capacity guard caught an oversized forward model *manufacturing* a fake positive result, not just noise ([confabulation/README.md](confabulation/README.md)). The directed-sculpting "reducibility" tap was inverted on its first pass and had to be rebuilt from a flow estimate into a floor-corrected stock ([full_loop/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md)), and its own drift-advantage numbers were later found inflated by two more instrument biases that each happened to favor the hoped-for result. Belief PR — the metric that opened directed sculpting's expansion story — was independently retired as a ranker by two sibling experiments once it was shown to anti-correlate with target quality ([endo_expansion](directed_sculpting/full_loop/endo_expansion/README.md), [endogenous_expansion](directed_sculpting/full_loop/endogenous_expansion/README.md)). The lesson accreting across all of this: distrust any single scalar until it's been cross-checked against a closed form or an orthogonal instrument, and expect the check to *change*, not just confirm, the number.

**2. "Does a self-model / ratchet loop compound?" is asked over and over, in progressively better-controlled forms, and the answer keeps coming back "only under specific, narrow conditions" rather than a clean yes or no.** The first, most direct version — port MNIST's gated ratchet onto RHM — is decisively negative: every *dynamic* reproduces, the *magnitude* never does, and the FOMAML diagnostic pins the reason on data stationarity itself: on a fixed corpus, the outer loop is identical to the inner loop, so no bilevel objective can manufacture a compositional-transfer signal ([ratchet/README.md](ratchet/README.md)). That result reframed the question from "what's wrong with the ratchet" to "what would a moving frontier even look like on RHM" — which is what the active-query, edit-control, sculpting, and eventually directed-sculpting lines all separately went and built. But building a genuinely moving frontier didn't automatically produce compounding either: `RHM_SCULPT_CONTINUAL_README.md` found one-shot internalization sets a large ceiling and continual re-internalization adds almost nothing on a fixed task, and directed-sculpting's own drift manipulation turned out to be *orthogonal* to expansion — what mattered was the grader's type, not whether the world moved at all (see throughline 3). The frontier has to be moving **and** graded correctly, and even then the "climb" result only appeared once an artificial scarcity (samples-per-event) was imposed to stop the model from getting invariance for free.

**3. The single most load-bearing distinction the program has converged on, discovered independently by at least four unrelated threads, is what *kind* of grading or target signal drives real capability expansion versus an illusion of it.** Label smoothing and focal loss first ruled out an easy villain (objective-induced sharpness bias isn't the cause of the composition-depth ceiling; it's a real capacity constraint) but showed gradient *reallocation* — not reweighting the same diluted signal — improves FM legibility ([LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md](LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md), [LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md](LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md)). Sleep-chunking and the latent loop then found that a *latent* target (not a token target) is what makes self-knowledge generalizable at all on RHM, at any depth or loop strength ([SLEEP_CHUNKING_RHM_README.md](SLEEP_CHUNKING_RHM_README.md), [RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md](RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md)). The specialization thread found that *reweighting* toward deep tokens actively harms performance while a *direct* oracle target for the deep feature breaks the depth stall outright — reweighting a diluted signal is not equivalent to supplying a direct one ([specialization/README.md](specialization/README.md)). Directed sculpting's biggest single finding is that a grounded, evaluative grader expands the belief's effective dimension while a dense, endogenous grader caps *below* the no-loop floor — a difference in grader type, not in whether the underlying world drifts ([full_loop/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md)). And minting converges on the same axis from a different direction: a verifier that certifies legality (support) filters out harm but a *density* oracle is what's actually missing for real capability gain — "the rule table is a support oracle, not a density oracle" ([minting/README.md](minting/README.md)). Four independent lines, one crystallizing belief: correctness/support is necessary but not sufficient; the grading signal has to carry the right kind of density/directness to produce real (not illusory) expansion.

**4. `m` (synonymic multiplicity), not `L` (depth), is the recurring bottleneck — discovered in the very first scaling sweep and rediscovered, sharpened, and exploited by nearly every later thread.** The original hyperparameter sweep found m dominates the scaling exponent 3:1 over L ([README.md](README.md#results)). Per-level loss decomposition explained why: learning is bottom-up and a fixed-capacity model can only compose 1–2 levels before m saturates it ([PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md](PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md)). `RHM_FRONTIER_AND_LEGIBILITY_README.md` then found it's `m` specifically — not tuple-space occupancy — that gates whether NTP ever *learns* the root, and dropping to m=2 opened the first substrate where a forward model's residual becomes hierarchy-legible, retroactively explaining every earlier RHM self-knowledge null as having run on a substrate with nothing to amplify. `PER_LEVEL_EXPANSION_README.md` independently re-confirmed the same m-gates-the-ceiling finding with a >200× capacity sweep. Nearly every subsequent thread (latent loop, sculpting, confabulation, active-query, directed sculpting) deliberately sits on the m=2/m=4 substrate this arc identified as the sweet spot.

## The arc, in order

**Setup and first instruments (06-20 → 06-23).** The scaling sweep establishes m as the bottleneck ([README.md](README.md)); residual-rank experiments build the program's first forward-model instrument, later shown to be measuring FM-capacity artifacts rather than DGP complexity ([RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md](RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md)); regime-transition and cosine sweeps locate the L=6/m=4 substrate where residual structure first becomes observable and establish it as the default ([REGIME_TRANSITION_README.md](REGIME_TRANSITION_README.md)); per-level loss decomposition gives the mechanistic bottom-up-learning picture behind all of it ([PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md](PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md)). Label smoothing, focal loss, and FM-surprise-weighted NTP probe whether the objective itself causes the depth ceiling — it doesn't (capacity does) — but gradient reallocation measurably improves FM legibility ([LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md](LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md), [LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md](LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md)).

**The ratchet arc, concluded negative (06-23 → 06-29).** A systematic attempt to reproduce MNIST's compounding self-model ratchet on RHM rules out capacity, task structure (RL), the distillation bottleneck, and FM-cosine regime one at a time, and the FOMAML diagnostic settles the mechanism: stationary data structurally cannot support compounding ([ratchet/README.md](ratchet/README.md)). This is the pivot that redirects the whole program toward building genuine non-stationary/control tasks.

**Legibility, regularization, and complexodynamics (06-29 → 07-07).** `RHM_FRONTIER_AND_LEGIBILITY_README.md` finds the m=2 substrate and the amplifier-not-source relationship between the self-model loop and the base objective. `RHM_FM_REGULARIZER_README.md` shows structured FM-predictability pressure compresses functional complexity below weight decay's floor. `RHM_COMPLEXODYNAMICS_README.md` reads the saved trajectories as confirming Aaronson's rise-then-fall complexity law, with two amendments: the descent needs an annealer (SGD alone arrests mid-descent, "a glass, not a liquid") and the floor is the DGP's own sophistication, not zero.

**Active RHM and the limits of planning without a control task (07-10 → 07-11).** [ACTIVE_RHM_README.md](ACTIVE_RHM_README.md) finds the arity impossibility from the sibling reaching experiments ports, but usability needs a value-of-information target, not a mean-transition forward model — and even fixed, internalizing the forward model has no headroom, because active querying is inference in disguise (acting already builds the plannable representation: act ≈ plan). This motivates building a genuine act≠plan task.

**Sculpting is born (07-11 → 07-19).** `RHM_EDIT_CONTROL_README.md` builds that control task (edit toward a target root) and hits a second obstacle — the belief-judge is gamed by off-manifold intermediates — fixed with a generator plus a cerebellar self-consistency veto, a pattern reused throughout everything downstream. `sculpting_control_task.md` validates a real DP-optimal lookahead prize before any learning is attempted, licensing `RHM_SCULPTING_README.md`'s learned beams. `RHM_SCULPT_CONTINUAL_README.md` finds internalization compounds once, hard, then exhausts on a fixed task. In parallel, `SLEEP_CHUNKING_RHM_README.md` and `RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md` establish the latent-vs-token target distinction and land the program's first generalizable self-knowledge result on RHM. Also in parallel, the specialization thread finds that breadth/reweighting can't break the depth stall but a direct oracle target can, and reframes "broad beats narrow" as evidence that shared deep structure is what makes generalization possible at all — pointing toward an intrinsic learning-progress drive ([specialization/README.md](specialization/README.md)).

**Confabulation (07-22).** The self-report/self-theory question gets made operational via the FM residual: a report head shows a first-person advantage only on the implementation target, never on behavioral/entity/world controls, and the loop turns out not to be necessary for it — closing-the-loop changes how well a model knows itself, not what there is to know ([confabulation/README.md](confabulation/README.md)).

**Residual decomposition supersedes rank (07-26 → 07-27, re-checked 08-02).** β and `R_res_participation` replace naive rank as the instrument of record, explaining three separate historical rank negatives as a saturated-FM noise floor — and a later trajectory re-measurement both strengthens the qualitative rise-then-fall story and forces a more conservative scoping of when β itself can be trusted ([residual_decomposition/README.md](residual_decomposition/README.md)).

**Directed sculpting — the current main line (07-28 → present).** Porting the sibling `mjc` experiment's active-curriculum loop onto RHM breaks immediately (RHM's rule usage is too uniform to support the port's relevance signal) and forces three new primitives — distractor channels, support-fixed drift, a repair-cost instrument — each validated only after catching an instrument bias that would have produced a confident wrong answer ([directed_sculpting/README.md](directed_sculpting/README.md)). The `full_loop` node then finds the reward-free relevance signal genuinely ports (recovering 76% of an oracle's advantage) and lands the arc's central discovery: expansion depends on the grader's *type* (grounded/evaluative expands; endogenous/dense caps below the no-loop floor), not on whether the world drifts — drift turns out to be orthogonal ([full_loop/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md)). A real climbing-axis positive only appears once samples-per-event are artificially scarce, removing the "invariance for free" that let a static-substrate model skip deep composition. The newest children (08-01 → 08-03) each chase one loose end: `level_moves` confirms the value's level-preference is real but suboptimal; `metering_sweep` vindicates the falsifier once compute is properly controlled and finds the relevance tap ~1200× cheaper than the reducibility tap; `endo_expansion` and `endogenous_expansion` are independently-built parallel attacks (not sequential iterations) that agree an endogenous grader does expand, just at reduced fidelity; `partial_hetero`/`shared_surface` report an honestly-flat, noise-floor-bounded transfer curve rather than overclaiming a null.

**Minting — a sibling thread on the same question (07-29 → 07-31).** Running in parallel to directed sculpting, minting asks whether self-generated data filtered by a grader can expand a narrowly-seeded learner. An apparent mirror-vs-non-mirror collapse gets retracted as ordinary overfitting; the real axis turns out to be legality-vs-location; two failure "doses" bracket the design space; and the eventual finding — a rule-table verifier is a support oracle, not a density oracle, so it prevents harm but doesn't drive gain — is explicitly framed (in [ideas/breadth_as_grader_heterogeneity.md](../../ideas/breadth_as_grader_heterogeneity.md)) as the sibling of directed sculpting's satiety/dilettantism failure mode: minting escapes one extreme, verification escapes the other ([minting/README.md](minting/README.md)).

## Where the program stands now

Both active threads — directed sculpting and minting — are converging from opposite directions on throughline 3: correctness/support alone doesn't produce real capability expansion; the grading signal needs to carry directness or density, and its *type* matters more than the non-stationarity of the world it's applied to. The instrumentation throughline (1) suggests the next scalar this program builds should be pre-registered against a closed form wherever one exists, given how often ad hoc metrics here have quietly measured the wrong thing. The compounding question (2) remains open in its strongest form: no thread has yet shown genuine multi-cycle compounding on RHM, only single-pass expansion or exhaustion-after-one-shot.
