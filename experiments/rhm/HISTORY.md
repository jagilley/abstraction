# History: experiments/rhm

*A "big picture" pass over experiments/rhm's history — the shifts in thinking, not the day-to-day. Written 2026-08-05. Cites paths to `README.md`s for depth; treat this doc as a map, not the territory.*

RHM (Cagnetta & Wyart's Random Hierarchy Model) was adopted as a synthetic substrate with a known, controllable data-generating process — a way to ask questions that natural-language experiments (`experiments/language_reduction/`) couldn't cleanly answer, because we can't independently vary depth, per-level ambiguity, and vocabulary in real text. See [README.md](README.md) for the model definition and current default hparams (L=6, m=4, v=8, s=2 — the "sweet spot" this whole history converges on early and never leaves).

## Era 1 — Instrument-building: scaling, residual rank, per-level loss (06-20 → 06-23)

The opening sweep found **m dominates L 3:1** in setting the scaling exponent ([SWEEP_README.md](SWEEP_README.md)) — the first sign that per-level *ambiguity*, not raw depth, is the resource models struggle against. The FM-residual instrument was built and calibrated here ([RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md](RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md), [REGIME_TRANSITION_README.md](REGIME_TRANSITION_README.md)), alongside the per-level loss decomposition ([PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md](PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md)) that became the standard readout for the rest of the arc: models learn RHM strictly bottom-up, and a capacity-bounded forward model's predictability of a layer tracks how well that hierarchy level has been learned. Loss-shaping experiments ([LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md](LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md), [LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md](LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md)) then settled a durable belief: the composition-depth ceiling is a **capacity constraint, not an NTP objective artifact** — smoothing hurts every level uniformly, while position-reweighting (focal loss, FM-surprise weighting) reallocates gradient without cost, foreshadowing every later attempt to build a "smarter" training signal.

## Era 2 — The ratchet arc: a clean negative, and a prior handed forward (06-23 → 06-29)

An MNIST result — a wake-sleep self-knowledge loop compounding 34%→48% over 4 cycles — was tested against RHM's known DGP. Seven successive hypotheses (wake-sleep at m=4 and m=2, a sparsity sweep, sparse ratchet, RL/gen-distillation, a FOMAML diagnostic, and meta-learning across rule-set diversity — indexed in [ratchet/README.md](ratchet/README.md)) each ruled out one candidate explanation — capacity, task richness, distillation form, FM-cosine regime, and finally true bilevel optimization itself. The FOMAML diagnostic ([ratchet/RHM_FOMAML_README.md](ratchet/RHM_FOMAML_README.md)) settled it: **no outer objective makes FM-predictability pressure improve compositional NTP on stationary data.** The one real dynamic recovered (RL+FM, [ratchet/RHM_RL_RATCHET_README.md](ratchet/RHM_RL_RATCHET_README.md)) still didn't compound. What this arc left behind: the belief that the MNIST mechanism is a domain-general training procedure. What it handed forward, stated explicitly in [README.md](README.md#ratchet--meta-learning-arc--concluded-negative-moved-to-ratchetreadmemd): **compounding requires a moving frontier that stationary RHM can't supply** — the question every later line of work is an answer to.

## Era 3 — Chasing the moving frontier (06-30 → 07-18)

A sequence of doc-sized experiments searched for that frontier on three axes. (1) *Target type*: [RHM_FRONTIER_AND_LEGIBILITY_README.md](RHM_FRONTIER_AND_LEGIBILITY_README.md) found m gates depth of learning and predicted the closed loop would finally compound on the easier m=2 substrate; [RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md](RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md) falsified that directly — even at m=2 a **token**-target loop still inverts self-knowledge, and only an oracle-privileged **latent** target produces real, transferable self-knowledge (a pure meta-knowledge effect; RHM's shared grammar makes object-level knowledge untransferable, unlike MNIST). [SLEEP_CHUNKING_RHM_README.md](SLEEP_CHUNKING_RHM_README.md) supplied the mechanism: token objectives dilute exponentially with depth, latent targets don't. (2) *Task type*: [ACTIVE_RHM_README.md](ACTIVE_RHM_README.md) found active *querying* is "inference in disguise" — a competent controller already saturates the plannable signal, so internalizing a forward model has no headroom — but its own next step scoped this to querying specifically. Building a genuine *editing* task ([sculpting_control_task.md](sculpting_control_task.md), [RHM_EDIT_CONTROL_README.md](RHM_EDIT_CONTROL_README.md)) reversed the null and surfaced a second problem (belief success gameable off-manifold), fixed with a cerebellar self-consistency veto. The resulting sculpting arc ([RHM_SCULPTING_README.md](RHM_SCULPTING_README.md)) is the era's centerpiece: latent planning starts as a merely-efficient surrogate for token planning, is shown to genuinely *beat* it under lossy channels, and — after internalizing the planning loop — beats it even on the clean channel, the first real internalization win on RHM and a direct (partial) reversal of ACTIVE_RHM's pessimism. (3) *Allocation*: iterating this loop on a **fixed** task ([RHM_SCULPT_CONTINUAL_README.md](RHM_SCULPT_CONTINUAL_README.md)) found a **weak ratchet** — value iteration compounds, belief-internalization is a one-shot ceiling-setter, and the frontier saturates after one pass, reproducing Era 2's plateau on the planning side. [specialization/README.md](specialization/README.md) found breadth-restriction and level-reweighting are inert-to-harmful — RHM's single shared grammar has no domain-specific structure to specialize on — and reframed the whole era's null as: what's missing is an *intrinsic learning-progress drive* over a genuinely non-stationary task. That reframing, plus sculpt-continual's explicit call for "a deeper L or fresh rule set" to reopen the ratchet, is what [directed_sculpting/README.md](directed_sculpting/README.md) (Era 7) was built to answer.

## Era 4 — Regularization and complexodynamics (07-01, 07-07)

Two results already carried in full in [README.md](README.md): FM-predictability pressure compresses functional complexity below weight decay's floor ([RHM_FM_REGULARIZER_README.md](RHM_FM_REGULARIZER_README.md)) — **self-knowledge is not load-bearing for functional simplification**, the open-loop pressure alone suffices — and the "First Law of Complexodynamics" rise-and-fall is confirmed empirically with two amendments: the descent needs an annealer (SGD alone freezes mid-descent), and it bottoms out at the DGP's own sophistication, not zero ([RHM_COMPLEXODYNAMICS_README.md](RHM_COMPLEXODYNAMICS_README.md)).

## Era 5 — The confabulation test (07-22)

Operationalized the Nisbett & Wilson worry (a self-report may reflect a learned self-*theory*, not self-*access*) via the FM decomposition: `FM(a_i)` is everything a theory could produce, so genuine access lives in the residual. Found the predicted dissociation — a report head on the residual beats a capacity-matched third-party observer only on the implementation target, nowhere else — and two pre-registrations failed usefully: the loop is *not necessary* for the effect (just weaker without it), and closing it changes *how well* the model knows itself, not *what there is to know*. Full account: [confabulation/README.md](confabulation/README.md).

## Era 6 — Residual decomposition retires the rank instrument (07-27)

Found `res_var ∝ act_var^β` across three domains (RHM, language, MNIST) and showed naive residual rank was, in every prior "surprising" negative, secretly reading a **saturated FM's noise floor** rather than DGP complexity — falsifying the `R_act ≈ R_comp + R_res` partition in [beliefs/dimensionality_expansion.md](../../beliefs/dimensionality_expansion.md). Replaced the single rank number with two: β (shape) and `R_res_participation` (frontier dimensionality in the model's own basis). Full account: [residual_decomposition/README.md](residual_decomposition/README.md).

## Era 7 — Directed sculpting: manufacturing the moving frontier (07-28)

Ported an mjc experiment (E3: an inner/outer loop with a forward-model-driven relevance signal over what to train on) onto RHM. The naive port failed immediately — RHM's rule usage is uniform by construction, so there's no allocation signal to exploit, exactly as Era 3's specialization result predicted. Three fixes built the substrate ([directed_sculpting/README.md](directed_sculpting/README.md)): distractor channels, support-fixed drift (encoding, not content, drifts — the DP optimum provably can't move), and a repair-cost instrument that survived two of its own traps (raw CE is *exactly* blind to this drift; the naive replacement metric is confounded by ordinary continued training) before cross-validating against the closed-form KL to 88–95%. On this substrate, [directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md) found the reward-free relevance signal is a smoking gun — it separates real tokens from distractors 13–18× and alone recovers 76% of an oracle's advantage — but **belief expansion is a property of the grader's type, not of non-stationarity**: a grounded evaluative grader expands the belief ~1.7×/~15× (dimension/control); an endogenous dense grader caps *below* the no-loop floor, identically whether the world drifts or not. Two both-negative drift predictions were later shown to be *scoped* rather than false: surface re-fit was repairing 91% of each drift event for free, and starving samples-per-event (not drift magnitude) makes the depth advantage migrate from surface to deep — the arc's first positive on the climbing axis, read as "invariance ≠ necessity." [directed_sculpting/full_loop/metering_sweep/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/metering_sweep/README.md) then ran the idea doc's own pre-registered falsifier and got a real but wrong-signed result — the expansion prize *rises* with collection budget — before a compute-controlled re-run reversed the sign entirely: at matched compute the prize is humped and turns over at the abundant end. **The instrument's first answer was a compute confound, not the claim.** [directed_sculpting/full_loop/partial_hetero/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/partial_hetero/README.md) swept sharing-depth between fully-independent and fully-shared channel geometries expecting a monotonic falloff and got a flat line — diagnosed not as a null about the geometry but as a readout blind above the level where channels share any alphabet; a child node ([shared_surface/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/partial_hetero/shared_surface/README.md)) fixed the vocabulary gap but exposed a second, more fundamental limit — a ±0.012 measurement floor larger than any effect either sweep could report — so both sweeps' flatness is currently *uninformative*, not negative.

## Era 8 — Minting: a support oracle is not a density oracle (07-29 → 07-31)

A separate line: seed a learner narrowly, let it mint its own sequences, accept some via a grader, retrain on the mix. Two setup failures were informative in themselves — the idea doc's prescribed regime overfits catastrophically, and at high minted-data dose *every* arm degrades equally including a real-data control, proving the harm mechanism is gradient-concentration, not autophagy. At the corrected regime, verified minting beats random and mirror-replay, but the entire achievable range sits within noise of a no-minting control, while equal-volume real data clears it by 18×. The mechanism, and the era's headline belief: **the exact rule table is a support oracle, not a density oracle** — it can delete illegal (out-of-support) continuations but can't correct in-support density error, which is what NTP actually needs. An earlier read of the "mirror hazard" (a self-referential grader collapsing to replay) was explicitly retracted once re-run outside a memorization regime — it had been overfitting, not the predicted mirror geometry — and the mirror/non-mirror split was rebuilt as a graded axis; a same-session crossover (verifier beats peer-review at low task validity, peer beats verifier at high validity) reframes the axis as separating *legality* gating from *location* gating. Full account: [minting/README.md](minting/README.md).

## Era 9 — Endogenizing the grader (07-29 → 08-02)

Two independent nodes closed the same gap named in `ideas/meta_learning_under_metered_data.md` — no run had been both endogenous *and* expanding. [directed_sculpting/full_loop/endogenous_expansion/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/endogenous_expansion/README.md) (a trained, frozen critic) recovered 19% of an external DP teacher's ballistic lift; [directed_sculpting/full_loop/endo_expansion/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/endo_expansion/README.md) (paid rollout, no value net) recovered 34%. Both converge on the same reframing: **the deficit is fidelity, not endogeneity** — fidelity-matched external controls bracket the endogenous grader's performance almost exactly, and "a grader is a ceiling approached from whichever side you start." The era's more consequential finding is a second, causal instrument failure: the belief-PR metric used since Era 3/7 to claim "expansion" **inverts** — a content-free random target produces the *largest* PR rise in either experiment while sitting at the no-loop floor on every functional readout, because PR reads idle capacity being filled, not target content. This retroactively flags every earlier PR-based expansion number (Stage 5, sculpt-continual) as needing a content control they never had. A parallel wirehead-detection instrument was independently built and independently broken by both nodes on the same day, for the same structural reason (the softmax over candidates only sees relative value, so absolute value is a gauge freedom) — recorded so it isn't rebuilt.

## Era 10 — The level axis and its self-correction (08-03 → 08-04)

The most recent work, and possibly the most consequential self-correction in this history. [directed_sculpting/full_loop/level_moves/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/level_moves/README.md) gave the relevance/value agent a level-indexed action space (commit to an entire abstract subtree, not just a leaf). A first pass showed a clean monotonic preference for higher levels — and turned out to be almost entirely **span**: a depth-matched distractor with *zero* certified task value showed an even steeper rise, because higher-level moves mechanically displace more of the pooled representation regardless of merit. A matched-span "lazy twin" control, which cancels span by construction, recovered a genuine (smaller) preference for abstract commitment — but its *slope with level* still didn't resolve, because the published corruption task leaves most damage locally repairable, so abstraction never had to matter. A new damage model that requires level-matched repair finally produced the predicted signal: preference for abstraction tracks how deep the error actually is. The follow-up, [level_moves/level_ladder/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/level_moves/level_ladder/README.md), asked whether an *allocator* should learn to climb this axis and found the allocation prize is essentially zero (a level-blind allocator recovers 99% of an oracle's advantage) while the *action-space* contrast is enormous (3.5× ballistic control from having level-indexed moves available at all). The theoretical statement this landed on, stated in the child README's own title: **levels are an action-space axis, not an allocation-space axis.** Channels are disjoint (budgeting over them is a real decision about which parameters get trained); levels are nested on the same positions (spending "at the wrong level" can't starve anything), so there is no allocation prize for a learning-progress-driven allocator to climb toward on this substrate — directly narrowing the applicability of the "satiety recruits ℓ+1" mechanism proposed in `ideas/adaptive_core_and_hierarchy_climb.md`. Flagged explicitly as an argument fitted to a null, not yet a direct test.

## Where this leaves us

The throughline from Era 2 onward is one long argument about what "a moving frontier" actually requires: not depth (Era 3), not self-reference in the grader (Era 9), not budget reallocation across an already-nested axis (Era 10) — but a grounded, sufficiently faithful, genuinely non-stationary target (Era 7's core positive result, and the ceiling every Era 8–9 endogenous substitute has bumped into). A second, equally load-bearing throughline is methodological: nearly every era's headline number was produced, then caught and corrected by an internal control — the repair-cost trap ([directed_sculpting/README.md](directed_sculpting/README.md)), the metering-sweep sign flip, the belief-PR inversion, the span confound. The instrument-correction pattern is now treated as the more durable output of a node than any single effect size, and each README states its own corrections inline rather than silently revising a number. Open, load-bearing threads: a belief-size-vs-quality readout that survives a content control (Era 9); the root-level (L4) dead end in the level-move work, unresolved across every instrument tried (Era 10); and whether a non-privileged, learned verifier can recover more of minting's gap once density-, not just support-, matching is attempted (Era 8).
