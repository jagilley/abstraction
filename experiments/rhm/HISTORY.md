# History: `experiments/rhm`

*A big-picture pass over ~6 weeks of work (2026-06-20 → 2026-08-08), read primarily from checkpoint READMEs rather than raw diffs. Not a changelog — a trace of which beliefs got made, broken, and remade.*

## Why RHM exists

RHM (Random Hierarchy Model, Cagnetta & Wyart 2024) was adopted after natural-language forward-model experiments hit a confound: interventions meant to isolate the data-generating process (DGP) turned out to also change the observation channel, entangling the two ([`README.md`](README.md), [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md)). RHM gives a synthetic hierarchy of composition rules (depth `L`, synonym multiplicity `m`, branching `s`, vocab `v`) with every token's ancestry known exactly — DGP and channel become independently tunable, and per-token hierarchy level becomes a real label instead of a proxy.

## Act I — scaling law, then an instrument (late June)

The project opened as a pure scaling-law probe: the first sweep found synonym multiplicity `m` dominates depth `L` roughly 3:1 in setting the loss exponent — resolving per-level synonym ambiguity, not stacking depth, was the bottleneck ([`SWEEP_README.md`](SWEEP_README.md)). Attention then shifted to forward self-models (FMs) predicting a model's own later activations, with FM-residual **rank** as the instrument for "how much unexplained structure is left." This belief eroded in stages: rank tracked FM/architecture mismatch more than DGP complexity ([`RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md`](RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md)); then it tracked `m`-sharpening overhead, not composition depth ([`LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md`](LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md)); and finally the whole `R_act ≈ R_comp + R_res` partition was shown to be arithmetically false, replaced by a power law `res_var ∝ act_var^β` that cleanly separates *shape* (β, capacity-invariant) from *level* (participation ratio, the thing that actually moves) — retroactively explaining three earlier unexplained nulls ([`residual_decomposition/README.md`](residual_decomposition/README.md)). The instrument itself became a recurring object of study, not just a ruler.

## Act II — what sets the composition ceiling (late June)

A parallel thread asked why models plateau after 1–2 hierarchy levels. `REGIME_TRANSITION_README.md` chased the hypothesis that the FM residual should sharpen from diffuse to rule-conditioned as mastery deepens; it failed at small scale before landing on the now-canonical 6-layer/L=6/m=4 configuration used everywhere downstream. `PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md` then confirmed strictly bottom-up learning with a hard ceiling at that setting. Two candidate explanations for the ceiling were tested and both **rejected as primary causes**: gradient-sharpening bias from level-0 dominance (label smoothing helped nothing — [`LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md`](LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md)) and raw model capacity (a >200× capacity range was irrelevant — [`PER_LEVEL_EXPANSION_README.md`](PER_LEVEL_EXPANSION_README.md)). The surviving explanation, converged on from both loss-weighting and expansion sweeps, is **information dilution set by `m` alone**: composition depth is a property of the DGP's synonym multiplicity, not of the objective's sharpness or the model's size. A useful side effect of this arc: reweighting loss toward FM-surprise makes models dramatically more self-model-legible (cosine → 0.995) without unlocking deeper composition — legibility and task performance came apart as separately-controllable axes ([`LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md`](LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md)).

## Act III — is deep structure there, and is it legible? (late June–July)

A four-doc arc split "does the model represent deep composition" into four separable questions. `RHM_DEEP_COMPOSITION_README.md` found the signal for deep synonymy is real and depth-flat, but no self-supervised local objective (NTP, masked-span, contrastive) ever concentrates it — only a privileged oracle target does; the lever is the objective, not capacity. `RHM_FRONTIER_AND_LEGIBILITY_README.md` refined "not learnable" to specifically `m≥4`, introduced FM-residual legibility as a construct (η² rises exactly where the base model is learning), and found "knowledge-grok without circuit-grok" — RHM's deep inverse never compresses into an isolable circuit the way modular arithmetic does. `RHM_COMPLEXODYNAMICS_README.md` reread the resulting rank rise-and-fall through Aaronson's complextropy lens: the transient hump is FM-idiosyncratic scaffolding, the floor is DGP-set and FM-invariant — a claim that survived a full instrument replacement when rank was later retired. `RHM_EDIT_CONTROL_README.md` pivoted from passive legibility to control and found a new, independent failure mode: off-manifold belief-faithfulness collapse lets planners game their own success classifier (true success ~0 while believed success ~100%), partially fixed by constraining edits to a learned generator's manifold plus a self-consistency veto. Standing view: recoverability, representability, learnability, legibility, and editability are four separately-gated properties, not one.

## Act IV — the ratchet: a fertile negative result (June 23–29)

A dedicated sub-thread ported the MNIST/language wake-sleep self-distillation "ratchet" — where FM-predictability pressure compounds across cycles — onto RHM. Every variant tested (base ratchet, m=2 headroom, L4, sparsity-matched, RL-driven generation, sparse NTP, first-order and true bilevel meta-learning via FOMAML/Reptile) converged on the same answer: **local FM-predictability pressure does not improve compositional NTP on stationary RHM data**, however the confound was sliced ([`ratchet/README.md`](ratchet/README.md) et al., marked "concluded (negative), retained for reproducibility"). The diagnosed reason — compounding needs a *moving frontier* (novel rules, growing depth) that a fixed rule set cannot supply — is explicitly credited in the parent README with routing the program away from passive self-distillation and toward the active/sculpting/latent-loop lines that now dominate. One genuinely positive sub-result survived: RL-based generation reward, distilled via the teacher's own rollouts rather than ground truth, prevented representational collapse and gave the first real self-knowledge advantage for the closed loop — but on outcome metrics, not composition depth.

## Act V — sleep chunking, the latent loop, and sculpting (July)

`RHM_FM_REGULARIZER_README.md` decoupled two conflated benefits of FM pressure: open-loop "be-predictable" pressure alone beats weight decay's compression floor and *improves* legibility, without needing the full self-knowledge loop. `SLEEP_CHUNKING_RHM_README.md` found the same bottleneck trained to reconstruct vs. predict its input produces opposite behavior — prediction chunks hierarchically, reconstruction doesn't — and traced the recursive-chunking ceiling to Korchinski–Favero–Wyart dilution costs. That "target, not capacity" insight fed the field's central pivot, `RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md`: crossing token-vs-latent target with open-vs-closed loop showed generalizable self-knowledge appears **only** under a DGP-aligned latent target, never a token target — overturning the MNIST/language-derived belief that closing the loop alone suffices. `RHM_SCULPTING_README.md` then converted this into active planning: an initial planner failed for task-design reasons (no lookahead value), and once fixed, latent-space planning matched token-space planning at ~8× lower cost on a clean channel and became genuinely *superior* once the channel turned lossy — with belief quality (not privilege) as the real bottleneck. `RHM_SCULPT_CONTINUAL_README.md` tested compounding directly and found a **weak ratchet**: policy improvement dominates, one-shot internalization sets most of the ceiling, and re-internalization barely adds more — representational headroom is extracted once and exhausts, matching the latent-loop prediction.

## Act VI — from passive to active RHM, and the current frontier (July 27–August)

`ACTIVE_RHM_README.md` asked whether RHM's autonomous framing could be made genuinely active like sibling control experiments; it found value-of-information (not mean-Δ) is required for planning, and diagnosed *why* internalizing a forward model has little headroom here: epistemic RHM is "inference in disguise" (acting ≈ planning). That negative motivated inventing a real control task — editing/sculpting rather than revealing ([`sculpting_control_task.md`](sculpting_control_task.md)) — which grew into `directed_sculpting/`, the most active current thread. Its first surprise was structural: RHM's rule usage is uniform, so naive relevance signals collapse and had to be engineered (distractor channels, entropy-matched drift, a repair-cost instrument that itself needed two bug fixes before it was trustworthy). Once built, `full_loop/` delivered a genuine positive — a reward-free relevance signal recovers 76% of an oracle's advantage — alongside negatives on the idea doc's drift-dependent predictions that were later partially reversed once instruments were repaired (an "invariance ≠ necessity" lesson: starving samples-per-event revealed the first positive on hierarchy-climbing). A `level_moves` finding of a monotone level preference was retracted in the same node that found it, replaced by a real, error-depth-graded abstraction premium; belief-participation-ratio was independently retired as an expansion ranker by two sibling children the same day ([`directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md)). The `residual_decomposition/trajectory/` child re-ran the complexodynamics arc on the corrected instrument: the rise-and-fall *shape* reproduced 7/7, but the scalar β itself failed its own capacity-invariance check on this substrate — trustworthy directions, untrustworthy magnitudes.

## Act VII — can a model teach itself? (July 16–August 7)

A separate arc asked whether self-generated supervision can substitute for external signal. `specialization/README.md` found neither breadth restriction nor level-reweighted loss unlocks deeper composition — only a privileged direct label does — and, more surprisingly, that concentrating even a direct target on a subtree fails to specialize because RHM's grammar is fully shared below the root (broad training generalizes to a subtree *better* than narrow training on it). `minting/README.md` tested self-generated data verified by a rule-table oracle: it beat no-filter and beat own-likelihood filtering, technically satisfying its preregistered falsifier, but only reached break-even against a static control — diagnosed as a *support* oracle (deletes bad mass) being fundamentally different from a *density* oracle (assigns correct probability), which is what NTP needs. `endogenous_teacher/` — explicitly preregistered after two prior burned intuitions — tested whether an FM's own residual, independent of token surprisal (confirmed via R²=0.113, anti-localized), can teach when used as a scalar loss reweighting; the registered null fired cleanly (`res` ≈ `res_shuffled`). The `cancellation/` child re-ran the claim through the forward path instead of the loss and replicated the mechanism across substrates but found no modularity payoff. Both prongs failed for the same diagnosed reason: every arm trained on plain NTP throughout, so only the *weighting*, never the *supervision itself*, was varied. `conditional_revision/SPEC.md` (2026-08-07, not yet run) is the direct response — switching from a depth FM (zero epistemic gap by construction) to a temporal FM with a genuine one, explicitly built to measure before intervening.

## Act VIII — confabulation (parallel probe, July 27 onward)

Independent of the allocation/teaching threads, `confabulation/README.md` asked whether a model's self-report tracks genuine internal access or a learned self-theory. Reports about the FM residual beat every capacity-matched third-party observer by +0.09–0.11 and the gap is flat in observer capacity (access-limited, not resource-limited); a preregistered guess that closing the loop was *required* for this was falsified (positive advantage survives open-loop), and a late correction found the original closed-loop amplification factor was a pooling artifact — the honest effect is smaller but better-explained: closing the loop improves access to what's already there rather than creating more to know.

## Standing beliefs as of 2026-08-08

- The composition ceiling is an information-dilution property of `m`, not objective sharpness or model capacity.
- Self-model legibility and task performance are separately controllable axes — you can buy one without the other.
- Recoverability / representability / learnability / legibility / editability of deep structure are four distinct, separately-gated properties.
- Passive FM-predictability pressure does not compound on stationary data; compounding needs a moving frontier (this is *why* the sculpting/active line exists).
- Self-knowledge appears to be **directional**, not reducible to a scalar magnitude — every scalar-reweighting attempt at self-teaching has failed or nulled, while directional/support-based signals (confabulation, minting's support filter) show real effects.
- Measurement artifacts have repeatedly biased toward the exciting story; a large share of this program's actual output has been *repaired instruments* (β/participation-ratio, floor-corrected reducibility, entropy-matched drift baselines) rather than single positive findings.
- Current open frontier: `directed_sculpting/full_loop/` (does abstraction-novelty reopen the ratchet?) and `conditional_revision/` (is there a genuinely epistemic, non-scalar self-revision signal at all?).
