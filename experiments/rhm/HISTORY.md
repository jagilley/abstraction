# History of `experiments/rhm`

RHM (Random Hierarchy Model) is a controlled data-generating process (DGP) for
language-like data with a known ground-truth tree structure. It was adopted
because natural-language experiments couldn't cleanly separate DGP
interventions from measurement-channel interventions — vocab reduction, e.g.,
changed the branching factor β but was entangled with what was being measured
(`experiments/rhm/README.md`, `experiments/rhm/CLAUDE.md`). The promise of RHM
is a known answer key: activations, residuals, and forecasts can be checked
against ground truth rather than inferred.

## Era 1 — Establishing the substrate (late June)

`experiments/rhm/SWEEP_README.md` set the first scaling fact that shaped
everything downstream: synonymic multiplicity **m** dominates the RHM scaling
exponent far more than depth L (α drops 34% for 4× m vs 12% for 2× depth).
`RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md` then asked whether forward-model (FM) residual rank
tracks DGP complexity and found instead that rank is dominated by
FM-architecture mismatch and capacity — a puzzle left explicitly unresolved
at the time (rank pinned at 90–96% across a 17× residual-norm range). This
puzzle sat unexplained for a month before being resolved in Era 8.

## Era 2 — The ratchet arc: does the MNIST result transfer? (06-23 – 06-29)

The A2A gated wake-sleep ratchet had compounded dramatically on MNIST. This
arc (`experiments/rhm/ratchet/README.md` and its children) asked whether the
same inject-FM-forecast / distill / reinitialize mechanism compounds on RHM.
It replicated MNIST *dynamics* but at negligible magnitude, and a sequence of
controlled eliminations — capacity-headroom (`RHM_RATCHET_M2_README.md`),
NTP sparsity (`RHM_SPARSITY_SWEEP_README.md`, `RHM_SPARSE_RATCHET_README.md`),
overparameterization (`RHM_L4_RATCHET_README.md`) — ruled out capacity and
density as the cause. A pivot to RL-as-rich-supervision
(`RHM_RL_RATCHET_README.md`) was the arc's most productive stretch:
generation-based distillation (distilling on teacher rollouts rather than
ground-truth NTP) resolved a stability/transfer tension and gave the first
RHM case where self-knowledge exceeded object-level learning — but still no
compounding across cycles. The closing diagnostics
(`RHM_FOMAML_README.md`, `RHM_META_LEARNING_README.md`) showed that on
*stationary* data the meta/outer loop collapses to the inner loop no matter
the objective. **Conclusion that reset the program's direction:** compounding
needs a moving frontier (novel rules/depth), which stationary RHM cannot
supply — this is the direct ancestor of the later active/sculpting lines.

## Era 3 — What makes RHM computation legible to a self-model? (early July)

With the ratchet question closed, focus shifted to characterizing *when*
FM residuals become hierarchy-legible at all.
`RHM_FRONTIER_AND_LEGIBILITY_README.md` found m gates how deep plain NTP
learns, and a co-trained FM residual becomes legible exactly where the base
model is learning (an "amplifier, not source" relationship).
`RHM_DEEP_COMPOSITION_README.md` sharpened this: deep structure is
recoverable and representable, but not learnable by any local self-supervised
objective tried — **dilution, not capacity, is the binding constraint**.
`SLEEP_CHUNKING_RHM_README.md` connected this to Korchinski–Favero–Wyart
theory (token objectives cost exponential-in-depth samples; latent-target
objectives are constant) and confirmed only external-role prediction produces
real top-down compression. `RHM_FM_REGULARIZER_README.md` showed
FM-predictability pressure alone compresses activation rank without eroding
deep structure — self-knowledge machinery isn't required for functional
simplification. `RHM_COMPLEXODYNAMICS_README.md` reframed the whole
trajectory as an Aaronson-style rise-then-fall of complexity.

## Era 4 — Self-knowledge and its limits (07-04 – 07-08)

`RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md` is a pivot document: closing the cerebellar
self-model loop produces generalizable self-knowledge on RHM for the first
time — but only under a *latent* (own-DGP-lifted) target, never a token
target. Follow-on ablations progressively refined a deflationary conclusion:
self-knowledge here is meta-knowledge generated by injection, and RHM's
dense, compact grammar leaves nothing for distillation to transfer — a
genuine disanalogy from the earlier MNIST/language results.

## Era 5 — Disentangling depth, capacity, and the loss function (mid July)

`REGIME_TRANSITION_README.md` records a first failed attempt (L=4) where the
FM captured 99%+ of computation and left no residual to structure — not a
falsification, a scale problem. A cosine-gap sweep found the counterintuitive
result that *higher* m makes the FM's job easier (an undertrained model's
trivial computation is trivially predictable), motivating a scale-up to
`L=6, m=4, v=8, s=2`, which became the default hyperparameters for all
subsequent RHM work. `PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md` then showed strictly
bottom-up learning (a wave propagating up the hierarchy) and a hard
composition-depth ceiling. `PER_LEVEL_EXPANSION_README.md` is a notable
self-correction: an early two-point comparison had confounded model capacity
with m, tempting an "one hierarchy level per layer" claim that was already
drafted into a paper; a proper >200× capacity sweep at fixed m showed
**capacity is nearly irrelevant — m sets the ceiling.** The claim was walked
back before publication. `LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md` then cleanly falsified
the hypothesis that NTP's sharpness bias caused the ceiling (smoothing hurt
at every level). `LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md` found the real lever was
position-weighting, not sharpness: reallocating gradient away from level-0
(focal loss, confidence thresholding) genuinely improved FM legibility,
peaking near cosine 0.995 — the strongest "model legible to its own
compressed self-model" result in the whole arc.

## Era 6 — RHM as a control task (mid July)

`RHM_EDIT_CONTROL_README.md`, `RHM_SCULPTING_README.md`, and
`RHM_SCULPT_CONTINUAL_README.md` built RHM into a genuine control task,
discovering belief-space planning is gameable off-manifold (99% success in
belief, ~0% true success) until actions are constrained to a learned
generator's manifold, and that latent-space planning — merely a cheaper
surrogate for token-space planning on clean channels — becomes genuinely
*superior* once the channel is lossy. Continual re-internalization turned out
to be only a weak ratchet; one-shot belief reshaping plus value iteration
captures nearly all the gain. `ACTIVE_RHM_README.md` found a clean structural
null for active querying (mean-Δ FMs can't plan epistemic queries), but a
genuine positive with a value-of-information head — motivating
`sculpting_control_task.md`'s planning-beats-reflex result, which finally
gave RHM a true control-task analog to the MuJoCo motor-learning work
(`experiments/mjc`) and directly seeded Era 9.

Parallel to this, `experiments/rhm/specialization/README.md` (07-16, with a
07-18 reframe and a 07-29 correction) found neither breadth restriction nor
level-reweighted NTP moves the depth frontier — only a *direct* deep target
does (root recovery 0.08→0.80) — and that concentrating even a direct target
onto a subtree fails to specialize, because RHM's single shared ruleset has
no domain-specific deep structure. This is the sharpest RHM-vs-language
disanalogy found, motivating an unbuilt context-sensitive DGP ("cut-3"). A
later note there also retracts a claim (a downstream doc had wrongly merged
this inert breadth result with an unrelated concentration result).

## Era 7 — Confabulation test (07-22)

`experiments/rhm/confabulation/README.md` used the FM-decomposition as an
operational answer to Nisbett–Wilson confabulation worries: self-reports
about the FM residual show a genuine, capacity-invariant introspective
advantage over third-party observers, while reports about behavior/entropy/
world-state show none. A correction here — an apparent 13× gap turned out to
be a pooling-estimator artifact (true gap ~9%, not 12×) — is an early
instance of a discipline ("trust the diagnostic, not the first number") that
becomes central in Era 9.

## Era 8 — The residual-rank retirement (07-26 – 07-27)

`experiments/rhm/residual_decomposition/README.md` resolves the Era 1 puzzle:
naive residual rank conflates *shape* and *magnitude*. The "flat rank across
17× residual-norm range" result was never a contradiction — it's exactly
predicted by a power law `res_var ∝ act_var^β`, where β is capacity-invariant
even as the magnitude moves. The new instrument (β +
`R_res_participation`) also retroactively falsifies the literal arithmetic of
a cross-repo belief (`R_act ≈ R_comp + R_res`) while preserving its
qualitative intuition. Per the repo convention of keeping prior results
reproducible, `RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md` was annotated as superseded rather
than deleted — falsifying an instrument without abandoning the underlying
question.

## Era 9 — Porting E3 to RHM: the directed-sculpting sprint (07-28 – 07-29)

The dominant recent thread ports the MuJoCo "E3" experiment's inner+outer
loop onto RHM (`experiments/rhm/directed_sculpting/README.md`). Since RHM's
rule usage is uniform by construction, there is no relevance measure to
allocate over — this required building non-tree **distractor channels** and
a support-fixed OU drift with a closed-form KL, which caught an 11.9×
miscalibration before it could masquerade as a real effect. Building the
repair-cost instrument hit two traps in immediate succession: raw
cross-entropy is exactly blind to this drift by construction, and a
gap-to-matched-reference readout was confounded by ordinary continued
training (fixed via difference-in-differences).

The full loop (`experiments/rhm/directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md`)
produced a smoking-gun positive — reward-free relevance separates real
tokens from distractors 13–18× with zero labels — but both headline
drift-dependent predictions (hierarchy climbing, drift-sustained
representational expansion) initially came back negative, each after
instrument repairs that pointed *toward* the hypothesized effect (an
inverted "reducibility tap," an own-world-grading artifact), strengthening
rather than weakening the null. Expansion turned out to be a property of
grader *type*, orthogonal to drift. On 07-29, the climb null was reasoned to
be scoped rather than general — surface re-fit was absorbing 91% of each
drift event "for free," so nothing had ever priced the integrated cost.
Starving samples-per-event at fixed drift magnitude produced the arc's first
positive climbing signal, with the depth advantage migrating from surface to
deep levels monotonically across four budgets.

**Open frontier as of 07-29:** a repair-cost readout that hasn't yet reported
cleanly three times running, a level-ordered allocation space, the unplaced
in-tree irreducible cell, unbuilt "aliasing drift," and extending the
necessity sweep past S=64 samples/event.

## Standing threads across the whole arc

- **m (synonymic multiplicity), not depth or capacity, is the recurring
  binding constraint** — from `SWEEP_README.md` through
  `PER_LEVEL_EXPANSION_README.md` to `specialization/README.md`.
- **Confounds get caught, not swept under the rug**: the capacity/m
  confound, the pooling-estimator artifact, the reducibility-tap inversion,
  and the residual-rank shape/magnitude conflation are all cases of a
  result being corrected in place rather than quietly discarded.
- **Stationary self-supervision has a ceiling; external signal (RL reward,
  latent targets, direct deep targets, drift) is what moves it** — this
  single idea threads the ratchet arc, the latent-loop arc, the
  loss-weighting arc, and the current directed-sculpting sprint.
