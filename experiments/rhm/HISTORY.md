# History of `experiments/rhm/`

*A big-picture retrospective, regenerated from scratch. See each cited README for detail — this file only traces the shape of the thinking.*

RHM (Random Hierarchy Model) is a synthetic-language generator with a known, exactly-specified data-generating process (a random context-free grammar over `L` levels with synonymic multiplicity `m`). Its purpose in this repo is to be a substrate where "did the model learn the true generative structure?" is answerable by construction, not by proxy. Almost every sub-thread below is best read as an attempt to build an *instrument* trustworthy enough to answer that question, followed by the discovery that the first version of the instrument was itself wrong.

## Era 1 — Mechanics and the composition ceiling (June 2026)

The earliest work ([`README.md`](README.md), [`SWEEP_README.md`](SWEEP_README.md), [`RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md`](RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md), [`PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md`](PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md)) established that models trained with plain next-token prediction (NTP) learn RHM bottom-up and stall at a depth ceiling well short of the root. The first hypothesis — that the ceiling was an *objective* artifact (loss shape, label sharpness) — was tested and rejected: label smoothing did nothing ([`LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md`](LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md)), and a later capacity sweep across 200× parameter counts moved the ceiling almost not at all, explicitly **retracting** an earlier essay's "one level of hierarchy per layer" reading ([`PER_LEVEL_EXPANSION_README.md`](PER_LEVEL_EXPANSION_README.md)). The real variable, settled over several docs, is **`m` (synonymic multiplicity), not occupancy or capacity** — `m=2` groks to the root, `m≥4` stalls regardless of how recoverable the structure is in principle ([`RHM_FRONTIER_AND_LEGIBILITY_README.md`](RHM_FRONTIER_AND_LEGIBILITY_README.md)). [`RHM_DEEP_COMPOSITION_README.md`](RHM_DEEP_COMPOSITION_README.md) generalized this: no local self-supervised objective tried (NTP, MLM, span-masking, contrastive/DINO/Sinkhorn invariance) reaches deep composition — the high-level signal is *intrinsically diluted* by any token-local task, not merely hard to optimize.

The one lever that worked was changing *what* the model predicts, not how hard it's pushed to predict it: [`SLEEP_CHUNKING_RHM_README.md`](SLEEP_CHUNKING_RHM_README.md) found hierarchical chunking only emerges under an external-role objective (predict a neighbor's latent), and connected this to a token-dilution vs. latent-target-cost account from the literature — crystallizing **"predict your own lifted latents, not tokens"** as the variable that structures everything downstream. [`RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md`](RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md) confirmed it directly: closing a self-knowledge loop (a forward model, FM, predicting the network's own residual) produces generalizable self-knowledge only under a *latent* target, never a token target, at any depth or loop strength. This is the origin of "FM residual as instrument," reused in nearly every later sub-thread.

In parallel, an active-control arc ([`ACTIVE_RHM_README.md`](ACTIVE_RHM_README.md) → [`RHM_EDIT_CONTROL_README.md`](RHM_EDIT_CONTROL_README.md) → [`RHM_SCULPTING_README.md`](RHM_SCULPTING_README.md), [`sculpting_control_task.md`](sculpting_control_task.md), [`RHM_SCULPT_CONTINUAL_README.md`](RHM_SCULPT_CONTINUAL_README.md)) ported a2a's "reaching" control machinery onto RHM and found active *querying* degenerates to inference (no internalization headroom), while active *editing* is genuinely plannable but the belief representation is gameable unless paired with a learned (not hand-built) veto. This active/plannable-belief template is the direct ancestor of the later `directed_sculpting/` program.

## Era 2 — The ratchet detour: meta-learning doesn't compound on fixed data

A large, systematic arc in [`ratchet/`](ratchet/README.md) asked whether the wake-sleep "gated ratchet" that produced a 48% win on MNIST would compound on RHM. Across nine variants — capacity ([`RHM_L4_RATCHET_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_L4_RATCHET_README.md), [`RHM_RATCHET_M2_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_RATCHET_M2_README.md)), supervision sparsity ([`RHM_SPARSITY_SWEEP_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_SPARSITY_SWEEP_README.md), [`RHM_SPARSE_RATCHET_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_SPARSE_RATCHET_README.md)), RL-based reward ([`RHM_RL_RATCHET_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_RL_RATCHET_README.md), [`RHM_RL_GEN_DISTILL_EXTENDED_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_RL_GEN_DISTILL_EXTENDED_README.md)), and bilevel/meta-learning methods ([`RHM_FOMAML_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_FOMAML_README.md), [`RHM_META_LEARNING_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_META_LEARNING_README.md)) — each hypothesis was tested and rejected in turn. The diagnosis that finally stuck: on **stationary** data, the outer loop is identical to the inner loop, so there is no bilevel signal to select compositional structure by. The arc is explicitly marked concluded/negative, and its stated implication — that compounding requires a *moving frontier* (novel rules, deeper levels, something that changes) — is the seed that grew into the later `directed_sculpting` and specialization programs.

## Era 3 — Directed sculpting: an active loop needs a relevance signal

Starting late July, work reorganized into subdirectories and pivoted to actively *directing* representation change rather than passively training on it. [`directed_sculpting/README.md`](directed_sculpting/README.md) tried to port mjc's inner+outer control loop (`value = lprog × visits`) onto RHM and hit an immediate wall: RHM's rule usage is uniform by construction, so there's no relevance measure to allocate budget over. The fix — non-tree distractor channels plus a drift/repair-cost instrument — became the substrate for everything after. [`full_loop/README.md`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md) shows the one clean, repeatedly-reproduced positive result of this whole era: the reward-free relevance signal separates real tokens from distractors 13–18×. But the two headline predictions — that drift would drive hierarchy-*climbing* and sustain "expansion" — came back negative, twice, after two rounds of instrument repair.

What follows is a chain of independently-launched children each chasing one piece of that null, and each finding the null was partly a broken instrument rather than a fact about the world:

- [`endogenous_expansion/`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/endogenous_expansion/README.md) and [`endo_expansion/`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/endo_expansion/README.md) forked independently onto the same question (does an endogenously-generated target expand capability?) with different designs, and converged on the same two findings: yes it expands, and **"a grader is a ceiling"** — the residual gap is target *fidelity*, not endogeneity per se (external teachers degraded to matched fidelity land in the same place). Both also independently discovered that belief effective-dimension (participation ratio) is *anti*-informative as a progress metric — it rises most under a zero-information random target. This PR-as-detector-not-ranker correction now propagates back into the parent [`full_loop/README.md`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md) §5.
- [`partial_hetero/`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/partial_hetero/README.md) tried to build the missing middle ground between fully-shared and fully-independent channels and got a flat result — not because sharing doesn't matter, but because the block-FM's unit of work (level-1 features) has no shared vocabulary to detect at partial sharing depths. Its child [`shared_surface/`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/partial_hetero/shared_surface/README.md) fixed the vocabulary gap by splicing surface tables instead of top tables, and found the curve is *still* flat — this time because the instrument's own noise floor exceeds any real effect. Each retraction is more precise than the last, not a reversal.
- [`composed_loop/`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/composed_loop/README.md), the most recent and most provisional (single-seed), composes the endogenous judge with a level-indexed action space under hierarchical damage and finds a sharp negative interaction: a good external grader exploits the richer action space, the endogenous judge captures none of the extra surplus — because a scalar terminal-success signal carries no depth information.

## Era 4 — Can the model's own signal be trusted? (confabulation, endogenous_teacher, conditional_revision)

A second, thematically distinct thread asks a version of the same question from the inside: is a model's self-generated signal — a self-report, a training gradient, a revision — actually about the model's own computation, or is it a plausible-looking artifact? [`endogenous_teacher/README.md`](endogenous_teacher/README.md) tried reweighting the NTP loss by an FM residual as an endogenous teaching signal and got a clean, pre-registered null: the residual tracks computational load, not epistemic difficulty, and is nearly independent of surprisal. Its own postmortem diagnosed the flaw as using a *depth*-wise FM with zero aleatoric component — which directly motivated [`conditional_revision/README.md`](conditional_revision/README.md), built around a *temporal* FM with a genuine one-token information gap. That thread found a real, validated signal (belief revision separable from surprisal) after fixing its own probe design once (an all-node probe drowned in noise; restricting to the ancestor chain fixed it) and catching a subtler error later — a "recovery" reading at root levels turned out to be two cancelling readout errors, not real signal. Its child [`local_loss/`](conditional_revision/local_loss/README.md) is the sharpest example of this pattern in the whole repo: a dramatic 19× loss reduction was progressively shown, commit by commit, to be mostly a *gauge* artifact (true scale-free predictability moves only ~1.6–1.9×), landing on the honest conclusion that the temporal target reproduces the same self-knowledge degeneracy as the depth-wise arm, just further along. [`sculpt_slip/README.md`](conditional_revision/sculpt_slip/README.md) closes out negatively for a related reason: the "prize" doesn't grow as data shrinks because the FM is bias-, not variance-, limited at every budget tested.

Structurally separate but on the same axis, [`confabulation/README.md`](confabulation/README.md) tested whether self-reports track a model's actual implementation vs. a learned self-theory reconstructable by an outside observer (a Nisbett & Wilson-style design) and found a genuine, capacity-robust first-person advantage for implementation-level reports — the one clearly positive result in this era — while behavioral/entangled/world reports show no such advantage, exactly as predicted.

## Era 5 — What's worth paying for: level economy and metering

A parallel late-arc thread priced the *costs* inside the sculpting loop. [`metering_sweep/README.md`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/metering_sweep/README.md) set out to test whether the value of monitoring/curation shrinks to zero as data gets cheap, and instead found its own round-1 "meter" had never been a real variable, then — once fixed — that its round-2 causal story ("damage is the denominator") was itself wrong, refuted by its own follow-up experiment. The claim only survives under an iso-compute control, where the prize is humped rather than monotonic. Its sharpest result: an honestly-priced cheap relevance tap recovers 87% of the oracle prize at 2% of budget, while an expensive reducibility tap recovers ~4% — a ~1200× cost-effectiveness gap.

[`level_moves/README.md`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/level_moves/README.md) directly attacked the hierarchy-climbing question left open in Era 3, and underwent its own self-correction mid-thread: an initial headline ("the value prefers higher levels, monotonically") was retracted once a matched-span control showed it was mostly a magnitude artifact; what replaced it — an abstraction preference that scales with how *deep* an error is — is more precise and more interesting. Its child [`level_ladder/README.md`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/level_moves/level_ladder/README.md) delivered this era's cleanest result: a privileged level-aware *allocator* is worth essentially nothing once channel allocation is fixed, but switching the *action space* to level-indexed moves is worth 3.5× — **where to spend is worth zero; what you can do is worth a lot.** `metered_climb/`[^private] was pre-registered as the natural next step (does climbing survive the same iso-compute confound metering_sweep found?) but was never executed — work moved on to Era 4's `endogenous_teacher` instead, leaving it as an open, well-specified, dormant follow-up.

## Era 6 — The shared-grammar ceiling (minting, residual_decomposition, specialization)

A third parallel program asked whether RHM's homogeneous, fully-shared grammar itself is what limits everything above. [`specialization/README.md`](specialization/README.md) found neither breadth-restriction nor level-reweighting can carve out extra depth for a subtree — and that concentrating signal onto one subtree actively *hurts* it, because the shared deep grammar is exactly what makes transfer sample-efficient in the first place; "broad beats narrow" was reframed from a specialization failure into a statement about why RHM generalizes at all. [`minting/README.md`](minting/README.md) tested whether a model can mint and filter its own synthetic data to escape a capacity cap, and found that filtering can only ever move a filter from harmful to break-even relative to no filtering — because a rule-based filter is a support oracle, not a density oracle, and NTP needs density, not just support. [`residual_decomposition/README.md`](residual_decomposition/README.md) is the era's methodological center: it disproved the repo's own earlier residual-rank arithmetic (`R_act ≈ R_comp + R_res` doesn't hold), replaced it with a capacity-invariant shape statistic (β) plus a participation measure, and used the new instrument to retroactively explain three previously-unexplained negative results elsewhere in the repo. Its child [`trajectory/README.md`](residual_decomposition/trajectory/README.md) reproduced the older complexity-rise-and-fall story 7/7 with the new instrument, but also found β itself fails its own invariance check on this substrate — a rare case where the new instrument is trusted for direction but explicitly not trusted for absolute magnitude.

## The throughline

Two things recur often enough across eras and independent sub-threads to count as standing lessons rather than coincidences. First, **almost every "negative" result in this repo turned out on closer inspection to be a negative result about the instrument, not about the hypothesis** — inverted taps, gauge artifacts, span confounds, anti-informative dimensionality metrics, cancelling readout errors — and the discipline that makes this repo's negatives trustworthy is that each one gets re-diagnosed rather than just reported. Second, the work has moved, era by era, from asking "can a model learn deep structure" (Era 1) to "can a model be made to actively seek it" (Eras 2–3) to "can a model's own signal about itself be trusted" (Eras 4–6) — each answer narrowing toward the same conclusion: self-generated signals and self-directed loops are usable, but only exactly as far as their generating process is faithful, never further, and detecting where that faithfulness runs out is now the central methodological occupation of this experiment line. The open threads at time of writing are `metered_climb/SPEC.md`[^private] (unexecuted), the cut-3 heterogeneous-DGP extensions implied by [`specialization/README.md`](specialization/README.md), and the unresolved reversals flagged in [`composed_loop/README.md`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/composed_loop/README.md).
