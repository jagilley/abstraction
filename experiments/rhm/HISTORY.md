# History: `experiments/rhm`

*A big-picture retrospective, regenerated from the repo's git history and README trail. Auto-updated periodically — treat as a fresh read each time, not an accumulating log.*

## Why RHM exists

The natural-language reduction experiments ([`experiments/language_reduction/STATUS.md`](../language_reduction/STATUS.md)) kept running into a problem: interventions on real text couldn't cleanly separate "changing the data-generating process" from "changing the observation channel," and spectral-denoising analyses were entangled with the statistics they measured. The Random Hierarchy Model (Cagnetta & Wyart, 2024) was adopted as a substrate where the DGP is fully known and controllable — depth `L`, synonym multiplicity `m`, branching `s`, vocab `v` — specifically so that forward-model and self-knowledge questions from [`a2a_forward`](../a2a_forward/README.md) could be tested with ground truth in hand. See [`README.md`](README.md) for the standing summary and [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) for the primitives.

## Era 1 — Calibrating the instrument (2026-06-20 → 06-23)

Before any self-knowledge question could be asked, the substrate itself had to be characterized. The scaling sweep ([`SWEEP_README.md`](SWEEP_README.md)) found `m` (per-level entropy) dominates the scaling exponent over `L` (depth) by ~3:1. The per-level loss decomposition ([`PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md`](PER_LEVEL_LOSS_README.md)) and regime-transition tracking ([`REGIME_TRANSITION_README.md`](REGIME_TRANSITION_README.md)) established the **L-to-m transition** as the default regime (L=6, m=4) and showed models learn bottom-up, plateauing at 1-2 composed levels. A deliberate falsification attempt — is the depth ceiling an artifact of cross-entropy's sharpening incentive? — closed cleanly negative: label smoothing ([`LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md`](LABEL_SMOOTHING_README.md)) changes confidence, not compositional depth. **The ceiling is a capacity constraint, not an objective bias.** Gradient-reallocation losses (focal loss, confidence thresholding, FM-surprise weighting — [`LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md`](LOSS_WEIGHTING_README.md)) do improve FM legibility at fixed capacity, foreshadowing that *what a model is trained to be predictable to* matters independent of what it's trained to predict. The original residual-rank instrument ([`RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md`](RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md)) was built here too — its own Experiment 3 (rank flat across a 17× norm range) already contained the seed of a problem that wouldn't be recognized as such for another five weeks (Era 5).

## Era 2 — The ratchet arc: chasing MNIST's compounding on stationary data (06-23 → 06-29)

MNIST's "gated ratchet" (elsewhere in the repo) compounds 34%→48% over four wake-sleep cycles via forward-model-predictability pressure. The RHM ratchet arc ([`ratchet/README.md`](ratchet/README.md)) is a chain of eliminations, each motivated by the previous experiment's leftover ambiguity, moving through **capacity → supervision density/richness → RL + distillation mechanics → optimization method** in that order ([`ratchet/RHM_RATCHET_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_RATCHET_README.md), [`ratchet/RHM_RATCHET_M2_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_RATCHET_M2_README.md), [`ratchet/RHM_L4_RATCHET_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_L4_RATCHET_README.md), [`ratchet/RHM_SPARSITY_SWEEP_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_SPARSITY_SWEEP_README.md), [`ratchet/RHM_SPARSE_RATCHET_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_SPARSE_RATCHET_README.md), [`ratchet/RHM_RL_RATCHET_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_RL_RATCHET_README.md), [`ratchet/RHM_RL_GEN_DISTILL_EXTENDED_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_RL_GEN_DISTILL_EXTENDED_README.md)). Along the way, an initially-promising 4-cycle trend was caught and retracted as noise once more cycles were run (p=0.15) — an early instance of a pattern that recurs throughout this history: **a positive result surviving only until someone runs the control that isolates the real driver.** The decisive move was [`ratchet/RHM_FOMAML_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_FOMAML_README.md): no outer objective (dense NTP, noisy RL, per-level NTP), meta-learned or not, makes FM-predictability pressure compound compositional NTP on stationary data. [`ratchet/RHM_META_LEARNING_README.md`](ratchet/RHM_META_LEARNING_README.md) confirmed even cross-rule-set meta-learning only buys anti-overfitting, not accelerated composition. **The question itself changed here**: from "does the ratchet compound" to "what would a moving frontier need to look like" — the question every subsequent era answers a piece of.

## Era 3 — Chasing the moving frontier (06-29 → 07-13)

[`RHM_DEEP_COMPOSITION_README.md`](RHM_DEEP_COMPOSITION_README.md) reframed the depth ceiling again: deep structure is recoverable (BP ceiling) and representable (oracle-labeled aux heads reach it), but no self-supervised local objective tried gets there — the high-level signal is intrinsically diluted (`KFW: vm^{ℓ+2}`). [`RHM_FRONTIER_AND_LEGIBILITY_README.md`](RHM_FRONTIER_AND_LEGIBILITY_README.md) then separated two previously-conflated DGP knobs — **occupancy** (recoverability) vs. **synonymic multiplicity `m`** (learnability) — and found m=2 lets plain NTP grok to the root, explaining why every earlier ratchet attempt (all run at m≥4) saw a structureless FM residual. This licensed m=2 as the substrate for everything downstream. On that substrate, [`RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md`](RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md) found the arc's clearest positive: generalizable self-knowledge appears **only** under a *latent* training target, never a token target — the FM residual is a map of the model's frontier, and a latent target moves that frontier deep and makes it FM-invariant. This is also where "sculpting" was first conceived: a latent objective as a *deliberate choice of what gets represented*, not a passive byproduct of token prediction. In parallel, [`RHM_FM_REGULARIZER_README.md`](RHM_FM_REGULARIZER_README.md) showed an **open-loop** (no self-knowledge, no injection) FM-predictability penalty compresses functional complexity below weight decay's floor — self-knowledge is *not* load-bearing for functional simplification, the pressure alone suffices — and [`RHM_COMPLEXODYNAMICS_README.md`](RHM_COMPLEXODYNAMICS_README.md) read the same trajectories as confirming Aaronson's rise-and-fall complexity law, with the floor being the DGP's own sophistication rather than zero.

Sculpting proper ([`RHM_SCULPTING_README.md`](RHM_SCULPTING_README.md)) grew out of [`RHM_EDIT_CONTROL_README.md`](RHM_EDIT_CONTROL_README.md)'s finding that editing toward a target root is plannable in belief space but gameable off-manifold (fixed with an on-manifold action space + consistency veto). Its first planning task was diagnosed as a dead end (greedy-decomposable, no lookahead prize) and rebuilt ([`sculpting_control_task.md`](sculpting_control_task.md)) to genuinely require lookahead; the mature result is that latent planning is a *cheap surrogate* for token planning on a clean channel, but only *beats* tokens once the channel becomes lossy. [`RHM_SCULPT_CONTINUAL_README.md`](RHM_SCULPT_CONTINUAL_README.md) then closed this sub-arc by finding a genuine but **weak** ratchet: value iteration is the big lever, one-shot belief internalization sets a ceiling, and continual re-internalization adds little before the belief frontier exhausts — a small-scale reproduction of Era 2's "compounds while the frontier climbs, then exhausts" prediction. [`SLEEP_CHUNKING_RHM_README.md`](SLEEP_CHUNKING_RHM_README.md) and [`specialization/README.md`](specialization/README.md) landed the same lesson from two more angles: a generic bottleneck (chunking, reweighting, subtree restriction) never recruits depth — only a *direct* deep/grounded objective does, and RHM's single shared grammar means narrowing training to a subtree actively hurts rather than specializes.

## Era 4 — Controllability: does active inference port? (07-10 → 07-11)

[`ACTIVE_RHM_README.md`](ACTIVE_RHM_README.md) asked whether the *controlled*-problem machinery from `a2a_forward`'s reaching arc (an agent whose forward model lets an external planner choose informative actions) ports to RHM's *autonomous* setting (a passive next-token predictor). The arity impossibility ports, but a mean-Δ forward model is a structural null for planning — value of information lives in the *variance* of an update, which a point estimate discards. Replacing the target with expected posterior entropy (Bayesian EIG) rescues planning at m=2 but not m=4, and the split is governed by a computable **per-instance query-rank correlation**, turning "is this problem controllable" into a number you can compute in advance. Phase 4 found this has no headroom for the reaching arc's "internalization" trick, for a structural reason: active-query RHM is inference in disguise (its only state is the belief-over-root), so **acting ≈ planning** — there's no policy/dynamics-model split for internalization to exploit, unlike control tasks.

## Era 5 — The confabulation payoff, and the residual-rank correction (07-22 → 07-27)

[`confabulation/README.md`](confabulation/README.md) is the arc's philosophical payoff: does a self-report track implementation (FM residual) or a recoverable self-theory (FM range)? Only the residual-direction target shows a first-person advantage over a capacity-matched third-party observer; on every control target the observer *beats* self-report. Closing the latent loop changes how well the model knows itself, not what there is to know.

Then, five weeks after Era 1's original residual-rank instrument shipped, [`residual_decomposition/README.md`](residual_decomposition/README.md) retired it. The belief `R_act ≈ R_comp + R_res` is false as arithmetic (`R_comp` is a redundant readout of `R_act`, `R_res` exceeds `R_act`, and naive rank is blind to magnitude — it *rises* as the residual shrinks 17×). It's replaced with two numbers: **β** (shape) and **`R_res_participation`** (frontier dimensionality in the model's own basis). This retroactively re-explains several old puzzles as a saturated-FM noise floor rather than genuine structure — including a case in `mjc`'s contact-residual work. [`residual_decomposition/trajectory/README.md`](residual_decomposition/trajectory/README.md) pushed the new instrument onto training trajectories and found a candid mixed result: the qualitative complexodynamics arc reproduces, but β itself fails its own capacity-invariance check outside a narrower regime than first advertised — logged as a bounded instrument, not a broken one.

## Era 6 — Two arcs running in parallel at the frontier (07-28 → 08-02, ongoing)

**Directed sculpting / full_loop** ([`directed_sculpting/README.md`](directed_sculpting/README.md), [`directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md)) ports `mjc`'s full E3 inner+outer loop onto RHM. The naive port was a confounded null (RHM rule usage is too uniform) until distractor channels, support-fixed rule drift, and a repair-cost instrument fixed it. The loop's reward-free "relevance" signal is a genuine positive (13–18× separation, 76% of oracle). Its central pivot: **expansion turned out to be a property of the grader's type** (grounded/evaluative vs. endogenous/dense), not of drift/non-stationarity — echoing Era 2's latent-vs-token split and Era 2's ratchet's "stationary data can't manufacture compounding" one level up. Two independent parallel cuts ([`full_loop/endo_expansion/README.md`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/endo_expansion/README.md), [`full_loop/endogenous_expansion/README.md`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/endogenous_expansion/README.md)) confirmed this and, together, retracted their own supporting metric: belief participation-ratio is anti-informative as an expansion ranker (a zero-information target produces the largest PR rise). [`full_loop/metering_sweep/README.md`](directed_sculpting/full_loop/metering_sweep/README.md) is the methodological side-quest, and it reversed itself mid-flight the same way Era 2's ratchet arc did: round 1 got a confidently wrong-signed answer because fixed epochs let collection budget and gradient steps grow together; round 2, holding compute fixed, found the true (humped) shape. That same over-training confound is now flagged, unresolved, against the arc's one positive on hierarchy-climbing (`full_loop/metered_climb/SPEC.md`[^private] — specification only, not yet run) — the open question this era leaves for whoever picks it up next.

**Minting** ([`minting/README.md`](minting/README.md)), running the same week but not cross-referenced from the main README, asks a related but distinct question: can a model **mint its own training data** and self-curate a curriculum without collapsing (autophagy)? An exact-parser build was needed first because the existing parser false-rejected 99.98% of genuine sequences. A "mirror" acceptance signal degenerated not by mode-collapsing on content (as predicted) but by pure replay — reframed as a mirror→non-mirror *axis* rather than a binary. A dose-response detour was needed because gradient-share concentration, not autophagy, was masking every arm's separation. The resolution: a rule-table verifier prevents autophagic collapse and genuinely expands support (~45-50% of accepted sequences fall outside the seed's support) but never approaches real held-out data — **the rule table is a support oracle, not a density oracle.** Verified self-generated tokens are worth ~nothing; what's scarce is information the model doesn't already carry.

## The throughline

Three patterns repeat across every era at every scale, and are the closest thing this program has to a crystallized prior: (1) **self-referential / endogenous signals plateau below grounded / external ones** — stationary-data ratchets don't compound (Era 2), token targets don't move the frontier but latent ones do (Era 3), dense endogenous graders cap below the no-loop floor while evaluative graders expand (Era 6), and a support oracle can't substitute for real density information (Era 6). (2) **Instruments get built, quietly ship a confound, and are only caught once someone runs the isolating control** — residual rank (Era 1 → corrected Era 5), the ratchet's early positive trends (Era 2), metering_sweep's round 1 (Era 6), and the still-open samples-per-event confound (Era 6). (3) **A "does X compound" question, once answered no, reliably dissolves into "what would make X move"** — this is the hinge at the end of Era 2, and it structures nearly everything from Era 3 onward.
