# History: RHM (Random Hierarchy Model)

*A big-picture pass over `experiments/rhm/`, written fresh from the READMEs as of 2026-08-10. Covers roughly 2026-06-20 → 2026-08-09 — a compressed but dense ~7-week arc. See [README.md](README.md) for the standing per-experiment index and [FILES.md](FILES.md) for the full file table.*

## Why RHM at all

The line exists because natural-language experiments hit an instrumentation wall: vocab reduction turned out to be a channel intervention (β not γ), and spectral denoising was entangled with the very statistics being measured ([README.md](README.md#goal)). RHM trades realism for **ground truth** — a known generative hierarchy, exact ancestry per token, and (as of August) an *exact* Bayesian belief-revision oracle via tree belief propagation ([conditional_revision/README.md](conditional_revision/README.md)). The whole arc below is best read as: what happens when you take questions that were murky on language/MNIST and get to ask them where you can check the answer.

## 1. The instrument comes first (06-20 → 06-23)

Before any mechanism question, the line had to establish what a forward-model (FM) residual even *means* on this substrate. The scaling sweep found **m (synonymic multiplicity) dominates L (depth) ~3:1** as the scaling bottleneck ([README.md §Scaling exponent sweep](README.md)). The first residual-rank measurements looked like "rank tracks DGP complexity" — but each of the three follow-up experiments quietly undercut that reading (rank tracks learning quality, not complexity per se; rank moves the *wrong direction* with FM capacity; architecture-matched sweeps showed norm drops 17× while rank barely moves at all). Per-level loss decomposition then supplied the mechanistic picture underneath all of it: learning is strictly bottom-up, and a fixed-capacity model plateaus at a hard composition-depth ceiling. Two objective-modification arcs (label smoothing, focal/threshold/FM-surprise-weighted loss) asked whether that ceiling is an artifact of NTP's sharpening incentive — **no**: it's a capacity constraint, not an objective-induced bias, though gradient reallocation still buys legibility (FM cosine 0.940→0.995) at near-zero cost.

The "rank tracks complexity" reading did not survive contact with better instruments. [residual_decomposition/README.md](residual_decomposition/README.md) (2026-07-27) is an explicit retraction: `res_var(i) ∝ act_var(i)^β` (R²=0.95–0.99) shows the residual is a *graded shadow* of the whole computation, not a leftover partition — falsifying `R_act ≈ R_comp + R_res` and explaining three earlier "positive" rank readings (including the 06-20 architecture-matched result) as a **saturated FM's noise floor**, not real structure. This retraction is recorded explicitly in [beliefs/trees/self_prediction_and_self_knowledge.md](../../beliefs/trees/self_prediction_and_self_knowledge.md#the-residual-of-a-capacity-limited-self-model-reflects-the-computational-complexity-of-the-modeled-system) — the belief survives only *directionally, along a training trajectory*, not as a cross-setting absolute comparison. This pattern — an early positive reading later diagnosed as an instrument artifact — recurs throughout the rest of the history and becomes something close to a house discipline.

## 2. A negative result redirects the whole program (06-23 → 06-29)

The [ratchet/](ratchet/README.md) arc asked the natural next question: MNIST's closed-loop wake-sleep ratchet *compounds* (34%→48% over 4 cycles) — does the same dynamic compound on RHM's decomposable hierarchy? It reproduces every MNIST **dynamic** (gate closing, FM tracking, robustness dissociation, crossover timing) but never the **magnitude** (peaks ~1%, no compounding). Capacity, task structure (RL), the distillation bottleneck, and FM-cosine regime were each ruled out in turn. The FOMAML diagnostic settled it: on **stationary** data, no outer objective can make FM-predictability pressure improve compositional NTP, because the outer loop reduces to the inner loop with nothing left to exploit — **compounding requires a moving frontier, and stationary RHM structurally can't supply one** ([ratchet/README.md](ratchet/README.md), `RHM_FOMAML_README.md`).

This negative result is the pivot for everything that follows in 07-28 onward: the "moving frontier" requirement is exactly what the directed-sculpting line (§5) goes and builds. The arc's [open threads](ratchet/README.md#open-threads-rehomed-from-the-parent-readme-next-steps) — the MNIST–RHM magnitude gap, and a "self-model-driven grokking" north star (L3 feature η² creeping up under a flat behavioral surface) — remain unclaimed.

## 3. Self-knowledge is directional, not scalar (07-01 → 08-05)

A second throughline, developed mostly independently of §2, concerns *what kind of thing* self-knowledge is. [RHM_FM_REGULARIZER_README.md](RHM_FM_REGULARIZER_README.md) (07-01) found an **open-loop** FM-predictability pressure beats weight decay's functional-complexity floor — answering "is closing the loop necessary for functional simplification?" with **no**. [confabulation/README.md](confabulation/README.md) (07-22) sharpened the frame with `a_j = FM(a_i) + r`: only reports about the residual `r` show a first-person advantage over capacity-matched third parties; reports about behavior are a dead null and reports about entropy/world-state go *negative* (the third party wins, because those facts are I/O-map-recoverable by construction). Crucially, the pre-registered expectation that **closing the loop is necessary** for this advantage also failed — open-loop shows the same dissociation, just weaker.

[endogenous_teacher/README.md](endogenous_teacher/README.md) (08-04/05) makes the resulting belief explicit and load-bearing: it cites the strong-confidence claim in [beliefs/trees/self_prediction_and_self_knowledge.md](../../beliefs/trees/self_prediction_and_self_knowledge.md#self-knowledge-is-encoded-directionally-what-kind-of-computation-was-surprising-rather-than-as-scalar-magnitude-how-surprising) — self-knowledge lives in residual *direction*, not magnitude — and tests it as a falsifier. Gate 0 shows the FM residual and token surprisal are almost orthogonal (R²=0.113) and **anti-localized** across hierarchy levels; collapsing that residual to a scalar and using it to reweight NTP loss then nulls completely (`res` ≈ `res_shuffled`). The [cancellation/](endogenous_teacher/cancellation/README.md) child pushes further into full-tensor routing rather than a scalar: the corollary-discharge mechanism itself replicates tightly across substrates (RHM and a looped ViT), but the downstream payoff doesn't — training depends on *having* a forecast, not on *which* forecaster supplies it, so the entanglement problem cancellation was meant to fix isn't present on RHM.

[conditional_revision/README.md](conditional_revision/README.md) (08-07/08) is this thread's most significant methodological move: surprisal bundles *reducible* surprise (belief revision) and *irreducible* surprise (a synonym draw), and RHM's known parse tree makes that split **exactly computable** via belief propagation — not a correlation hunt. The fix that makes the signal appear at all is temporal, not incidental: moving the FM's conditioning gap from depth (same input, zero gap — where Gate 0 read residual and surprisal as *anti-correlated*) to time (one real token ahead) **flips the correlation sign** (−0.34 → +0.65) and produces a residual with a genuine aleatoric component. Even so, belief revision only beats surprisal at d2/d3 (AUC 0.69/0.61 against a 0.50 null) — **nothing survives above d3**, bounded by how much belief the model actually holds near the root. Porting the same question to control substrates ([sculpt_slip/](conditional_revision/sculpt_slip/README.md), [local_loss/](conditional_revision/local_loss/README.md)) killed the applied half of the line: the FM is **bias-dominated, not variance-dominated**, at every data budget tried, so an aleatoric-uncertainty filter has nothing to buy.

## 4. Where RHM stops resembling language (specialization, minting)

Two threads probed whether RHM's shared-hierarchy structure can support the kind of domain specialization natural language shows. [specialization/README.md](specialization/README.md) found that concentrating even a *direct* deep oracle target onto a subtree **hurts** that subtree relative to broad training (Cut-2a's "Student-B > Student-A", worst at capacity-limited m=8) — flagged as the **first RHM-vs-language disanalogy**: RHM's single shared ruleset means there is no domain-specific deep structure to specialize *on*, so "go uniquely deep on A" is the wrong frame here. This motivates a partially-heterogeneous DGP (context-sensitive rules) as the next substrate, closer to how language actually has non-shared deep structure per domain. [minting/README.md](minting/README.md) tested a related breadth question — self-generated data accepted by graders ranging from a mirror to the exact rule table — and found the rule table behaves as a **support oracle, not a density oracle**: verified self-generated data is 100% legal and genuinely expands support, but NTP is density matching, so a support filter alone can't fix in-support density error and buys almost nothing against real data at equal volume.

## 5. Directed sculpting: the current frontier, and a self-correcting rhythm (07-28 → 08-05)

This is where the ratchet arc's "needs a moving frontier" diagnosis (§2) gets built out, porting an mjc experiment's inner/outer planning loop onto RHM. The **groundwork** ([directed_sculpting/README.md](directed_sculpting/README.md)) first had to defuse a confounded null: RHM rule usage is uniform by construction, so a naive port's `value = lprog × visits` term collapses to `lprog` with no lever. Three primitives fixed this — distractor channels, support-fixed rule drift (non-stationarity with the DP optimum provably unmoved), and a repair-cost instrument that survived two of its own traps (raw CE is *exactly* blind to this kind of drift; the naive repair gap is confounded by ordinary continued training).

The **loop** ([full_loop/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/README.md)) found a genuine positive — a reward-free relevance signal separates real tokens from distractors 13–18× with zero channel labels — but the arc's real pivot finding is that **belief expansion is a property of the grader's type, not of non-stationarity**: a grounded evaluative grader expands belief and control substantially; an endogenous dense grader caps *below* the no-loop floor, in both static and drifting worlds alike. Both drift-dependent predictions this line started with came back negative, each after a repair that removed a bias favoring the positive answer — the climbing-null is scoped, not closed: starving samples-per-drift-event (rather than drift magnitude) produced the arc's first real positive on hierarchy climbing.

What follows is a small case study in the self-correction habit noted in §1. [level_moves/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/level_moves/README.md) first read a level preference as real (level-ℓ moves outscoring lower levels several-fold) — but this didn't survive its own confound: level-ℓ moves rewrite more tokens, so |ΔV| rises with span regardless of value, and a depth-matched distractor null rose *faster* than the real tree. A matched-span paired control then recovered a real, smaller effect: the value genuinely prefers abstract commitment, and — after further repairing the damage-injection protocol to be on-grammar — that preference turns out to be **graded by whether a commitment at that level can actually reach the injected error**, i.e. sensitivity to compositional structure rather than depth as such. [metering_sweep/README.md](directed_sculpting/full_loop/metering_sweep/README.md) found its own previously-published ladder was inflated ~17% by a compute-overfitting confound the idea doc's stated falsifier had simply never been run against — round 2 at matched compute shows the prize is humped, not monotone, though the qualitative contrasts survive. [partial_hetero/](directed_sculpting/full_loop/partial_hetero/README.md) and its [shared_surface/](directed_sculpting/full_loop/partial_hetero/shared_surface/README.md) child both landed as **underpowered nulls** rather than resolved negatives — flat curves sitting inside the instrument's own noise floor, explicitly logged as such rather than over-interpreted.

## The shape of it

Read end to end, the arc's real product isn't any single positive result — it's a hardening discipline around **not trusting the first read of an instrument**. Nearly every headline finding in this directory (residual rank, the level-preference result, the metering ladder, Gate 0's sign) was measured once, looked clean, and then got a second, harder look that either killed it, reframed it, or found the confound that had been faking it. The two standing, repeatedly-reconfirmed beliefs that came out clean are that **self-knowledge is directional rather than scalar** (§3, now cross-validated on confabulation, endogenous-teacher, and cancellation) and that **stationary data cannot sustain a compounding ratchet** (§2) — the second of which is the reason the entire directed-sculpting program (§5), the field's largest active thread, exists at all. The open frontier questions are: what conditional_revision's exact BP oracle can still teach a scalar-blind training signal now that the direction/scale split is settled; whether a partially-heterogeneous DGP (§4) can make RHM a fairer analogy to language's genuine domain structure; and, per the ratchet arc's unclaimed thread, whether the L3-η²-creeps-while-behavior-is-flat pattern is this substrate's own quiet grokking signature.
