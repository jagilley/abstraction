## One-line arc

A forward self-model splits a model's own computation into `a_j = FM(a_i) + r`, where `FM(a_i)` is *everything a self-theory could produce* and `r` is its complement. Reports about `r` show a **first-person advantage over every capacity-matched third-party observer** (+0.09 to +0.11), while reports about behaviour show **none** (−0.001) and reports about output entropy or world-state go **negative** (the observer wins). The advantage survives a 24× sweep of forward-model capacity, and the observer ladder is **flat in capacity** — the third party is access-limited, not resource-limited.

## The question

The standing objection to any LLM-introspection claim is Nisbett & Wilson: a report about one's own processing may be generated by a learned *theory* of oneself rather than by *access* to oneself. The forward-self-model decomposition gives that objection an operational form for the first time — `FM` is, by construction, the best compressible predictor of M's own computation learned from observing M, so **confabulation lives in the range of the self-model and whatever cannot be confabulated lives in the residual**.

Operationalized as: a signal `S` is introspective for M iff (1) `S` is a function of M's internal state, (2) `S` is not *cheaply* recoverable from M's input–output map, (3) M's behaviour is causally sensitive to `S`. **(2) is the discriminator.** Calibration and entropy are functions of the I/O map, so a third party can compute them — that is self-*inference*. `r` is a function of the *implementation*: two models with identical I/O behaviour have different residuals, and the only route to it from outside is to rebuild M and FM, at which point the observer has stopped observing and become a copy.

## Setup

Identical to RHM_LATENT_LOOP's m4 regime, so the wake half is a controlled fork:

- **DGP**: distinct-rule RHM, `v=16, s=2, L=6, m=4` (occupancy 0.25), rule seed 0.
- **M**: causal GPT 8L/8H/256D (6.34M), `post_block0 → post_block6`, injection after block 1 through the `UnifiedGate`.
- **Conditions**: `ntp_aux_cl` (latent target, closed loop) and `ntp_aux` (latent target, open loop). **The latent target is not optional** — latent-loop showed RHM's token target has *inverted* self-knowledge (fresh-FM SK −0.67), so a report battery on `ntp_cl` would test the channel on a model with nothing to report.
- **Harness validation**: both conditions reproduce the reference val loss exactly — `ntp_aux_cl` **2.3829** vs 2.383, `ntp_aux` **1.5447** vs 1.545. Any difference in the battery is attributable to the battery, not to a drifted recipe.

**Report targets**, all emitted by a head on `post_block7` over standalone (non-injected) forward passes with a fresh-FM instrument, on a sequence-level train/test split:

| target | reported quantity | determined by |
|---|---|---|
| **IMPL** | 8-way spherical-k-means cluster of the direction of M's own residual `r_t` | **implementation** |
| **BEHAV** | is M's own next-token argmax correct | I/O map |
| **ENT** | quartile of M's own output entropy | I/O map |
| **WORLD** | ground-truth DGP ancestor feature at d3 | input |

## The capacity guard (why half this experiment is controls)

The residual only means anything if the FM is small enough that what it misses is a genuine *computational* gap. On a toy DGP an over-capacity FM saturates and `r` degenerates into architectural-mismatch noise — and here that does not merely null the experiment, **it manufactures the headline**: the report head sits downstream of `a_j` so M can report noise perfectly well, while no observer can predict noise from tokens and logits. IMPL would show a large "introspective advantage" built from junk.

This is not hypothetical. RHM's own [REGIME_TRANSITION](../REGIME_TRANSITION_README.md) was declared *"not testable at this scale"* at FM cosine 0.994, and a smoke run here (M trained 300 steps) reproduced the artifact exactly: `ens_cos` 0.65, d6 η² 0.003 — junk by both diagnostics — yet IMPL still returned **+0.118 advantage and a 2.07× steering ratio**.

So every IMPL number is swept over instrument-FM capacity and carries two discriminators:

- **`ens_cos`** — input-centered pairwise residual cosine across independent fresh FMs (different seeds). High ⇒ input-determined, a real gap any FM misses identically. Low ⇒ FM-idiosyncratic noise.
- **hierarchy `η²`** — is the residual conditioned on the ground-truth DGP ancestor features?

Capacity is swept on **both** `d_head` and `mlp_mult`, because moving `d_head` alone barely changes total parameters (the FM's MLP is a ~131K floor): `(4, 0.25) → (128, 4.0)` spans **1.4% → 33.5%** of the predicted blocks' 4.72M parameters. Head count is fixed at M's 8 throughout, since [RESIDUAL_RANK](../RESIDUAL_RANK_README.md) found a head-count mismatch dominates the residual.

**The regime is clean.** Cosine 0.78–0.90 (not 0.99), `ens_cos` 0.84–0.91 (not 0.65).

**IMPL is the only target with a positive introspective advantage.** BEHAV is a dead null; on ENT and WORLD the third-party observer *beats* the self-report. That is criterion (2) doing exactly its job: facts about the I/O map are third-person accessible, the residual is not.

### The observer ladder is flat in capacity — access-limited, not resource-limited

This is the sharpest single result, and it is invisible in the summary table. IMPL, CL, h16m1:

| observer | 1L/64D | 2L/128D | 4L/192D | 8L/256D | half data |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `O_input` (tokens only) | 0.376 | 0.398 | 0.381 | 0.376 | — |
| `O_io` (tokens + M's logits) | 0.616 | 0.676 | 0.680 | 0.670 | 0.637 |

`O_input` is **completely flat** from 64D to 256D, and `O_io` plateaus at 0.68 by 2L/128D — a capacity-matched 8L/256D observer, the same size as M itself, does no better than a 2-layer one. Halving its data barely moves it (0.670 → 0.637). The third party is neither capacity-limited nor data-limited; it has plateaued well below the self-report because it lacks *access*.

The **WORLD** target is the internal control that makes this interpretable: there `O_input` climbs steeply with capacity (0.227 → 0.407 → 0.522), exactly as an input-determined target should. So the ladder *can* climb when the information is in the input — it simply doesn't for IMPL.

### Test 3 — matched-KL steering, and its control passes

Every direction is rescaled to the same behavioural effect (KL 0.0100 on the logits), so behaviour is held constant and only the steered subspace varies:

| family | matched KL | **IMPL-flip** | BEHAV-flip (control) |
|---|---|---|---|
| residual span | 0.0100 | **0.139** | 0.024 |
| prediction span | 0.0100 | **0.063** | 0.023 |

BEHAV-flip is 0.024 vs 0.023 — **the control is matched**, confirming the two interventions really are behaviourally equivalent — while the IMPL report moves **2.20×** more under residual-span steering. A theory-driven report cannot show that asymmetry, because it can only track theory-visible structure.

## The loop amplifies but is not necessary

| | CL (`ntp_aux_cl`) | OL (`ntp_aux`) |
|---|---|---|
| IMPL margin | +0.33 to +0.36 | +0.08 to +0.17 |
| IMPL advantage | +0.09 to +0.11 | +0.06 |
| steer ratio | 2.09 – 2.20× | 1.33 – 1.68× |
| BEHAV / ENT / WORLD advantage | −0.001 / −0.035 / −0.038 | +0.002 / +0.007 / +0.011 |

CL is stronger on every measure, tracking the known SK gap (fresh-FM 0.78 vs 0.56). **But OL is not null** — it shows a positive IMPL advantage with the three control targets at zero as well. The pre-registered guess that the loop would be *required* is **falsified**: the dissociation comes from the FM decomposition itself, and closing the loop roughly doubles-to-triples it.

## The η² recheck — and a correction it forced

The main run reported d6 η² ≈ 0.024 (CL) against latent-loop's 0.322, a 13× discrepancy. Cause: `_compute_hierarchy_eta2` ([../rhm_latent_loop.py:186](../rhm_latent_loop.py)) uses the **last token only** — the root-completing position, where deep structure is maximal — while the main run pooled all 64 positions. `eta2_recheck` reloads the saved wake checkpoints (no retraining) and imports the reference function itself:

| condition | our d6 | reference d6 | our d5 | reference d5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ntp_aux` | **0.295** | 0.295 | **0.381** | 0.381 |
| `ntp_aux_cl` | **0.322** | 0.322 | **0.421** | 0.421 |

Identical to three decimals — a pure estimator artifact, and an independent confirmation that the residual is DGP-structured exactly as the reference found. Structure sits deep (d5 0.40–0.43 > d6 0.31–0.33 > d4 0.13–0.22 ≫ d3–d1), is **capacity-invariant** across the 24× sweep (d6 0.310–0.332 CL), and is conditioned on *which feature* rather than *which synonym rule* (rule η² ≈ 0.023–0.027 against feature η² ≈ 0.32).

**Correction.** An earlier reading of the pooled numbers treated the CL-vs-OL gap (0.024 vs 0.002) as a real 12× effect. It is not: on the reference estimator the gap is 0.322 vs 0.295, about **+9%**. The pooled estimator did not merely dilute the signal, it manufactured a between-condition contrast that does not exist.

This *improves* the interpretation. The residual is **essentially equally DGP-structured in CL and OL**, so the large CL/OL margin gap cannot be explained by there being more structure to report on — it must come from M *encoding* that structure better, which is precisely the SK gap. **Closing the loop does not change what there is to know about yourself; it changes how well you know it.**

## Limitations — the parts that did not work

1. **`O_act` failed as a ceiling.** It was meant to be an upper bound (post_block0 → target *is* FM + a probe, so it should nail IMPL), but it came in at **0.666**, below `O_io` (0.680) and well below the self-report (0.776). Reconstructing 6 blocks + the FM from `a0` in 3000 steps is simply beyond it. So the experiment has **no validated ceiling**, and the claim rests on the observer ladder's flatness rather than on a demonstrated upper bound.
2. **Test 2 (channel ablation) is a weak discriminator here.** Under `shuffle_r`, ENT falls 0.917 → 0.458 and WORLD 0.510 → 0.299, not just IMPL (0.776 → 0.213). At cosine ~0.88 the residual is a large fraction of `a_j`, so shuffling it is a big generic perturbation that degrades everything. The ordering is right; the test should not carry weight.
3. **ENT is the cautionary case, and it is worth keeping.** Margin +0.267 with advantage −0.035 — a large confabulator gap and *no* introspective advantage. **Margin alone is not evidence of introspection**; only the advantage separates. That the battery distinguishes them is a point in its favour.
4. **The report head is trained.** This is a claim about the report's causal grounding, not its spontaneity. Use ≠ report ≠ awareness.
5. **Single seed**, one rule seed, one DGP regime.
6. The introspection, if granted, arguably belongs to the composite (M + FM) rather than to M alone — which matches the cerebellar analogy but should be said rather than elided.
7. OL requires ~8× larger perturbations for the same KL (eps ≈ 15 vs 1.8), so OL and CL steering magnitudes are not directly comparable; only the within-condition residual/prediction ratio is.

## Reproduction

```bash
cd experiments/

# the battery (one condition per detached run; ~hours on L4)
modal run --detach -m rhm.confabulation.rhm_confabulation::confabulation_test \
    --conditions "ntp_aux_cl" --tag cl
modal run --detach -m rhm.confabulation.rhm_confabulation::confabulation_test \
    --conditions "ntp_aux" --tag ol

# reference-estimator eta2 (reloads saved checkpoints, no retraining)
modal run --detach -m rhm.confabulation.rhm_confabulation::eta2_recheck

# wiring smoke (minutes; numbers meaningless -- FM cosine 0.99, the artifact regime)
modal run -m rhm.confabulation.rhm_confabulation::confabulation_test --smoke
```

Results: `/data/rhm_confabulation/v16_s2_L6_m4_distinct/{cl,ol}_results.json`, `eta2_recheck.json`; wake checkpoints under `.../ckpt/` (the run resumes from them by default, keyed on `(cond, n_steps, seed)`).

**Gotcha**: the smoke deliberately sets `diag_seqs=100 < n_report_sequences=400` so the chunked-prediction truncation path is exercised. An earlier smoke had `diag_seqs == n_report_sequences`, which hid an off-by-chunk bug that then killed two full 20K-step runs. The wake checkpoint now saves the instant training ends, so a measurement crash can never again cost a training run.

## Next steps

1. **A real ceiling.** `O_act` needs far more training (or initializing from a trained FM) before it bounds anything. Without it, "the observer plateaus" is suggestive but not closed.
2. **A token report channel.** The head is currently an auxiliary MLP; emitting the report through M's own LM head over a reserved report vocabulary would make it a *report* rather than a probe.
3. **Language.** RHM's residual is a deep inference-depth gap; language's is diffuse and full-rank. Whether the observer ladder stays flat there is the generalization test.
4. **Drop Test 2 or sharpen it** — e.g. steer-based ablation at matched perturbation norm, rather than wholesale substitution.
5. **Seeds.** Everything here is n=1.
