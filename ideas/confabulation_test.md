# The Confabulation Test: separating introspection from self-theory

**Status**: design, pre-registration. No results yet.
**Depends on**: [activation_to_activation_forward.md](activation_to_activation_forward.md) (the FM primitive), [../experiments/rhm/RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md](../experiments/rhm/RHM_LATENT_LOOP_README.md) (the only RHM condition with generalizable self-knowledge — the substrate), ../papers/forward_self_models_paper1.md[^private].
**Related**: [gradient_verbalization.md](gradient_verbalization.md), [self_model_needs_a_loop.md](self_model_needs_a_loop.md).

## The claim

A forward self-model decomposes a model's own computation into two parts:

```
a_j  =  FM(a_i)  +  r
        ^^^^^^^     ^
        the part a compressed        the part no model
        model of M can anticipate    of M anticipated
```

`FM(a_i)` is, by construction, **everything a self-theory could produce**. A self-theory is a predictor of M learned from observing M; FM *is* the best compressible such predictor, learned from M's own activity alone. So:

> **Confabulation lives in the range of the self-model. Whatever cannot be confabulated lives in its orthogonal complement — the residual.**

This gives the standing objection to every LLM-introspection claim (Nisbett & Wilson: a self-report may be generated by a *theory* of oneself rather than *access* to oneself) an operational form for the first time. We can compute both components, ablate either, and steer along either.

## The criterion

A signal `S` is **introspective** for a system M iff:

1. `S` is a function of M's internal state;
2. `S` is not *cheaply* recoverable from M's input–output behaviour — a capacity-matched third-party observer with full access to M's inputs and outputs cannot predict `S` as well as M reports it;
3. M's behaviour is causally sensitive to `S`.

**(2) is the discriminator**, and it's where every other candidate signal dies. Calibration, output entropy, "I'm not sure about that" — all are functions of the I/O map, hence third-person accessible, hence self-*inference*, not introspection. The residual is a function of the **implementation**: two models with identical I/O behaviour have different residuals.

### On the resource bound in (2)

Everything M computes is a deterministic function of M's input, so an unbounded observer can recover `r` — by rebuilding M and FM. That is exactly the point, and it is not a weakening. The only route to third-person knowledge of `r` is *simulation*: at the point where an observer can predict the residual, it has stopped observing M and has become a copy of M. **The residual is private in the precise sense that reconstructing it from outside costs as much as being the system.** Behaviour-facts are cheap from outside; implementation-facts are not.

This makes the measurement a **scaling curve, not a point estimate**: how big does an outside observer have to be to know what M knows about itself?

## Substrate: RHM, `ntp_aux_cl`

Chosen over language (too expensive for a 4-arm × 3-target factorial) and grokking (its forward self-model sits at R²≈1.0 — no residual to report on; see `experiments/inverse_dynamics/`).

RHM gives us three things nothing else does:

- **A native token channel.** RHM is autoregressive, so a report can be *emitted* through M's own LM head rather than read off by us with a probe.
- **Ground-truth DGP latents.** Per-position hierarchy level and per-level ancestor labels give an exactly-known **world-report** control target.
- **A characterised self-knowledge precondition.** RHM_LATENT_LOOP established that generalizable self-knowledge appears on RHM **only** under the latent target with the loop closed (`ntp_aux_cl`: fresh-FM SK **+0.78**, co-trained 0.91) and is *inverted* under a token target at any loop strength (`ntp_cl`: −0.67). This is load-bearing: **the experiment must run on `ntp_aux_cl`, not `ntp_cl`.** Running it on the token target would test the report channel on a model that has no self-knowledge to report.

That writeup also tells us the RHM latent residual is a **deep DGP/inference-depth gap** that no 1-layer FM can compute (ens_cos 0.98, FM-invariant). That is the ideal IMPL target: precisely the kind of fact that is free to read from inside and expensive to reconstruct from outside.

Config, inherited unchanged from RHM_LATENT_LOOP: distinct-rule RHM `s=2, L=6, v=16, m=4`; M = causal GPT 8L/8H/256D (~6.34M); FM = `TransformerForwardModel` 1L/8H/d_head 16 (~264K, matched-head), `post_block0 → post_block6`, injected after block 1 through the unified gate.

## The central threat: FM capacity on a toy DGP

**This is the confound most likely to produce a false positive, and it has to be managed before any IMPL number means anything.**

The residual is only interesting if the FM is small enough that what it misses is a genuine *computational* gap. On a small DGP the FM saturates easily — and then `r` degenerates into architectural-mismatch noise. This repo has already hit exactly that wall: RHM's `REGIME_TRANSITION` was declared **"not testable at this scale"** at FM cosine 0.994, and `RESIDUAL_RANK` found a capacity sweep where residual rank *rose* with FM capacity because removing structured mismatch errors leaves diffuse noise behind.

The danger here is worse than a null. Consider what a junk residual does to this specific design:

- the report head sits at `post_block{n-1}`, downstream of `a_j`, so **M can report noise perfectly well**;
- no third-party observer can predict noise from tokens and logits — not because it is *private*, but because it is *junk*;
- so IMPL shows a large "introspective advantage" and BEHAV shows a clean null.

That is the exact signature the experiment is looking for, produced by nothing. **A mis-set FM capacity manufactures the headline result.**

### The two discriminators

Every capacity point therefore carries two measurements, and no IMPL number may be read without them:

- **`ens_cos`** — train several *independent* fresh FMs (different seeds) on the same frozen model and measure input-centered pairwise residual cosine. High ⇒ the residual is input-determined: a real gap that any FM misses identically. Low ⇒ FM-idiosyncratic noise. (`RHM_LATENT_LOOP` Exp 4 established this instrument; latent residuals hit 0.98 there.)
- **hierarchy `η²`** — is the residual conditioned on the ground-truth DGP ancestor features? A genuine capacity gap is; noise is not.

### The failure mode, observed

A smoke run (M trained only 300 steps, so its computation is nearly trivial) shows the artifact concretely:

| instrument | cosine | `ens_cos` | d6 η² | IMPL advantage | steer ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| d_head 4 | 0.991 | **0.661** | **0.003** | +0.118 | 2.07× |
| d_head 32 | 0.993 | **0.651** | **0.002** | +0.086 | 1.83× |

`ens_cos` ≈ 0.65 (independent FMs leave *different* residuals) and η² ≈ 0.002 (no hierarchy conditioning) both say this residual is junk — and yet IMPL still shows a healthy advantage and a 2× steering ratio. **The target signature, produced by noise.** Compare `RHM_LATENT_LOOP` at 20K steps under the latent target: `ens_cos` 0.98, d6 η² ≈ 0.32.

Note also that shrinking the FM barely moved cosine (0.9934 → 0.9913). When capacity is not the binding constraint, noise is — itself a tell that you are in the wrong regime.

### Design consequences

1. **Sweep the instrument FM's capacity** rather than trusting one setting, and report the whole curve. Sweep *both* `d_head` and `mlp_mult`: moving `d_head` alone barely changes capacity, because the FM's MLP is a ~131K parameter floor that dominates the total. The four points `(d_head, mlp_mult) ∈ {(4, 0.25), (16, 1.0), (64, 2.0), (128, 4.0)}` span **1.4% → 33.3%** of the predicted blocks' 4.72M parameters, which brackets the a2a saturation point (~44% of predicted layers → cosine 0.999).

   The claim to look for is that the introspective advantage tracks the *genuine-gap* diagnostics. If the advantage is instead **largest where the residual is noisiest**, that is the artifact announcing itself, and the right response is to report it as such.
2. **Match head count** (`n_head` fixed at the model's 8). `RESIDUAL_RANK` found a 1H-FM vs 4H-model mismatch dominated the residual; `RHM_LATENT_LOOP` chose matched heads deliberately so the residual reflects computation rather than architecture.
3. **Measure capacity against the blocks predicted, not the whole model.** The a2a capacity scaling result is that an FM saturates once it reaches roughly the parameter count of the layers it predicts (~44% → cosine 0.999). Against a 6-block gap that is the meaningful denominator.
4. Grokking fails this test outright — R²≈1.0, no residual — which is an independent reason it cannot host this experiment.

## The report channel

Two variants, escalating:

- **(a) Aux head** — an MLP head on `post_block7` emitting a categorical report. Cheap; gets the dissociation.
- **(b) Token channel** — a reserved `[REPORT]` position appended to the sequence, with the report emitted through M's own LM head over a reserved report vocabulary. Routes the report through M's own computation and output channel; this is the version that is actually a *report* rather than a probe.

Build (a) first, escalate to (b) if the dissociation holds.

## Report targets (crossed with everything below)

| Target | What is reported | Determined by |
|---|---|---|
| **IMPL** | K-way cluster of the direction of M's own FM residual `r_t` (k-means, K=8, fit on train activations); magnitude-bucket as a secondary variant | M's **implementation** |
| **BEHAV** | Will M's next-token prediction be correct? / output-entropy bucket | M's **I/O map** |
| **WORLD** | Ground-truth DGP ancestor label at a given hierarchy level | M's **input** |

The three have deliberately different theoretical profiles. Only IMPL should show a first-person advantage. WORLD and BEHAV are the built-in controls: they hold the report *architecture* fixed and vary only what is being reported about.

## The four tests

### Test 1 — the third-person observer ladder (the core)

Train observers that see only what a third party sees, matched on training data budget, and sweep their capacity:

- `O_input(c)` — input tokens only
- `O_io(c)` — input tokens + M's full output distribution at every position
- `O_act` — input + M's `post_block0` activations (**ceiling**: this is just FM + a probe, and should nail IMPL)
- M's own report (**first person**)

**Prediction.** On BEHAV and WORLD: `O_io` saturates at small `c` and matches M — no first-person advantage. On IMPL: `O_input` and `O_io` stay far below M at every affordable `c`, approaching M only as `c → |M| + |FM|`; `O_act` nails it.

The gap `M − O_io` on IMPL is the **introspective advantage**. The *shape of the observer scaling curve* is the real measurement — a quantitative form of "how private is this fact?"

**Data-budget control (essential).** An observer that underperforms because we starved it of data would fake an introspective advantage. So the strongest observer is also trained at half data. If halving the data barely moves it, the shortfall on IMPL is about capacity and access, not about the budget we handed the third party. Report this alongside every advantage number; without it the Test 1 gap is not interpretable.

### Test 2 — channel ablation

At eval, ablate components of the state feeding the report head:
- **ablate-r**: replace `a_j` with `FM(a_i)` on the path to the report head (residual removed).
- **ablate-p**: project out the span of `FM(a_i)` (self-theory component removed).

**Prediction.** IMPL collapses under ablate-r and survives ablate-p. BEHAV survives both. *Caveat, stated up front:* this is a linear-subspace ablation and is the weakest of the four tests. It is corroborative, not decisive.

### Test 3 — matched-KL counterfactual steering (the sharpest)

Steer `a_j` along PCs of the **residual span** versus PCs of the **FM-prediction span** (the self-theory's range) — rescaling every direction so all of them produce the **same behavioural effect** (a target KL on the logits).

Matching KL is what makes this test work. It means matched behaviour, so any difference in how far the IMPL report moves cannot be a behavioural artifact, and BEHAV report-flip becomes a built-in control that should come out *equal* across the two families.

|  | IMPL report flip | BEHAV report flip |
|---|---|---|
| steer **residual** span @ matched KL | **large** | equal (control) |
| steer **prediction** span @ matched KL | small | equal (control) |

A theory-driven report can only track theory-visible structure, so it cannot produce this asymmetry. The headline statistic is the **residual/prediction IMPL-flip ratio at matched behaviour**.

*(An earlier version compared quiet vs loud quartiles of residual PCs at fixed perturbation size. That leaves behavioural effect uncontrolled — the smoke run separated KL by only ~3×, which is not enough to call anything behaviour-null. Matched-KL supersedes it.)*

*Risk*: if the residual is fully behaviour-relevant, no low-effect directions exist. This is cheap to check first; fall back on Tests 1/2/4 if so.

### Test 4 — confabulation-forcing (the positive control)

Train a report head *only* on what a self-theory would say: targets `ĉ = cluster(FM'(a_i))` from a **second, held-out** FM. This head is confabulating by construction. Verify it **fails** Tests 1–3 — no first-person advantage, survives ablate-r, invisible to behaviour-null steering.

Without Test 4, a null on Test 1 is uninterpretable. This is what calibrates the whole battery: it demonstrates the tests can detect confabulation when confabulation is definitely present.

## Loop arm

Run the whole battery on **OL** (`ntp_aux`, SK 0.56) and **CL** (`ntp_aux_cl`, SK 0.78). Prediction: the introspective advantage requires the loop — OL M carries the residual in its activations only incidentally, CL M is under gradient pressure to encode it. If OL shows the same advantage, the loop is not necessary for introspection and the finding is about the FM decomposition alone — still interesting, but a weaker claim, and we should say so.

## What would falsify this

- **`O_io` matches M on IMPL.** The residual would then be cheaply recoverable from the I/O map, criterion (2) fails, and the introspection argument fails with it. This is a real risk and the most informative outcome in either direction.
- **IMPL report survives ablate-r.** The report is driven by something other than the residual.
- **CONFAB passes the tests.** The battery does not discriminate and needs redesign.
- **IMPL report accuracy is at chance even in the full condition.** The residual cluster is not reportable at all; nothing downstream is interpretable.

## What this does *not* establish

Worth stating plainly so we don't over-claim in any writeup:

- The report head is **trained**. This is not spontaneous introspection; the claim is about the report's *causal grounding*, not its spontaneity.
- Use ≠ report ≠ awareness. Nothing here touches phenomenal consciousness, and we should not gesture at it.
- The introspection, if found, arguably belongs to the **composite** (M + FM), not to M alone. That matches the biology — cerebellar forward models are a separate structure — but it should be said rather than elided.
